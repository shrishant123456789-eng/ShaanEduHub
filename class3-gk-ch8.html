💡 Amazing Inventions

Inventors are people who make new things to help us.

Alexander Graham Bell: Invented the Telephone. (Ring Ring! Bell!)

Thomas Alva Edison: Invented the Light Bulb. (He gave us light!)

Wright Brothers: Invented the Airplane. (They flew like birds!)

Charles Babbage: Known as the Father of the Computer.

💡 Tip: Bell made the phone 'Ring' (Bell -> Telephone).

🧊 States of Matter

Everything around us is made of Matter. It has 3 forms:

1. Solid: Hard. It keeps its shape.

Examples: Ice, Wood, Stone, Table.

2. Liquid: Wet. It flows and takes the shape of the cup.

Examples: Water, Milk, Juice.

3. Gas: Invisible (mostly). It spreads everywhere.

Examples: Air, Steam, Oxygen.

❓ Check: Is water a Solid? (No, it's a Liquid)

[Image: Ice cube (Solid) -> Water (Liquid) -> Steam (Gas)]
💻 Computer Basics

A computer is a smart machine.

CPU (Central Processing Unit): The BRAIN of the computer.

Monitor: The SCREEN. It looks like a TV.

Keyboard: Used for TYPING. It has many keys.

Mouse: Used for POINTING and clicking.

💡 Tip: CPU thinks, Monitor shows!

🔒 Internet Safety

When we use the internet, we must be safe.

  • 🚫 Don't Share: Never share your password or address with strangers.
  • 🗣️ Tell Parents: If you see something scary, tell your parents immediately.
  • ⏳ Limit Time: Don't play games all day. Play outside too!

❓ Check: Should you tell your password to a friend? (No!)
