📖 Chapter Notes

1. Countries & Capitals

A capital city is where the government of a country operates. Every country has one.

Image: World Map Highlighting USA, UK, Japan
🇮🇳 India Example: Just like New Delhi is the capital of India, Washington D.C. is the capital of USA.
💡 Tip: "Tokyo" sounds like "Talk-yo". Japan loves technology (talking robots)!
❓ What is the capital of France? (Ans: Paris)
2. World Currencies

Currency is the money used in a country. Different countries use different notes and coins.

Image: Symbols of $, €, £, ¥
🇮🇳 India Example: We use Rupees (₹). USA uses Dollars ($). Europe uses Euro (€).
💡 Tip: UK uses Pounds (£). Think of a heavy "Pound" cake from London!
3. National Flags

A flag represents a country's identity. Colors and symbols have meanings.

Image: Flags of Canada, USA, Japan
🇮🇳 India Example: Our Tiranga has a Chakra. The Canadian flag has a Maple Leaf.
❓ Which country has a red circle (Sun) on white? (Ans: Japan)
4. Continents

There are 7 continents. Asia is the largest, Australia is the smallest.

🇮🇳 India Example: India is located in the continent of Asia.
💡 Tip: 4 A's: Asia, Africa, Antarctica, Australia. Plus Europe, North & South America.
5. International Organizations

Groups where countries work together.
• UN (United Nations): For world peace (HQ: New York).
• WHO: World Health Organization (Health for all).
• UNICEF: Helps children worldwide.

Image: UN Logo (Olive branches)
6. Time Zones

When it is day in India, it might be night in America. The world is divided into time zones based on the sun.

🇮🇳 India Example: IST (Indian Standard Time) is 5 hours 30 mins ahead of London (GMT).
7. Global Cultures

Traditions vary everywhere.
• Japan: People bow to greet (Konnichiwa).
• Italy: Famous for Pizza and Pasta.
• France: Famous for Fashion and Cheese.

8. World Wonders

Amazing structures built by humans.
• Taj Mahal (India): White marble.
• Great Wall (China): Very long wall.
• Eiffel Tower (France): Iron tower in Paris.
