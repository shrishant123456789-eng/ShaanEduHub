Study Notes

1. Simple Scientific Facts
Definition: Science is the study of the world around us by observing and experimenting.
Explanation: We see science everywhere. Why does an apple fall? Why is the sky blue? Science answers these questions.
Indian Example: When your mother makes curd from milk, that is a scientific chemical change!
Memory Tip: 🔬 Observe -> Question -> Learn.
Quick Check: Is cooking food a part of science? (Yes)
Image: Kid observing a butterfly
2. Matter Around Us
Definition: Anything that has weight and takes up space is called Matter.
Explanation: Matter exists in three forms: Solid (hard), Liquid (flows), and Gas (invisible/floats).
Indian Example: Ice (Solid) -> Water (Liquid) -> Steam from chai (Gas).
Memory Tip: 🧊💧💨 (Solid, Liquid, Gas)
Quick Check: Is air matter? (Yes, it takes space)
Image: Ice cube, Water glass, Steam
3. Energy – Light, Heat & Sound
Definition: Energy is the ability to do work.
Explanation: Sun gives Light energy. Fire gives Heat energy. Speakers give Sound energy.
Indian Example: We use solar heaters on roofs to heat water using sunlight.
Memory Tip: ☀️🔥🔊 Light helps see, Heat warms, Sound is heard.
Quick Check: What energy does a bulb give? (Light & Heat)
Image: Sun, Bonfire, Radio
4. Simple Machines
Definition: Tools that make our work easier are called machines.
Explanation: A Lever helps lift heavy things (seesaw). A Pulley helps pull things up (water well).
Indian Example: Drawing water from a well using a wheel (Pulley).
Memory Tip: 🚜 Machine = Less Effort, More Work.
Quick Check: Are scissors a machine? (Yes, a lever)
Image: Well with pulley, Seesaw
5. Famous Inventions & Inventors
Definition: Creating something new that did not exist before is an Invention.
Explanation: Thomas Edison invented the Bulb. Graham Bell invented the Telephone. The Wright Brothers invented the Airplane.
Indian Example: Zero was invented by Indian mathematician Aryabhata.
Memory Tip: 💡 Bulb = Edison, ☎️ Phone = Bell.
Quick Check: Who invented the light bulb? (Edison)
Image: Light bulb, Old telephone
6. Space – Sun, Moon & Planets
Definition: Space is the vast emptiness outside Earth's atmosphere.
Explanation: The Sun is a hot ball of gas (Star). The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
Indian Example: We see the Full Moon (Purnima) and New Moon (Amavasya) in India.
Memory Tip: 🌕 Moon changes shape, ☀️ Sun stays same.
Quick Check: Is the Sun a planet? (No, it's a star)
Image: Starry night sky
7. Solar System
Definition: The Sun and the 8 planets revolving around it form the Solar System.
Explanation: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
Indian Example: In Hindi, planets are called 'Grah' (e.g., Mangal Grah is Mars).
Memory Tip: 🌍 Earth is the 3rd planet from Sun.
Quick Check: Which is the largest planet? (Jupiter)
Image: Solar system diagram
8. Satellites
Definition: An object that moves around a planet.
Explanation: Natural Satellite: Moon. Artificial Satellite: Machines sent by humans for TV and weather.
Indian Example: ISRO sent Chandrayaan to the Moon. INSAT helps us watch TV.
Memory Tip: 📡 Dish TV works because of satellites.
Quick Check: Is Moon a satellite? (Yes, natural)
Image: Satellite in orbit
9. Science in Daily Life
Definition: Using scientific knowledge to make life easier.
Explanation: Refrigerators keep food fresh. Cars help us travel. Medicines cure sickness.
Indian Example: Using a pressure cooker to cook Dal faster (uses steam pressure).
Memory Tip: 🏥🚌🏠 Science is everywhere!
Quick Check: Does a fan use electricity? (Yes)
Image: Kitchen appliances, Bus
10. Curiosity & Scientific Thinking
Definition: Wanting to know 'Why' and 'How' things happen.
Explanation: Scientists are curious. They ask questions and do experiments to find truth.
Indian Example: Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam was very curious as a child about how birds fly.
Memory Tip: 🤔 Ask WHY? Learn NEW!
Quick Check: Should we ask questions to learn? (Yes!)
Image: Child thinking with question marks
