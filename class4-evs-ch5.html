⚡ Quick Revision
  • Food gives us energy, helps us grow, and protects us from diseases.
  • Nutrients: Carbs, Proteins, Fats, Vitamins, Minerals.
  • Balanced Diet: Contains all nutrients in right amount + roughage + water.
  • Deficiency Diseases: Caused by lack of specific nutrients (e.g., Night blindness - Vit A).
  • Preservation: Boiling, Drying, Salting, Refrigeration.
  • Hygiene: Wash hands before eating, keep food covered.
  • Water: Must be boiled or filtered before drinking.
