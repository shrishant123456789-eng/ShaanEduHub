MY WRITING NOTES

1. Word Formation & Sentences

We build words using a Root Word (main word) plus parts added before or after.

  • Prefix: Added BEFORE (e.g., Un + Happy = Unhappy).
  • Suffix: Added AFTER (e.g., Help + ful = Helpful).
[Image: Lego blocks joining to form words like Play+ful]
💡 Tip: PREfix comes PRE (before).

Quick Check: Add a suffix to 'Kind'. (Ans: Kindness)

2. Paragraph Writing

A paragraph is a group of sentences about ONE topic. Keep it between 100-120 words.

Structure:

  1. Topic Sentence: Introduces the main idea.
  2. Supporting Details: Explains more (Body).
  3. Concluding Sentence: Wraps up the idea.
🍔 Tip: Think of a Burger! Top Bun (Intro), Patty (Details), Bottom Bun (Conclusion).
3. Story Writing

Every story needs a Beginning, Middle, and End.

  • Beginning: Introduce Characters & Setting (Who/Where).
  • Middle: The Problem or Action (What happened?).
  • End: The Solution (How did it end?).
[Image: A mountain path showing Start -> Problem -> Goal]
4. Dialogue Writing

Writing a conversation between two people. Use speech marks (" ").

Example: "Where are you going?" asked Ravi.
"I am going to school," replied Sita.

🗣️ Tip: Start a new line for a new speaker.
5. Picture Composition

Look at a picture and write about it. Observe details -> Sequence events -> Write meaningful sentences.

Don't just list things ("I see a tree"). Describe actions ("The birds are chirping on the green tree").

6. Descriptive Writing

Use "Sensory Words" to describe a Person, Place, or Object.

  • Sight: Shiny, tall, colorful.
  • Sound: Loud, whisper, crash.
  • Touch: Soft, rough, cold.
  • Smell/Taste: Sweet, fresh, burnt.
[Image: Five senses icons - Eye, Ear, Hand, Nose, Tongue]
7. Creative Vocabulary

Avoid boring words! Use Strong Verbs and Adjectives.

  • Instead of "Said" → Use "Shouted", "Whispered", "Mumbled".
  • Instead of "Walked" → Use "Marched", "Strolled", "Ran".
8. Editing & Rewriting

Always check your work (Proofreading).

  • Check Capital Letters.
  • Check Punctuation (., ?, !).
  • Check Spelling.

Example: "i like apple" → "I like apples."
