📘 Journey Notes

1. Going Places (Transport) 🚗✈️

We travel from one place to another using different vehicles. This is called Transport.
There are 3 main ways to travel:

  • Land Transport: Vehicles that move on roads or tracks.
    Examples: Car 🚘, Bus 🚌, Train 🚆, Bicycle 🚲.
  • Water Transport: Vehicles that move on water.
    Examples: Ship 🚢, Boat 🛶, Yacht.
  • Air Transport: Vehicles that fly in the sky. It is the fastest way!
    Examples: Aeroplane ✈️, Helicopter 🚁.

[Image: Land, Water and Air transport vehicles]

2. Fast & Slow Transport 🏎️🐢

Some vehicles go very fast, and some are slow.

Fast Vehicles: Aeroplane, Train, Car. They use an engine and fuel (Petrol/Diesel). ⛽

Slow Vehicles: Bicycle, Rickshaw, Bullock Cart 🐂. They are often pulled by humans or animals.

3. Public vs. Private 🚌🚘

  • Private Transport: Your own vehicle. Only your family travels in it. (Car, Scooter).
  • Public Transport: Many people travel together. We buy a ticket. (Bus, Metro, Train).

4. Road Safety Rules 🚦

We must be careful on the road!

  • Red Light: Stop! 🛑
  • Yellow Light: Wait / Get Ready. ⚠️
  • Green Light: Go! 🟢
  • Always walk on the Footpath. 🚶‍♂️
  • Cross the road only at the Zebra Crossing. 🦓

[Image: Traffic Light and Zebra Crossing]

5. Talking to Others (Communication) 📞📬

Sending messages or talking to someone is called Communication.

  • Letters: We write on paper and drop it in a Letter Box. The Postman brings it. 📮
  • Telephone: Used to talk to people far away. ☎️
  • Mobile Phone: A small phone we can carry in our pocket. We can send messages too! 📱
  • Internet: We use computers to send Emails and learn new things very fast. 💻
