1. Types of Plants

Plants are of different shapes and sizes. They are grouped into:

**Trees**: Big, tall and strong plants. They have a hard woody stem called Trunk. (e.g., Mango, Banyan)
**Shrubs**: Smaller than trees. Bushy plants with hard woody stems. (e.g., Rose, Hibiscus)
**Herbs**: Very small plants with soft green stems. (e.g., Mint, Coriander)
**Climbers**: Weak stems. Need support to grow up. (e.g., Money Plant, Grapevine)
**Creepers**: Weak stems. Grow along the ground. (e.g., Watermelon, Pumpkin)
💡 Tip: Climbers climb UP, Creepers creep ON GROUND.

2. Parts of a Plant

Just like we have body parts, plants have parts too:

**Root**: Grows under the soil.
**Stem**: Grows above the soil.
**Leaf**: Green flat part.
**Flower**: Beautiful colorful part.
**Fruit**: Tasty part that has seeds.
**Seed**: Baby plant is inside this.
[attachment_0](attachment)

3. Functions of Plant Parts

**Root**: Holds the plant tightly in soil. Absorbs water.
**Stem**: Carries water and food to all parts. Supports the plant.
**Leaf**: Makes food for the plant.
**Flower**: Turns into a fruit.
**Fruit**: Protects the seeds.

4. How Plants Make Food

Plants are the only living things that make their own food!

The **Leaf** is the Kitchen of the plant.
Ingredients needed: **Sunlight + Air + Water + Green Colour (Chlorophyll)**.
This process is called Photosynthesis.
[attachment_1](attachment)
💡 Tip: No Sun = No Food for plants!

5. Uses of Plants

Plants give us many things. They are our best friends.

We get wood for furniture.
We get cotton for clothes.
We get paper, gum, and rubber.
They give us fresh air (Oxygen) to breathe.

6. Food, Medicine, Shelter

**Food**: We eat roots (Carrot), stems (Sugarcane), leaves (Spinach), fruits (Apple).
**Medicine**: Tulsi, Neem, Turmeric, Clove cure our sickness.
**Shelter**: Birds make nests on trees. Monkeys live on trees.

7. What Plants Need

To stay alive and grow, plants need:

1. Air
2. Water
3. Sunlight
4. Soil (Nutrients)

8. Germination of Seeds

The journey of a seed becoming a plant.

When a seed gets air, water, and warmth, it opens up.
A tiny baby plant comes out. This is called **Germination**.
Example: Soaking chana (gram) seeds sprouts them.

9. Importance of Plants

Without plants, there would be no food.
Without plants, there would be no Oxygen.
Plants make the earth beautiful and green.
They bring rain and keep the air cool.

10. Conservation of Plants

We must **NOT** cut down trees.
We should plant more trees (Afforestation).
Water the plants daily.
"Save Trees, Save Earth".
