📘 Learning Notes

1. The World of Colours 🌈

Everything around us has a color!

  • Primary Colors: Red 🔴, Blue 🔵, Yellow 🟡.
  • Green: Color of grass and leaves. 💚
  • Orange: Color of an Orange fruit and Carrots. 🧡
  • Purple: Color of Brinjal and Grapes. 💜
  • White: Color of Milk and Snow. 🥛
  • Black: Color of Crow and Hair. 🐈‍⬛

2. Shapes Around Us 🔺🔵

Look at the things around you. They have different shapes!

  • Circle: Round like a Ball or Sun. No corners. ⚪
  • Square: Like a Box or Handkerchief. It has 4 equal sides. 🟥
  • Rectangle: Like a TV screen or Mobile phone. Opposite sides are equal. 📟
  • Triangle: Like a Sandwich or Pizza slice. It has 3 corners. 🔺
  • Oval: Like an Egg. 🥚
  • Star: Like the stars in the sky. ⭐

3. Fun with Numbers & Patterns 🔢

Numbers: We use numbers to count things. 1, 2, 3...
Patterns: When shapes or colors repeat in a sequence.
Example: 🔴 🔵 🔴 🔵 ... (Next is 🔴)

4. Opposites & Sorting ↔️

Opposites: Words that mean completely different things.

  • Big 🐘 vs Small 🐜
  • Hot ☕ vs Cold 🍦
  • Up ⬆️ vs Down ⬇️
Sorting: Putting same things together. (Putting all red balls in one basket).
Odd One Out: Finding the thing that is different from others.
