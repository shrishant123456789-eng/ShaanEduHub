Chapter 3: Fractions

📚 Chapter Notes
1. Meaning of Fractions
A fraction is a part of a whole. It has two parts: Numerator (top) and Denominator (bottom).
Numerator = Parts taken. Denominator = Total parts.
Example: In 3/4, 3 is Numerator, 4 is Denominator.
[Image: Circle divided into 4 parts, 3 shaded]
💡 Tip: Down = Denominator (Both start with D).
2. Types of Fractions
Proper: Numerator < Denominator (Small head). e.g., 2/5.
Improper: Numerator > Denominator (Big head). e.g., 7/4.
Mixed: Whole Number + Fraction. e.g., 1 1/2.
[Image: Chart showing 1/2, 5/2, 2 1/2]
💡 Tip: Improper is "Top Heavy".
3. Equivalent Fractions
Fractions that have the same value even if they look different.
Multiply or divide top and bottom by the same number.
1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 50/100
[Image: 1/2 pizza vs 2/4 pizza]
4. Simplest Form
When the numerator and denominator have no common factor other than 1.
Divide both by HCF.
4/8 -> Divide by 4 -> 1/2
[Image: Cutting numbers down]
5. Comparing Fractions
Same Denominator: Bigger numerator is bigger.
Same Numerator: Smaller denominator is bigger.
Unlike: Cross multiply or use LCM.
[Image: Comparing 1/3 and 1/5]
💡 Tip: Cross Multiply to check winner.
6. Addition of Fractions
Like Fractions: Add numerators, keep denominator same.
Unlike Fractions: Find LCM, make denominators same, then add.
1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5
[Image: Adding shaded parts]
7. Subtraction of Fractions
Same rule as addition. For unlike fractions, find LCM first.
4/7 - 1/7 = 3/7
[Image: Removing slices]
8. Like & Unlike Fractions
Like: Same denominators (1/7, 3/7, 5/7).
Unlike: Different denominators (1/2, 1/3, 1/4).
[Image: Group of fractions]
9. Operations on Mixed Fractions
First convert Mixed to Improper. Then solve. Then convert back to Mixed if needed.
1 1/2 + 2 1/2 = 3/2 + 5/2 = 8/2 = 4
[Image: Steps: Multiply, Add, Keep Denominator]
10. Fraction of a Number
"Of" means Multiply.
Divide by denominator, multiply by numerator.
3/4 of 20 -> (20 ÷ 4) x 3 = 5 x 3 = 15
[Image: 20 stars, circling 15]
11. Word Problems
Read carefully. Check for keywords: "Left" (-), "Total" (+), "Of" (x).
Rohan ate 1/2. Sister ate 1/4. Total = 3/4.
[Image: Boy eating cake]
