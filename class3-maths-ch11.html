1. Fraction as Part of a Whole 🍕

Fraction means a part of something whole.

Imagine a big Pizza! 🍕 If you eat one slice, you are eating a fraction of the pizza.

A Fraction has two numbers:

2. Basic Types of Fractions 🍫

Let's look at a Chocolate Bar.

1. Half (½): When we divide something into 2 equal parts.

2. One-Third (⅓): When we divide something into 3 equal parts.

3. One-Fourth (¼): Also called a Quarter. 4 equal parts.

3. Fractions in Daily Life 🍎

Fractions are everywhere!

  • Cutting an Apple in half 🍎
  • Sharing a Cake 🎂
  • Coloring parts of a drawing 🎨

Important: Fractions must have EQUAL parts.

5. Introduction to Decimals 🔢

Decimals are another way to write fractions.

When we divide a whole into 10 equal parts, each part is One-Tenth (1/10).

1/10 = 0.1
2/10 = 0.2
5/10 = 0.5 (This is Half!)

The dot (.) is called the Decimal Point.
