1. Indian Currency 🇮🇳

Hello Friends! 👋 Today we are going to the market. But wait! To buy things, we need Money (Paisa).

In India, our currency is called Rupee (₹) and Paise (p).

Coins (Sikke): Hard and round metal money.

We use coins for small amounts like ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, and ₹10.

Notes (Kagaz ke note): Paper money.

Notes are for bigger amounts.

Symbol of Rupee is ₹

2. Counting Money 🪙

Let's count how much money is in my piggy bank!

If I have two ₹5 coins, how much is it?

5 + 5 = ₹10

If I have one ₹10 note and one ₹5 coin?

10 + 5 = ₹15

Tip: Always count the big notes first, then the small coins.

3. Addition of Money ➕

Raju bought a pencil for ₹5 and an eraser for ₹3. How much did he spend?

We simply ADD the numbers.

₹5 (Pencil)
+ ₹3 (Eraser)
-------
₹8 (Total)

Sometimes we have rupees and paise. We add rupees with rupees and paise with paise.

4. Subtraction of Money ➖

Raju gave ₹10 to the shopkeeper. The total cost was ₹8. How much money will he get back?

We SUBTRACT to find the Change (Bacha hua paisa).

₹10 (Given)
- ₹ 8 (Cost)
-------
₹ 2 (Change)

5. Buying & Selling 🛍️

When we go to a shop:

  • The price on the tag is the Cost.
  • We give money to the shopkeeper.
  • If we give more money, we get Change back.
  • The shopkeeper gives us a Bill or Receipt.

6. Word Problems 📝

Let's solve a story!

"Sita has ₹50. She wants to buy a doll for ₹30 and a chocolate for ₹10. Does she have enough money?"

Step 1: Find total cost.
30 + 10 = ₹40.

Step 2: Compare.
She has ₹50. Cost is ₹40.

Answer: Yes! She has enough money. (50 > 40)
