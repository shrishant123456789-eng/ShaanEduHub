1. What is the Internet? 🌐

Imagine a giant spider web that connects the whole world!

[Image: A drawing of Earth with lines connecting computers]

The Internet is a huge network of computers connected together. It helps people share information, talk to each other, and learn new things from anywhere in the world.

Indian Example: Just like Indian Railways connects different cities in India, the Internet connects computers all over the world.
💡 Tip: Internet = INTERconnected NETwork.

2. Uses of Internet 💻

Why do we use this magic network?

  • Education: Taking online classes on Zoom or Google Meet.
  • Communication: Sending messages on WhatsApp or Emails.
  • Entertainment: Watching cartoons on YouTube.
  • Information: Searching for "Tiger Project" details.
[Image: Kids learning on a laptop]
Real Life: During holidays, you video call your grandmother who lives in another city. That is the Internet!

3. Web Browsers & Search Engines 🔍

Don't get confused between the Car and the Driver!


Web Browser (The Car): It is the software you open to see the internet.
Examples: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari.


Search Engine (The Driver/Map): It is a website that helps you find things.
Examples: Google, Bing, Yahoo.

[Image: Icons of Chrome, Edge and Google Logo]
💡 Tip: You open Chrome (Browser) to visit Google (Search Engine).

4. Websites and URLs 🏠

Every house has an address. Every website has an address too!

Website: A collection of webpages (like a book has pages).
URL: The address of the website. It stands for Uniform Resource Locator.

Example: https://www.wikipedia.org
Just like your home address leads the postman to your house, the URL leads the browser to the website.

5. Email Basics 📧

Email stands for Electronic Mail.

It is like writing a letter, but it reaches instantly!

Email Address Parts:
rahul.student (Username)
@ (At symbol - Must have!)
gmail.com (Domain name)

[Image: An envelope with an @ symbol stamp]
Use: You can send your holiday homework to your teacher via email without going to school!

6. Online Safety Rules 🛡️

The internet is useful, but we must be safe. Follow these Golden Rules:

  1. Strong Passwords: Never use "123456". Use a mix of letters and numbers (e.g., "Tiger#2024").
  2. Don't Share Personal Data: Never tell strangers your address, school name, or phone number.
  3. Safe Sites: Look for the 🔒 Lock Icon next to the website address. It means it is safe.
💡 Tip: S.M.A.R.T. -> Stay Safe, Don't Meet, Accepting Files is risky, Reliable sources, Tell an adult.

7. Cyber Etiquette for Kids 🤝

Manners are important, even online!

  • Be Kind: Do not use bad words.
  • NO SHOUTING: Writing in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS means you are shouting. Don't do it.
  • Respect Privacy: Don't share your friend's photo without asking.

8. Simple Internet Projects 📝

Try these with your parents:

  1. Open Google Chrome.
  2. Type "Map of India" in the search bar.
  3. Click on "Images" to see the map.
  4. Open Notepad, type a hello message, and pretend to save it as an email!

Important Questions to Learn

Q1. What is the full form of URL?
Ans: Uniform Resource Locator.

Q2. Which symbol is mandatory in an email address?
Ans: The '@' (at) symbol.

Q3. Give two examples of Web Browsers.
Ans: Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Q4. Why should we look for a lock icon 🔒?
Ans: It shows that the website is secure and safe to use.

Q5. What does writing in ALL CAPS mean?
Ans: It is considered rude, like shouting at someone.
