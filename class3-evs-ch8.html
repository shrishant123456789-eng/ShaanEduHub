Chapter Notes

1. Meaning of Shelter
Definition: A shelter is a place where we live. It covers us and keeps us safe.
Teacher Says: Imagine standing in heavy rain without an umbrella. You get wet! A house is like a big umbrella. It protects us from heat, cold, rain, wind, thieves, and wild animals.
Image: A happy family inside a house while it rains outside
Indian Example: Birds make nests on trees. We build houses on land. Both are shelters!
Tip: Shelter = Safety!
Quick Check: Does a house protect us from thieves? (Yes/No)
2. Types of Houses
People build different houses based on money and climate.

A. Kutcha House
Made of: Mud, straw, bamboo, and dry leaves.
These are not very strong. They are mostly found in villages.
Example: A hut in a village.
B. Pucca House
Made of: Bricks, cement, iron, steel, and wood.
These are very strong and last for many years. Found in cities and towns.
Example: Bungalows and Flats (Apartments) in cities like Mumbai or Delhi.
C. Special Houses
  • Tents: Made of canvas cloth. Used by soldiers and campers. Easy to fold and carry.
  • Igloos: Made of snow blocks. Found in very cold places. Eskimos live here.
  • Houseboats: Houses floating on water! Found in Kashmir and Kerala.
Image: Collage of Hut, Building, Tent, Igloo, Houseboat
Tip: Kutcha = Weak (Mud), Pucca = Strong (Cement).
Quick Check: Can we fold a Pucca house?
3. Houses in Different Regions
The roof of a house depends on the weather of that place.
Teacher Says:
1. Flat Roofs: In hot places like plains (our cities).
2. Sloping Roofs: In places with heavy rain or snow (hills). The slope helps water and snow slide off easily.
3. Stilt Houses: In places with heavy floods (like Assam). They are built on tall bamboo poles to keep water away.
Image: A house with sloping roof vs flat roof
Indian Example: Houses in Shimla have sloping roofs because of snow.
Tip: Slope = Slide (Snow slides down).
4. Materials Used to Build Houses
Different materials make a house strong or beautiful.
We need:
• Bricks & Stones: For walls.
• Cement: To join bricks together.
• Iron & Steel: For pillars and strength.
• Wood: For doors and windows.
• Glass: For window panes.
Image: Bricks, Cement bag, Wood pile
Tip: Brick + Cement = Strong Wall!
5. Keeping Our Home Clean
A clean home is a healthy home.
Teacher Says: Dust and dirt bring germs. Germs make us sick. We must:
• Dust furniture daily.
• Sweep and mop the floor.
• Remove cobwebs (spider homes) from corners.
Example: Using a broom (Jhadu) every morning.
Tip: No Dirt = No Disease!
6. Rooms in a House
Different rooms are for different work:
• Drawing/Living Room: Where we sit with guests.
• Bedroom: Where we sleep.
• Kitchen: Where food is cooked.
• Bathroom: Where we bathe.
• Dining Room: Where we eat food.
• Study Room: Where children study.
Image: Cross-section of a house showing rooms
Quick Check: Where do you cook food?
7. Furniture and Household Items
Items make our life easy:
• Sofa and Chairs (Living room)
• Bed and Wardrobe (Bedroom)
• Gas stove and Utensils (Kitchen)
• Bucket and Mug (Bathroom)
Tip: Everything has its own place!
8. Need for Shelter
Why do we need a house?
We cannot live outside because:
• The Sun is too hot.
• Rain makes us wet.
• Winter is too cold.
• Thieves might steal our things.
• Wild animals can hurt us.
Example: Even a dog needs a kennel to sleep safely.
9. Care of Our Home
Taking care of home means:
• Keeping things in their right place.
• Putting garbage in dustbins.
• Keeping drains covered so mosquitoes don't breed.
• Planting trees around the house.
Tip: Clean Home = Happy Home!
10. Safe and Comfortable Homes
A good home allows fresh air and sunlight to enter.
Teacher Says:
• Sunlight: Kills germs. So, windows are important.
• Ventilation: Windows let fresh air in and bad air out.
• Wire Netting: Put on windows to stop flies and mosquitoes but let air in.
Image: Window with wire mesh and sunlight coming in
Quick Check: What kills germs naturally? (Sunlight)

Important Questions

Q1. Why do we need a house?
Ans: We need a house to protect us from heat, cold, rain, thieves, and wild animals.
Q2. What is a Kutcha house made of?
Ans: A Kutcha house is made of mud, straw, wood, and dry leaves.
Q3. What is a Pucca house made of?
Ans: A Pucca house is made of bricks, cement, steel, and glass.
Q4. Who lives in Igloos?
Ans: Eskimos live in Igloos.
Q5. Where are Houseboats found in India?
Ans: Houseboats are found in Kashmir and Kerala.
Q6. Why do houses in hills have sloping roofs?
Ans: Sloping roofs help snow and rainwater to slide down easily.
Exp: If the roof was flat, snow would collect and break the roof.
Q7. What is a tent made of?
Ans: A tent is made of thick cloth called canvas.
Q8. Why should we have wire netting on windows?
Ans: Wire netting stops mosquitoes and flies from entering but allows fresh air to come in.
Q9. Why is sunlight good for our home?
Ans: Sunlight kills germs and keeps the house dry and cheerful.
Q10. Where should we throw garbage?
Ans: We should always throw garbage in a covered dustbin.
Q11. What is a Stilt house?
Ans: A house built on tall bamboo poles to avoid flood water.
Q12. What is a bungalow?
Ans: A bungalow is a big Pucca house with many rooms, usually one storey.
Q13. What is an apartment?
Ans: Apartments are tall buildings with many houses (flats) in them.
Q14. Which room is used for bathing?
Ans: The Bathroom is used for bathing.
Q15. In which room do we entertain guests?
Ans: In the Drawing room or Living room.
Q16. What material is used to make window panes?
Ans: Glass is used to make window panes.
Q17. Why do we sweep our floors?
Ans: To remove dust and dirt.
Q18. What are ventilators?
Ans: Small openings near the ceiling to let hot air go out.
Q19. Why should drains be covered?
Ans: Covered drains stop insects and bad smells from spreading.
Q20. What is a caravan?
Ans: A house on wheels that can move from place to place.
Q21. What keeps a house cool in summer?
Ans: Thick walls and high ceilings help keep a house cool.
Q22. Name two things made of wood in a house.
Ans: Doors and cupboards.
Q23. Why do people in cities live in multi-storey buildings?
Ans: Because there is less space (land) in cities.
Q24. What is a temporary house?
Ans: A house that can be moved or is not permanent, like a tent.
Q25. What is a permanent house?
Ans: A house that cannot be moved, like a Pucca house.
Q26. Why do we need open spaces around a house?
Ans: For fresh air and for children to play.
Q27. How can we decorate our house?
Ans: With flowers, paintings, and rangoli.
Q28. What should we do with spider webs?
Ans: We should remove them regularly.
Q29. Why are floors made of cement or tiles?
Ans: Because they are easy to clean and wash.
Q30. What is the most important thing in a home?
Ans: The people living in it and their love for each other!
