☀️ The Solar System

Our Solar System is like a big family in space.

The Sun: It is a big ball of hot fire. It is a Star, not a planet! It gives us heat and light.

The Planets: There are 8 Planets that go around the Sun.

1. Mercury (Smallest)

2. Venus (Hottest)

3. Earth (Our Home - Blue Planet)

4. Mars (Red Planet)

5. Jupiter (Largest)

6. Saturn (Has beautiful rings)

7. Uranus (Cold)

8. Neptune (Farthest)

The Moon: It is Earth's natural satellite. It changes shape (Phases of Moon).

💡 Trick to remember order: My Very Efficient Mother Just Served Us Noodles.

👨‍🚀 Famous Astronauts

Astronauts are brave people who travel to space.

Neil Armstrong: The first person to walk on the Moon (1969).

Famous line: "One small step for man..."

Rakesh Sharma: The first Indian to go to space.

Famous line: "Saare Jahan Se Achha."

Kalpana Chawla: The first woman of Indian origin in space. She was very brave.

🌍 Continents & Oceans

Our Earth is made of land and water.

Continents (Land): There are 7 Continents.

  • Asia: The Largest continent (India is here!).
  • Australia: The Smallest continent.
  • Others: Africa, North America, South America, Europe, Antarctica.

Oceans (Water): There are 5 Oceans.

  • Pacific Ocean: The Largest and Deepest.
  • Arctic Ocean: The Smallest.
  • Others: Atlantic, Indian, Southern.

💡 Tip: Imagine 'PAISA' for oceans (Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic).

[Image: World Map showing 7 Continents]
🌞 Day & Night

Why does the sun rise and set?

Rotation: The Earth spins like a top on its axis. This causes Day and Night. It takes 24 hours.

Revolution: The Earth goes around the Sun. This causes Seasons (Summer, Winter). It takes 365 days (1 Year).
