📘 Study Notes

1. Helping Parents at Home 🏠

Helping means doing work together. Our parents work very hard. We must help them.
🌟 You can clean your room.
🌟 You can set the plates on the table.
🌟 You can help mom in the kitchen.
Story: Ravi saw his mom was tired. He filled water bottles and kept them in the fridge. Mom was so happy!

❓ Activity: How will you help your mom today?

2. Helping Friends 🤝

Friends help each other.
📚 If your friend forgets a book, share yours.
📝 If they don't understand homework, explain it to them.
Helping makes friendship strong and beautiful!

3. Helping Elders 👵👴

Elders like grandparents need our help because they are old.
✅ Give them your seat in a bus.
✅ Hold their hand while walking.
✅ Carry their bags.
Being gentle with elders shows good manners.

4. Helping Needy People 🍞

Needy people are those who do not have enough food or clothes.
🌟 We should give them food.
🌟 We can give our old clothes to them.
Kindness is giving to others without asking for anything back.

5. Sharing Food & Toys 🍎🧸

Sharing is Caring!
If you have two apples, give one to your friend.
Let friends play with your toys.
When we share, everyone becomes happy. Selfish children play alone, but kind children have many friends.

6. Caring for Animals 🐶🐱

Animals cannot speak, but they feel pain and hunger.
✅ Give milk to a cat or bread to a dog.
✅ Put water in a bowl for birds.
🚫 Never throw stones at animals. That is cruel.

7. Being Gentle & Kind 💖

Gentle means being soft and careful.
🌟 Speak softly, do not shout.
🌟 Help someone stand up if they fall.
🌟 Do not push or hit anyone.
A kind heart is the most beautiful thing!

8. Saying Kind Words 😊🗣️

Use magic words to spread happiness:
🗣️ "Please" when you want help.
🗣️ "Sorry" if you hurt someone.
🗣️ "Thank You" when someone helps you.
These words are small but very powerful!
