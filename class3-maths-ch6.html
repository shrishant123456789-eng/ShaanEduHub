1. 2D Shapes (Flat Shapes) 🟦🔺

These shapes are flat like paper. They have Sides and Corners.

🟥
Square
4 equal sides
4 corners
▭
Rectangle
Opposite sides equal
4 corners
🔺
Triangle
3 sides
3 corners
⚪
Circle
0 sides
0 corners

Examples: 🍕 Pizza slice is a Triangle. 📺 TV screen is a Rectangle.

🔎 Activity: Find a Circle in your room right now!

2. 3D Shapes (Solid Shapes) 📦⚽

These shapes are solid. You can hold them. They have Faces, Edges, and Corners.

🧊 Cube (Dice)
📦 Cuboid (Book)
⚽ Sphere (Ball)
🍦 Cone (Ice Cream)
🛢️ Cylinder (Can)

A Sphere has no corners and rolls easily!

3. Types of Lines ➖

Lines help us draw shapes.

  • 📏 Horizontal (Sleeping Line): Like a sleeping person. 🛌 ──
  • 🗼 Vertical (Standing Line): Like a tree or pole. 🌲 │
  • 🎋 Slanting Line: Like a slide or ladder. 🎢 /
  • 〰️ Curved Line: Like a snake or rainbow. 🌈 🐍

4. Angles (Corners) 📐

When two lines meet, they make an Angle (Corner).

└ Right Angle
Like the letter 'L'
📒 Book Corner

Look at the corner of your phone screen. That is an angle!

5. Perimeter (The Boundary) 🚶‍♂️

Perimeter is the total distance around a shape.

Imagine walking around a park 🏞️.
Start → Walk Side 1 → Side 2 → Side 3 → Side 4 → Finish.

Perimeter = Sum of all sides

Example: A square with side 2cm.
Perimeter = 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8cm.

6. Symmetry (Mirror Image) 🦋

If you fold a shape and both halves match exactly, it is Symmetrical.

🦋 Butterfly
🍁 Leaf
❤️ Heart

The line that divides them is called the Line of Symmetry.
