📘 Study Notes

1. My Beautiful Country 🇮🇳

The name of our country is India. It is also called Bharat.
People of India are called Indians.
We love our country very much! ❤️

Capital City: The capital of India is New Delhi.

2. Our National Flag 🚩

Our flag is called the Tiranga (Tricolor). It has 3 colors:

  • Saffron (Orange): At the top. It stands for strength and courage. 🟧
  • White: In the middle. It stands for peace and truth. ⬜
  • Green: At the bottom. It stands for growth and life. 🟩
In the center of the white band, there is a blue wheel called the Ashoka Chakra. It has 24 spokes. ☸️

3. National Symbols 🦚🐅

Every country has special symbols. Here are ours:

  • National Animal: Tiger 🐅 (Strong and brave).
  • National Bird: Peacock 🦚 (Beautiful and colorful).
  • National Flower: Lotus 🌸 (Grows in water, very pretty).
  • National Fruit: Mango 🥭 (King of fruits).
  • National Tree: Banyan Tree 🌳.
  • National River: The Ganga 🌊.

4. Anthem & Emblem 🎶🦁

National Anthem: "Jana Gana Mana". We sing it at school. We must stand straight when we sing it. It was written by Rabindranath Tagore.

National Emblem: It has 4 Lions standing back to back. We can see it on coins and notes. 🦁🦁🦁

5. Famous People & Places 🏰👴

Freedom Fighters:
Mahatma Gandhi is called the "Father of the Nation". He taught us truth and non-violence.
Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister. Children called him "Chacha Nehru".

Famous Places:
📍 Taj Mahal is in Agra. It is white and beautiful.
📍 Red Fort is in Delhi. The Prime Minister hoists the flag here.
