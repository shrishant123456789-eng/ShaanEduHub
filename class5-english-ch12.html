1. Error Detection & Correction

Concept

Sentences often have hidden mistakes in grammar, spelling, or punctuation. To be a good editor, you must spot them.

Wrong: She don't like apples.
Right: She doesn't like apples.

💡 Tip: Check Subject-Verb Agreement first! He/She/It needs 's' or 'es' in verbs.

[Visual: Magnifying glass over text]

Quick Check: Find the error: "They is playing." (Ans: is -> are)

2. Mixed Grammar Practice

Concept

In real life, we use Tenses, Prepositions, and Adjectives all at once. Mixed grammar helps us connect these rules.

"Yesterday (Past), I went to the market (Preposition) and bought red apples (Adjective)."

💡 Tip: Read the whole sentence to understand the time (Tense) and position (Preposition).

3. Reading-Writing Integration

Concept

Read a text and then write a response. This connects your understanding with your expression.

Read: "Rohan broke the vase."
Write (Response): "Rohan should apologize to his mother."

[Visual: Open book with pen]

4. Listening-Speaking Integration

Concept

Listen carefully to instructions or stories, then speak (or write) your answer clearly.

Input: "Turn left at the post office."
Output: "Okay, I will turn left after the post office."

5. Critical Thinking

Concept

Thinking beyond the text. Asking "Why?", "How?", and "What if?".

Fact: The river is dirty.
Critical Thought: "We should stop throwing plastic to clean it."

💡 Tip: Use words like 'Because', 'Therefore', and 'However'.

6. Real-Life Communication

Concept

Using English for daily tasks like shopping, asking directions, or introducing friends.

Shopkeeper: "May I help you?"
You: "Yes, I need a packet of milk, please."

7. Mini Projects

Project Ideas

  • Poster Making: Design a poster on 'Save Water' with a catchy slogan.
  • Short Speech: Prepare a 1-minute speech on 'My Favorite Hobby'.
  • Mini Storybook: Fold papers to make a 4-page book with drawings.
[Visual: Art supplies and paper]

8. Revision & Assessment

Concept

Checking your own work is the final step. Always revise before submitting.

💡 Tip: Read your answer backward to catch spelling mistakes!
