1. Meaning of Force

Definition: A push or a pull acting on an object is called Force.

Explanation: To move anything, you either push it away or pull it towards you. This action is force.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Opening a door (Push/Pull) or kicking a football in the park.
🖼️ Visual: Boy pushing a swing
💡 Tip: Remember 'P-P' -> Push or Pull!
❓ Check: Is kicking a ball a push or a pull? (Ans: Push)
2. Effects of Force

Force can do four things: Move a stationary object, Stop a moving object, Change direction, Change shape.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Mother rolling chapati dough (Changes shape). Goalkeeper catching a ball (Stops it).
🖼️ Visual: Rolling dough (Shape change)
💡 Tip: MS-DS (Move, Stop, Direction, Shape)
❓ Check: Squeezing a sponge changes its ____? (Ans: Shape)
3. Contact Forces

Definition: Forces that act only when objects touch each other.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Hitting a cricket ball with a bat. The bat must touch the ball.
🖼️ Visual: Bat hitting ball
❓ Check: Is friction a contact force? (Ans: Yes)
4. Non-Contact Forces

Definition: Forces that act from a distance without touching.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: A magnet pulling iron nails from a distance. Leaves falling from a tree (Gravity).
🖼️ Visual: Magnet attracting nails
❓ Check: Does gravity need touch? (Ans: No)
5. Muscular Force

Definition: The force exerted by our muscles.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Lifting your heavy school bag or pulling a rickshaw.
🖼️ Visual: Rickshaw puller
💡 Tip: Muscles = Body Power.
❓ Check: Which force helps you run? (Ans: Muscular)
6. Gravitational Force

Definition: The force by which Earth pulls everything towards its center.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: If you drop a laddu, it falls down, never up!
🖼️ Visual: Apple falling from tree
💡 Tip: Earth is a giant magnet for things.
❓ Check: Who discovered gravity? (Ans: Newton)
7. Magnetic Force

Definition: The force exerted by a magnet on iron, nickel, or cobalt.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Stickers on the fridge door stick due to magnets.
🖼️ Visual: Horseshoe magnet
❓ Check: Do magnets attract wood? (Ans: No)
8. Friction

Definition: A force that opposes motion. It acts when two surfaces rub against each other.

Effect: It slows down or stops moving objects.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: A rolling ball stops on the ground after some time due to friction.
🖼️ Visual: Ball stopping on grass
❓ Check: Smooth surfaces have ___ friction. (Ans: Less)
9. Advantages of Friction

It helps us walk without slipping. It helps us write with a pen. It helps brakes stop a car.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: You can hold a glass of milk because of friction between your hand and the glass.
🖼️ Visual: Writing on paper
💡 Tip: Friction is a Grip!
❓ Check: Can we walk on ice easily? (Ans: No, less friction)
10. Disadvantages of Friction

It causes wear and tear (damage). It produces unwanted heat. It slows down speed.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: The soles of your school shoes get worn out after a year.
🖼️ Visual: Worn out shoe sole
❓ Check: Does friction produce heat? (Ans: Yes)
11. Work

Definition: Work is said to be done ONLY when force applied moves an object through a distance.

Formula: Work = Force × Distance.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Pushing a wall all day = No Work (Wall didn't move). Pushing a vegetable cart = Work Done.
🖼️ Visual: Man pushing a wall (X) vs Cart (✓)
❓ Check: If distance is 0, is work done? (Ans: No)
12. Energy

Definition: The ability or capacity to do work.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: You need food to play cricket. Food gives you energy.
🖼️ Visual: Battery indicator full
❓ Check: SI Unit of Energy? (Ans: Joule)
13. Types of Energy

There are many forms: Mechanical, Heat, Light, Sound, Electrical, Chemical, etc.

🖼️ Visual: Icons of Sun, Bulb, Speaker
❓ Check: Energy from the sun is? (Ans: Solar)
14. Mechanical Energy

Energy an object has due to its position (Potential) or motion (Kinetic).

🇮🇳 Daily Life: A stretched rubber band (Potential). A moving cricket ball (Kinetic).
🖼️ Visual: Bow and Arrow
💡 Tip: ME = PE + KE
❓ Check: Water in a dam has which energy? (Ans: Potential)
15. Heat Energy

Energy that gives us the sensation of warmth.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Burning wood in a Chulha (stove) for cooking. The Sun.
🖼️ Visual: Bonfire
❓ Check: Is Sun a source of heat? (Ans: Yes)
16. Light Energy

Energy that helps us to see things.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Tubelights, Diyas (lamps) during Diwali, Sun.
🖼️ Visual: Diya lamp
❓ Check: Plants use light for? (Ans: Photosynthesis)
17. Sound Energy

Energy produced by vibrations that we can hear.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Ringing of a temple bell or school bell.
🖼️ Visual: Temple Bell
❓ Check: Can sound travel in vacuum? (Ans: No)
18. Electrical Energy

Energy generated by the movement of electrons. Used to run appliances.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Fans, TV, and Fridge run on electricity.
🖼️ Visual: Electric Plug
❓ Check: Do we get shock from electricity? (Ans: Yes, be careful)
19. Sources of Energy

Resources from which we get energy. Main source is the Sun.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Sun, Wind, Water, Coal, Petrol.
🖼️ Visual: Sun shining bright
❓ Check: Ultimate source of energy? (Ans: Sun)
20. Renewable Energy

Sources that can be replenished and never run out.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Solar panels on rooftops, Windmills.
🖼️ Visual: Solar Panel
💡 Tip: Re-new-able = Can be New again.
❓ Check: Is wind renewable? (Ans: Yes)
21. Non-Renewable Energy

Sources that are limited and will finish one day.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Coal used in trains, Petrol in scooters.
🖼️ Visual: Petrol Pump
❓ Check: Is Coal renewable? (Ans: No)
22. Conservation of Energy

Saving energy and not wasting it.

🇮🇳 Daily Life: Switching off lights when leaving the room. Using carpooling.
🖼️ Visual: Switch Off Sign
❓ Check: Should we waste water? (Ans: No)
