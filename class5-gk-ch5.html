📝 Study Notes

1. Major Scientific Inventions

Definition: An invention is a new process, machine, or device created by someone.

Image: Lightbulb, Telephone, Computer

Key Facts:

  • Thomas Edison: Light Bulb 💡
  • Alexander Graham Bell: Telephone ☎️
  • Wright Brothers: Airplane ✈️
  • Charles Babbage: Computer 💻
Tip: "Bell" rings on a Telephone!
2. Space Science

Concept: Study of the universe outside Earth.

Image: Solar System
  • Solar System: Sun + 8 Planets.
  • Satellite: Object revolving around a planet (Moon is natural).
  • Gravity: Force pulling us down.
Tip: My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles (Planets order).
3. Indian Space Achievements (ISRO)

ISRO: Indian Space Research Organisation.

Image: Rocket Launch
  • Aryabhata: India's first satellite.
  • Chandrayaan: Mission to Moon 🌕.
  • Mangalyaan: Mission to Mars 🔴 (First Asian nation to reach Mars orbit).
  • Vikram Sarabhai: Father of Indian Space Program.
4. Renewable vs Non-Renewable Energy

Renewable (Clean): Sun (Solar), Wind, Water. Never runs out.

Non-Renewable (Limited): Coal, Oil, Gas. Can run out and causes pollution.

Image: Solar Panel vs Coal Factory
Tip: Sun shines every day (Renewable)!
5. Modern Technology in Daily Life

Tech makes life easier and faster.

  • Internet: Connects computers worldwide.
  • GPS: Global Positioning System (Maps).
  • WiFi: Wireless Internet.
  • Smartphone: Mini computer in pocket.
6. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Definition: Smart machines that think like humans.

Image: Robot Brain

Examples: Voice assistants (Siri, Alexa), Face ID unlock, YouTube recommendations.

Tip: AI = Artificial (Not Real) + Intelligence (Brain).
7. Robotics & Automation

Robot: A machine programmed to do tasks automatically.

  • Uses: Making cars in factories, drones for delivery, exploring Mars.
  • Automation: Working without human help.
8. Science in Real Life

Science is everywhere!

  • Friction: Helps us walk without slipping.
  • Evaporation: Drying clothes in sun.
  • Photosynthesis: Plants making food using sunlight.
