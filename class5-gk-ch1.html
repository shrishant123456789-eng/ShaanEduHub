1. Earth: Our Home Planet
[Image: Earth showing Axis & Layers]

Definition: The Earth is the 3rd planet from the Sun. It is the only known planet with life.

Basic → Advanced

Basic: It looks round like a ball.
Advanced: Its true shape is a "Geoid" (bulges at equator, flat at poles).

Layers: Crust (outside), Mantle (middle), Core (center).

Movements:
Rotation = Spins on axis (24 hrs) → Causes Day/Night.
Revolution = Goes around Sun (365 days) → Causes Seasons.

India Context: When it is day in India, it is night in USA because of Rotation.
💡 Tip: Rotation = Day (Short word, short time). Revolution = Year (Long word, long time).
2. Continents & Oceans
[Image: World Map]

Continents (7): Asia (Largest), Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia (Smallest).

Oceans (5): Pacific (Deepest/Largest), Atlantic, Indian (Named after a country), Southern, Arctic (Smallest).

India Context: India is in the continent of Asia and has the Indian Ocean below it.
3. Seas, Gulfs & Straits
[Image: Palk Strait Map]

Sea: Smaller than ocean (e.g., Arabian Sea).
Gulf: Sea partly enclosed by land (e.g., Gulf of Kutch).
Strait: Narrow water channel connecting two large water bodies.

India Context: Palk Strait separates India and Sri Lanka.
4. Major Landforms
[Image: Mountains vs Plateaus]

Mountains: High peaks (Himalayas).
Plateaus: Flat table-lands (Deccan Plateau).
Plains: Flat fertile land (Indo-Gangetic Plain).
Deserts: Dry sandy land (Thar Desert).

5. Volcanoes & Earthquakes
[Image: Volcano Structure]

Volcano: Opening in crust where Magma (underground) comes out as Lava.
Earthquake: Shaking of ground due to movement of tectonic plates. Measured by Richter Scale.

India Context: Barren Island (Andaman) is India's only active volcano.
6. Climate Zones
[Image: Earth Climate Zones]

Torrid Zone: Hot (Near Equator).
Temperate Zone: Moderate (Where most people live).
Frigid Zone: Very Cold (Near Poles).

7. Natural Disasters
[Image: Safety Drill]

Disasters like Floods, Cyclones, Tsunamis, and Droughts occur naturally.

💡 Safety Tip: During an earthquake -> Drop, Cover, Hold.
8. Environment Protection
[Image: 3 Rs symbol]

Sustainability means using resources without destroying the future.

The 3 Rs: Reduce (waste), Reuse (items), Recycle (plastic/paper).

India Context: Swachh Bharat Abhiyan helps keep our environment clean.
