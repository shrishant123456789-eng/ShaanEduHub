Chapter 3: Subtraction

1️⃣ What is Subtraction? (घटाना)

Subtraction means taking away something. It helps us find out how many are left or the difference between two numbers.

The sign for subtraction is Minus (-).

🍎🍎🍎🍎🍎 (5 Apples)
➖ 🍎🍎 (Take away 2)
= 🍎🍎🍎 (3 Left)
5 - 2 = 3

Real Life: If you have 10 chocolates and give 4 to your friend, how many do you have?
Answer: 10 - 4 = 6 chocolates! 🍫

2️⃣ Subtraction up to 4-Digit Numbers

We subtract numbers by arranging them in columns: Ones (O), Tens (T), Hundreds (H), Thousands (Th).

⚠️ Always start subtracting from the Ones place (Right side).

  Th  H  T  O
   4  5  6  8
-  2  1  4  3
-------------
   2  4  2  5

👉 8 - 3 = 5 (Ones)
👉 6 - 4 = 2 (Tens)
👉 5 - 1 = 4 (Hundreds)
👉 4 - 2 = 2 (Thousands)

3️⃣ Subtraction with Borrowing (हासिल वाला घटाना)

When the top number is smaller than the bottom number, we knock on the neighbor's door and borrow 1.

Example: 52 - 38
1. Can we do 2 - 8? NO! ❌
2. Borrow 1 Ten from 5. Now 5 becomes 4.
3. The 2 becomes 12. ✅
4. Now, 12 - 8 = 4.
5. Tens place: 4 - 3 = 1.
Answer: 14

4️⃣ Horizontal & Vertical Subtraction

Horizontal (Sleeping Line): 50 - 20 = 30

Vertical (Standing Line):

50
- 20
----
30

5️⃣ Word Problems 📝

Look for magic words in the question:

  • Left (बचे हुए)
  • Remaining (शेष)
  • Difference (अंतर)
  • How many more? (कितने अधिक)

Example: A shop had 500 toys. Sold 200. How many left?
Answer: 500 - 200 = 300 Toys.

6️⃣ Mental Subtraction 🧠

Tricks to subtract fast in your head!

Trick: Subtracting from 100, 1000 etc.
100 - 36 = ?
Think: 36 + 4 = 40.
40 + 60 = 100.
Answer = 60 + 4 = 64!

7️⃣ Checking Subtraction ✔️

We can check if our answer is correct using Addition.

Difference + Smaller Number = Bigger Number

Subtraction: 10 - 4 = 6
Check: 6 + 4 = 10 ✅ (Correct!)
