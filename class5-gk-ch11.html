📚 Study Notes

1. Money & Currency

Definition: Money is what we use to buy things. In India, our currency is called the Indian Rupee (₹).

Basic: We use coins (₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10, ₹20) and notes (₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500).

Advanced: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) prints our notes. The Governor of RBI signs them.

Imagine a ₹500 note. Can you see Gandhi Ji? Can you see the RBI seal?
💡 Tip: Every note has a "Security Thread" (a shiny strip) to prevent fake money.
❓ Quick Check: Whose photo is on Indian notes? (Ans: Mahatma Gandhi)
2. Banking Awareness

Definition: A bank is a safe place to keep our money.

Basic: We Deposit (put in) money to save it. We Withdraw (take out) money when we need it.

Advanced: An ATM (Automated Teller Machine) lets us take out cash without going into the bank. You need a Card and a Secret PIN.

Imagine a Bank Passbook. It records all your money in and out.
💡 Tip: Never share your ATM PIN with anyone, not even friends!
3. Digital Payments

Definition: Paying money without using cash (paper notes).

Basic: Using a phone to pay at a shop by scanning a code.

Advanced: UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's fast payment system. QR Code (Quick Response) is the square box pattern we scan.

⬜⬛⬜ Imagine scanning a QR Code with a phone camera. Beep! Paid!
❓ Quick Check: What do you scan to pay? (Ans: QR Code)
4. Savings & Budgeting

Definition: Saving means keeping money for the future.

Basic: Using a Piggy Bank (Gullak) to save coins.

Advanced: Budgeting is listing your 'Needs' (Food, School) vs 'Wants' (Toys, Games). Always spend on Needs first.

🐖 Piggy Bank = Small Savings. Bank Account = Big Savings.
💡 Tip: "A penny saved is a penny earned."
5. Trade & Transport

Trade: Buying and selling goods. Import: Buying from other countries. Export: Selling to other countries.

Transport: Moving goods. Road (Trucks), Rail (Goods Train), Water (Ships - Cheapest for heavy loads), Air (Cargo Planes - Fastest).

🚚 Trucks carry food. 🚢 Ships carry oil and cars across oceans.
6. Communication

Definition: Sending and receiving messages.

Types: Personal: Phone call, Letter, Email. Mass: TV, Radio, Newspaper (for many people at once).

Advanced: Internet allows instant messages via WhatsApp or Email.

📧 Email = Electronic Mail. Sent via internet instantly.
7. Postal & Emergency

PIN Code: Postal Index Number (6 digits). Helps postman find your area.

Emergency Numbers:

  • 112: All Emergency (Police, Fire, Ambulance)
  • 100: Police
  • 101: Fire
  • 108: Ambulance
💡 Tip: Just remember 112. It works for everything!
8. Consumer Awareness

Consumer: A person who buys things.

MRP: Maximum Retail Price. A shopkeeper cannot charge more than this.

Expiry Date: The date after which food/medicine is unsafe.

Rights: Always ask for a Bill (Receipt) when you buy expensive items.

🧾 Always check MRP and Expiry Date on a packet of chips!
