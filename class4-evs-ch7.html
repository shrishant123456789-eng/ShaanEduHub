📖 Chapter Notes

1. Sources of Water

Water comes from many places. These places are called sources of water.

A. Natural Sources (God-made)

  • Rain (Main source) 🌧️
  • Rivers, Lakes, Ponds, Oceans
  • Glaciers (Melting ice from mountains)

B. Man-made Sources (Human-made)

  • Wells and Tube wells
  • Dams and Canals
  • Handpumps
[Image: Drawing of Rain, River, and a Handpump]
💡 Tip: Rain is the father of all water sources!

2. Uses of Water

We cannot live without water. We use it for:

  • 🚰 Drinking (To stay alive)
  • 🍲 Cooking food
  • 🛁 Bathing and Cleaning
  • 🌾 Farming (Growing crops)
  • 🏭 Industries (Factories need water to make things)
  • 🔥 Putting out fires
[Image: Kids drinking water and a farmer watering plants]

3. The Water Cycle

Water never disappears; it just changes form and moves in a circle. This is the Water Cycle.

  1. Evaporation: Sun heats water in rivers/lakes. It turns into gas (vapour) and goes up. ☀️
  2. Condensation: Water vapour cools down high in the sky and forms clouds. ☁️
  3. Precipitation: When clouds get heavy, water falls down as Rain. 🌧️
  4. Collection: Rainwater fills rivers and oceans again.
[Image: Diagram of Water Cycle: Sun -> Vapour -> Clouds -> Rain]

4. Three States of Water

Water can be found in three forms:

  • Solid: Ice 🧊 (Frozen water)
  • Liquid: Water 💧 (What we drink)
  • Gas: Water Vapour ♨️ (Steam from boiling water)
💡 Try this: Put water in freezer -> Ice. Boil water -> Steam.

5. Water Pollution

Meaning: When water gets dirty and unsafe to use.

Causes:

  • Throwing garbage in rivers.
  • Factory chemicals going into lakes.
  • Washing clothes or bathing animals in rivers.

Effects: Causes diseases like Cholera, Typhoid, and Jaundice. Kills fish.

[Image: Dirty river with plastic bottles floating]

6. Conservation & Rainwater Harvesting

Conservation means saving water.

  • Close the tap while brushing. 🪥
  • Fix leaking pipes immediately. 🔧
  • Use a bucket to bathe, not a shower. 🚿❌

Rainwater Harvesting: Collecting rainwater on roofs and storing it in tanks underground for later use.

[Image: House with pipe collecting rain into a tank]

8. Role of Water in Life

  • Plants: Need water to make food (Photosynthesis) and grow.
  • Animals: Need water to drink and cool their bodies.
  • Humans: 70% of our body is water! Digestion needs water.
