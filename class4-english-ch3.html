Class Notes

1. What is a verb?

A verb is a word that tells us what a person, animal, or thing is doing. It is an "Action Word".

Without a verb, a sentence cannot be complete! It is the heart of a sentence.

Image: A boy running or jumping

Example: Rahul plays football every evening.

💡 Tip: If you can "do" it, it's a verb!
Check: Is 'Apple' a verb? No, you can't "apple", but you can "eat" an apple!
2. Action Verbs vs Linking Verbs

Action Verbs: Show physical or mental activity. (Jump, Think, Sing).

Linking Verbs: Connect the subject to a word that describes it. They don't show action.

Image: Comparing "Running" (Action) with "Is Happy" (Linking)

Example: Simran dances (Action). Simran is happy (Linking).

💡 Tip: Linking verbs are like a bridge (= sign).
Check: In "The sky is blue", which word is the linking verb? (Answer: is)
3. Helping Verbs (is, am, are, was, were, be, been)

Helping verbs come before the main verb to help show the time of the action.

Present: is, am, are | Past: was, were | Future: will be.

Image: A small robot (helper) holding a big box (main verb)

Example: I am eating breakfast. They were playing.

💡 Tip: Helping verbs always have a partner (the main verb).
Check: What is the helping verb in "We are going home"? (Answer: are)
4. Simple Present Tense

We use this for habits, daily routines, or facts that are always true.

Rule: Add 's' or 'es' for He/She/It. Don't add for I/You/We/They.

Image: A sun rising or a boy brushing teeth

Example: The sun rises in the east. I go to school daily.

💡 Tip: Use this for things you do "every day".
Check: Correct this: "He play cricket." (Answer: He plays cricket)
5. Simple Past Tense

This shows actions that happened and finished in the past.

Rule: Usually add '-ed' to the verb (walked, played). Some are special (went, ate).

Image: A calendar with "Yesterday" marked

Example: I watched a movie yesterday. She went to Delhi.

💡 Tip: Look for words like "yesterday", "last week", or "ago".
Check: What is the past tense of 'Drink'? (Answer: Drank)
6. Simple Future Tense

This shows actions that will happen later (tomorrow or in the future).

Rule: Use "will" before the action verb.

Image: A crystal ball or a future date

Example: We will travel to Mumbai next month.

💡 Tip: Future = "will" + verb.
Check: Make a future sentence with "Eat". (Example: I will eat pizza)
7. Present Continuous Tense

This shows actions that are happening RIGHT NOW.

Rule: is/am/are + verb + "ing".

Image: A clock saying "Now" with someone reading

Example: She is reading a book now. You are listening to me.

💡 Tip: ING means "In Progress".
Check: Use "ing" for "The cat (sleep)". (Answer: The cat is sleeping)
8. Past Continuous Tense

This shows actions that were going on at some time in the past.

Rule: was/were + verb + "ing".

Image: Someone sleeping at 10 PM yesterday

Example: I was doing my homework when it rained.

💡 Tip: Was (one person) / Were (many people).
Check: "They ___ playing" (was/were). (Answer: were)
9. Future with "will" and "going to"

Will: Quick decisions or promises. | Going to: Plans already made.

Image: Thinking cloud vs. a packed suitcase

Example: I will help you. I am going to visit my grandma today.

💡 Tip: "Going to" is for a fixed plan.
Check: Which one is a plan? "I will eat" or "I am going to eat"?
10. Modals (can, could, shall, should)

Can: Ability (I can do it). | Could: Past ability or polite request.

Shall/Should: Advice or suggestion (You should study).

Image: A strong arm (can) and a teacher (should)

Example: You should eat vegetables. Can I take your pen?

💡 Tip: Should = Good Advice. Can = Power/Permission.
Check: Use a modal for "It's cold. You ___ wear a jacket." (Answer: should)
11. Subject–Verb Agreement

The verb must match the subject. One person = Singular verb. Many people = Plural verb.

Image: One bird flies vs. Two birds fly

Example: The boy runs. The boys run.

💡 Tip: If the subject has no 's', the verb usually needs one!
Check: "The dog ___ (bark/barks)". (Answer: barks)
12. Verb Phrases

A group of words that act as a verb (Helping verb + Main verb).

Image: A train engine (helping) pulling a carriage (main)

Example: The kids have been playing for hours.

💡 Tip: It's just a "team" of verbs.
Check: Find the phrase in "She is writing". (Answer: is writing)
13. Verb Corrections

Always check the time and the subject before choosing a verb.

Image: A magnifying glass over a sentence

Example: Incorrect: "He go school." Correct: "He goes to school."

💡 Tip: Read the sentence aloud; if it sounds funny, check the verb!
Check: Fix this: "I seen a tiger." (Answer: I saw a tiger)

🚀 Quick Revision Sheet

  • ✅ Verb: Action word (Heart of the sentence).
  • ✅ Is/Am/Are: Present helpers.
  • ✅ Was/Were: Past helpers.
  • ✅ Simple Present: Habit/Daily (Add -s for He/She/It).
  • ✅ Simple Past: Finished action (Usually ends in -ed).
  • ✅ Simple Future: "Will" + Verb.
  • ✅ Continuous: -ing form (Happening now or then).
  • ✅ Can: Ability | Should: Advice.
  • ✅ Agreement: One person → Verb+s | Many → No 's' on verb.
