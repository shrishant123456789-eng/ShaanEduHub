🏛️ Countries & Capitals

Just like India has New Delhi, every country has a Capital City!

USA (United States of America): Washington D.C.

UK (United Kingdom): London

Japan: Tokyo

China: Beijing

France: Paris

💡 Tip: Beijing sounds like 'Ring a Bell' -> China is huge!

💵 Flags & Money

Every country has its own flag and money (currency).

USA: Currency is Dollar ($). Flag has Stars and Stripes.

UK: Currency is Pound (£). Flag is called the Union Jack.

Japan: Currency is Yen (¥). Flag has a red sun on white.

Europe: Many countries use the Euro (€).

❓ Check: Which country uses the Dollar? (USA)

🗽 Famous Landmarks

These are famous places people travel far to see!

Statue of Liberty: A big green lady holding a torch. Located in New York, USA.

Eiffel Tower: A tall iron tower in Paris, France.

Great Wall of China: A very long wall in China used for protection.

Pyramids: Triangle-shaped tombs in Egypt.

💡 Tip: Eiffel Tower looks like the letter 'A' for Awesome Paris!

[Image: Collage of Eiffel Tower, Statue of Liberty, Great Wall]
🏷️ Nicknames of Places

Some places have special nicknames (Sobriquets) because of their special features.

Japan: Land of the Rising Sun.

Egypt: Gift of the Nile.

Thailand: Land of White Elephants.

Jaipur (India): Pink City.

Australia: The Land Down Under.

❓ Check: Which country is the Land of Rising Sun? (Japan)
