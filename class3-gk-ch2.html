🌱 Parts of a Plant & Functions

Just like we have body parts, plants have parts too!

1. Roots: They grow under the ground. They hold the plant tightly and drink water from the soil.

2. Stem: It grows above the ground. It carries water and food to all parts. It supports the plant.

💡 Tip: The Stem is like an elevator!

3. Leaves: They are green and flat. They make food for the plant using sunlight.

💡 Tip: The Leaf is the Kitchen of the plant.

🌳 Types of Plants

Plants come in different shapes and sizes:

1. Herbs: Very small plants with soft, green stems.

Example: Mint, Coriander, Spinach.

2. Shrubs: Medium plants with hard, woody stems. They look bushy.

Example: Rose, Hibiscus, Tulsi.

3. Trees: Big, tall plants with a thick, hard trunk.

Example: Mango Tree, Banyan Tree, Neem Tree.

4. Climbers: Weak stems. They need a stick or wall to climb up.

Example: Money Plant, Grapevine.

5. Creepers: Weak stems. They grow along the ground because their fruit is heavy.

Example: Watermelon, Pumpkin.

☀️ Photosynthesis (Making Food)

How do plants eat? They cook their own food!

Photosynthesis is the process by which leaves make food.

To make food, a plant needs 3 things:

  • ☀️ Sunlight
  • 💧 Water
  • 🌬️ Air (Carbon Dioxide)

💡 Tip: Photo means Light. Synthesis means Mixing.

[Image: Sun shining on a leaf + Water from roots]
✨ Special Plants

Some plants are very special and useful.

Medicinal Plants: Used to cure sickness.

Tulsi (Cough), Neem (Skin), Aloe Vera (Burns).

Insectivorous Plants: Plants that eat insects!

Pitcher Plant, Venus Flytrap.

Important Questions 🌟

Q1. Which part of the plant is called the 'Kitchen'?

Ans: Leaf

Because leaves make food for the plant using sunlight.

Q2. Name a plant that grows along the ground.

Ans: Watermelon / Pumpkin

These are called Creepers because they have heavy fruits.

Q3. What do roots do?

Ans: Absorb water

Roots hold the plant firmly and drink water from soil.

Q4. Give an example of a Medicinal Plant.

Ans: Tulsi / Neem

Tulsi is used to cure coughs and colds.

Q5. What does a plant need to make food?

Ans: Sunlight, Air, and Water

This process is called Photosynthesis.
