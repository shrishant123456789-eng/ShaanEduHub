📝 My Study Notes

1. Composition of Air

Definition Air is a mixture of many gases. It is all around us but we cannot see it.

The main gases in air are:

  • Nitrogen (78%): Most abundant gas.
  • Oxygen (21%): Needed for breathing and burning.
  • Other Gases (1%): Carbon dioxide, water vapor, argon, dust particles.
🖼️ [attachment_0](attachment)
💡 Tip: Remember "NO"! N for Nitrogen (Biggest), O for Oxygen (Second).

Daily Life: When you blow a balloon, you are filling it with this mixture of gases!

2. Importance of Air for Life

Why we need it?

  1. Breathing: Humans and animals need Oxygen. Plants take in Carbon Dioxide.
  2. Burning: Fire cannot burn without Oxygen.
  3. Tires & Balls: Compressed air is used in footballs, cycle tires, and car tires.
  4. Seed Dispersal: Wind carries seeds to new places to grow.
🖼️
💡 Tip: No Air = No Fire + No Life.
3. Air Pollution – Sources & Control

Problem When harmful smoke, dust, and chemicals mix with air, it becomes polluted.

Sources: Smoke from cars, factories, burning garbage, and Diwali crackers.

Control:

  • Plant more trees (They act as filters).
  • Use electric vehicles or bicycles.
  • Do not burn plastic or leaves.
🖼️

Daily Life: In cities like Delhi, pollution causes coughing and burning eyes in winter.

4. Weather Elements

Definition Weather is the condition of air at a specific place and time. It changes every day.

Main Elements:

  • Temperature: How hot or cold it is (Measured by Thermometer).
  • Wind: Moving air. Gentle wind is a breeze; strong wind is a storm.
  • Rainfall: Water falling from clouds.
  • Clouds: Determine if it will be sunny or gloomy.
🖼️ [Icons of Sun, Cloud, Rain, Wind]
💡 Tip: Weather changes like your mood! (Happy/Sunny, Sad/Rainy).
5. Climate vs. Weather

Many students get confused! Here is the difference:

Weather Climate
Short time (Hours/Days) Long time (Many Years)
Changes quickly Stays largely same
Example: "It is raining today." Example: "Kashmir has a cold climate."

6. Effect of Weather on Life

Impact Weather decides what we eat, wear, and do.

  • Summer: We wear cotton clothes, eat watermelon, and use fans.
  • Winter: We wear woolens, drink hot soup, and sit in the sun.
  • Monsoon: We use umbrellas and farmers sow seeds for crops (Rice).
🖼️
7. Natural Disasters

Warning Sometimes nature becomes violent.

  • Flood: Too much rain causes water to overflow into houses.
  • Drought: No rain for a long time; land cracks and crops die.
  • Storm/Cyclone: Very strong winds that uproot trees.
🖼️
💡 Tip: Disasters are emergencies. We must stay calm and help others.

⭐ Important Questions (Study List)

Q1. Which gas is most abundant in air?
Ans: Nitrogen (78%).
Q2. Which gas supports burning?
Ans: Oxygen.
Q3. What is moving air called?
Ans: Wind.
Q4. Name the instrument to measure temperature.
Ans: Thermometer.
Q5. What is the blanket of air around Earth called?
Ans: Atmosphere.
Q6. Why do we feel sticky in the rainy season?
Ans: Due to high humidity (moisture in air).
Q7. What gas do plants use to make food?
Ans: Carbon Dioxide.
Q8. What is a gentle wind called?
Ans: Breeze.
Q9. Which season is best for drying clothes?
Ans: Summer (due to heat and sun).
Q10. What causes air pollution in cities?
Ans: Vehicle smoke and factory fumes.
Q11. Define Evaporation.
Ans: Change of water into water vapor on heating.
Q12. Define Condensation.
Ans: Change of water vapor into water on cooling.
Q13. Why do we wear wool in winter?
Ans: To keep our body warm and trap heat.
Q14. What happens during a drought?
Ans: Rivers dry up, crops die, and there is shortage of water.
Q15. Is weather the same everywhere?
Ans: No, it varies from place to place.
Q16. What is the role of the Ozone layer?
Ans: It protects us from harmful sun rays.
Q17. Which gas is used in fire extinguishers?
Ans: Carbon Dioxide.
Q18. What is a storm?
Ans: Very strong wind often with rain and thunder.
Q19. How do trees help the air?
Ans: They release Oxygen and clean the air.
Q20. What is humidity?
Ans: The amount of water vapor present in the air.
Q21. Why shouldn't we sleep under trees at night?
Ans: Because plants release Carbon Dioxide at night.
Q22. Name a disease caused by air pollution.
Ans: Asthma or Lung infection.
Q23. What is the water cycle?
Ans: Continuous movement of water between Earth and Sky.
Q24. Does air have weight?
Ans: Yes, air has mass and occupies space.
Q25. Which is heavier: Hot air or Cold air?
Ans: Cold air is heavier; Hot air rises up.
Q26. What causes land breeze?
Ans: Land cooling down faster than the sea at night.
Q27. What causes sea breeze?
Ans: Land heating up faster than the sea during the day.
Q28. Name a natural disaster involving wind.
Ans: Cyclone.
Q29. What is climate?
Ans: The average weather pattern over a long time.
Q30. Why is the sun important for weather?
Ans: The sun's heat causes winds and evaporation (rain).

🔄 Quick Revision

  • Air: Mixture of N (78%), O (21%), CO2 (0.03%).
  • Properties: Air has weight, occupies space, exerts pressure.
  • Pollution: Harmful for lungs; control by planting trees.
  • Weather: Day-to-day condition (Sun, Rain, Wind).
  • Climate: Long-term pattern (e.g., Desert is hot).
  • Disasters: Flood (Too much water), Drought (No water).
  • Seasons: Summer, Winter, Monsoon affect our lifestyle.
