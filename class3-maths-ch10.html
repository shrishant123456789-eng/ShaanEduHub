1. What is Data? 📝

Data is information that we collect.

Imagine you want to know the favorite fruit of your friends. You ask them and write it down. That list is Data!

🍎 Rahul likes Apple
🍌 Simi likes Banana
🍇 Raj likes Grapes

Data helps us know things quickly!

2. Collecting Data 🧺

We can collect data by counting things around us.

Let's count the toys in the box:

🚗 🧸 🚗 🧸 🤖 🚗

Count:
Cars 🚗: 3
Bears 🧸: 2
Robots 🤖: 1

👉 Try This: Count the number of fans in your home!

3. Organizing Data (Tables) 📋

After collecting data, we arrange it in a Table to make it clear.

Color Number of Kids
🔴 Red 5
🔵 Blue 8
🟢 Green 3

Now we can easily say: Blue is the most popular color!

5. Pictograph 🖼️

A Pictograph uses pictures or symbols to represent data.

Example: 😊 = 1 Student

Monday 😊 😊 😊 (3)
Tuesday 😊 😊 (2)

A pictograph makes data look fun and easy to read!

Sometimes 1 Picture = 2 or 5 items.
⭐ = 2 Stars. So ⭐⭐ = 4 Stars.

6. Bar Graph 📊

A Bar Graph uses rectangular bars to show numbers. The height of the bar tells us the value.

5Mon
8Tue
3Wed

Which bar is tallest? Tuesday (8).

7. Reading & Answering Questions 🧐

We look at the table or graph to find answers.

  • Total: Add all numbers.
  • Difference: Subtract smaller from bigger.
  • Most/Least: Look for highest/lowest value.
