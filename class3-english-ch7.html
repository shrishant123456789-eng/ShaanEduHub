Chapter Notes

1. What are Tenses?

Tenses tell us WHEN something happens. It is all about time.

Past: Before now.
Present: Happening now.
Future: Will happen later.
💡 Remember: Time flows like a river. Past -> Present -> Future.
[Image: Timeline showing Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow]
2. Present Tense (Now)

We use this for things happening today or every day.

I play football.
She eats an apple.
💡 For He/She/It, we add 's' or 'es' to the verb (Runs, Goes).
3. Past Tense (Before)

We use this for things that already happened.

I played football yesterday.
She ate an apple.
💡 Many past words end with 'ed' (Walked, Talked).
4. Future Tense (Later)

We use this for things that will happen later.

I will play football tomorrow.
She will eat an apple.
💡 Magic Word: "Will". Just add "Will" before the verb!
5. Time Words

Special words help us guess the tense.

Yesterday = Past (Was/Were)
Today / Now = Present (Is/Am/Are)
Tomorrow = Future (Will)

Quick Revision

  • Present: Happens now. (I walk)
  • Past: Already happened. (I walked)
  • Future: Will happen. (I will walk)
  • Yesterday is for Past Tense.
  • Tomorrow is for Future Tense.
  • Is/Am/Are = Present. Was/Were = Past.
