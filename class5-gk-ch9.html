📝 Study Notes

1. Indian Festivals & Traditions

Definition: Celebrations of cultural or religious events.

Explanation: India is called the "Land of Festivals". We have Harvest festivals (Pongal, Onam, Baisakhi), Religious festivals (Diwali, Eid, Christmas), and National festivals (Republic Day).

🏠 Example: Lighting diyas on Diwali (Festival of Lights).

💡 Memory Tip: Harvest = Food = Happy Farmers 🌾

Check: Which festival uses colours? (Holi)

2. World Festivals

Definition: Famous celebrations across the globe.

Explanation: Different countries have unique ways to celebrate. Example: Chinese New Year (Dragon dance), Halloween (Costumes), Thanksgiving (Gratitude).

🎃 Example: Carving pumpkins for Halloween in USA.
[Image: Chinese Dragon Dance]
3. Classical & Folk Dances

Definition: Traditional dance forms telling stories.

Explanation:
• Classical: Strict rules (Bharatanatyam - TN, Kathak - North India).
• Folk: People's dance (Bhangra - Punjab, Garba - Gujarat).

💃 Example: Garba during Navratri.
[Image: Dancer in Classical Attire]
4. Music Forms

Definition: Art of sound and rhythm.

Explanation: India has Hindustani (North) and Carnatic (South) classical music. Instruments: Sitar, Tabla, Flute. Western music has Jazz, Pop, Rock.

🎵 Example: Zakir Hussain plays the Tabla.
[Image: Sitar and Tabla]
5. Famous Monuments

Definition: Old buildings with historical importance.

Explanation: Taj Mahal (India - Marble), Eiffel Tower (France - Iron), Pyramids (Egypt - Stone tombs), Statue of Liberty (USA).

🏛️ Example: Taj Mahal was built by Shah Jahan.
[Image: Taj Mahal Sketch]
6. UNESCO World Heritage

Definition: Places listed by UNESCO for protection.

Explanation: UNESCO stands for United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. It protects sites like Ajanta Caves, Sun Temple, Great Wall of China.

🌍 Example: Protecting nature (Kaziranga) and culture (Hampi).
[Image: UNESCO Logo / Old Temple]
7. Handicrafts & Symbols

Definition: Items made by hand using skill.

Explanation: Pashmina Shawls (Kashmir), Madhubani Painting (Bihar), Terracotta (West Bengal). National Symbols: Peacock (Bird), Lotus (Flower).

🎨 Example: Making clay pots (Pottery).
[Image: Madhubani Art Pattern]
8. Cultural Diversity

Definition: Variety of cultures living together.

Explanation: "Unity in Diversity". People speak different languages, eat different food, wear different clothes, but are one nation.

🤝 Example: Celebrating Eid and Diwali together with friends.
