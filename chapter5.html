Step 1: The Script Lab (Basic)

Foundations of Retention

BASIC

The Golden Rule: A script is not just text; it is a blueprint for visual retention. In 2026, if you don't hook in 3 seconds, you lose.

Structure (H-B-C Model):

  • Hook (0-5s): Visual + Audio interruption.
  • Body (Value): The meat of the content. High density.
  • CTA (Call to Action): Specific instruction (Save, Share, Buy).
🇮🇳 Indian Context Example

Concept: "Finance Reel"

❌ Bad: "Hello friends, today I will talk about SIP." (Scroll away)

✅ Good: "Stop keeping money in Savings Account! Here is how ₹5000 becomes ₹1 Crore." (Visual of money growing + Fast text).

🛠️ Tool: Notion Scripting Template
Use a 2-column layout in Notion. Left side: Visual/B-Roll. Right side: Audio/Dialogue. This forces you to think visually.

Step 2: Narrative Engineering (Medium)

Frameworks & Storyboarding

MEDIUM

Adapted for 60-second content:

  1. The Status Quo: Showing the problem (e.g., Struggling to edit video).
  2. The Inciting Incident: Finding a new AI tool.
  3. The Struggle: Learning the tool (Montage).
  4. The Transformation: Final professional result.
  5. The Return: Telling audience "Link in bio".

Attention: Stop the scroll.
Interest: State a fact/stat.
Desire: Show the benefit/lifestyle.
Action: Tell them what to do.

🛠️ Tool: Final Draft / Google Docs
For long-form YouTube (10m+), use color-coding. Yellow = B-Roll, Green = Talking Head.

Step 3: Growth Systems (Advanced)

Algorithms & Distribution

ADVANCED

The Content Flywheel: Don't create once. Repurpose.

Example: 1 YouTube Video (Long) → Cut into 5 Shorts → Transcribe for 1 LinkedIn Post → Screenshot for 1 Tweet → Audio for Podcast.

📊 Industry Insight

Engagement Loops: Algorithms reward "Session Time". If your comment section has a debate, your video goes viral.
Strategy: Pin a controversial question in comments.

🛠️ Tool: Planoly / Metricool
Schedule posts for "Best Time". Analyze "Retention Rate" graphs. If retention drops at 0:15, your script was boring there. Cut it next time.

Step 4: The Business of Creation (Expert)

Monetization & Legal

EXPERT

The Monetization Ladder:

  1. Level 1: Platform Revenue (AdSense) - Unstable.
  2. Level 2: Affiliates/Sponsorships - Better.
  3. Level 3: Digital Products (E-books, Courses) - High Margin.
  4. Level 4: SaaS/Community (Recurring Membership) - Wealth.
⚖️ Legal Check (India Context)
Copyright Act 1957: "Fair Dealing" allows use for criticism/review.
Rule: Never use full songs without license. Use royalty-free (Epidemic Sound/Artlist).

📘 Important Questions (Revision)

  • Q1: What is the ideal hook length for a Reel?
    Ans: 0 to 3 seconds max. Visual change required every 2s.
  • Q2: Define 'Watch Time' vs 'Retention'.
    Ans: Watch time is total minutes viewed. Retention is the % of the video watched. Retention triggers virality.
  • Q3: What is a CTA?
    Ans: Call to Action. Direct instruction to the audience (e.g., "Check the link").
  • Q4: Explain the 1000 True Fans theory.
    Ans: You only need 1000 people willing to buy everything you make to earn a living, not millions of followers.
  • Q5: What is B-Roll?
    Ans: Supplemental footage inserted as a cutaway to help tell the story or mask cuts.
