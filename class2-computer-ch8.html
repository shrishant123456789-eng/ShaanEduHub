🤖 What is an Operating System?

The Operating System (OS) is the BOSS of the computer!
It helps the computer start and work.
It tells all the parts (like mouse and keyboard) what to do.
"When Rohan presses the power button, the OS wakes up the computer!" 😊

👮‍♂️
🪟 Examples of OS

Just like you have a name, Operating Systems have names too!
1. Windows 🪟 (Seen on most computers)
2. Android 🤖 (Seen on mobile phones)
These are the most famous ones.

🖥️ The Desktop

When the computer starts, the first screen you see is called the Desktop.
It is like a study table where we keep our things.
It has a pretty picture in the background!

🖼️ Icons

The small pictures on the Desktop are called Icons.
Each icon stands for a program or file.
Examples:
🎨 Paint, 🎮 Games, ➕ Calculator.
"Click an icon to open it!"

🔘 Start Button & Taskbar

Start Button: It is like a door 🚪. It helps us begin our work and open programs.

Taskbar: It is the long bar at the bottom of the screen ➖. It shows which programs are open.

🖱️ Open & Close

To Open: Move mouse pointer to an icon and click (or double click).
To Close: Click the X button at the top corner.
"Open Paint -> Draw -> Close Paint"
