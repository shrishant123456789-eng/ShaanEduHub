📚 Chapter Notes

1. Personal Hygiene
Keeping our own body clean is called Personal Hygiene.
Germs love dirt. If we are dirty, germs attack us. We must brush teeth twice a day, take a bath daily, and trim nails.
🇮🇳 Example: Raju washes his hands with soap before eating Roti and Sabzi.
[Image: Boy brushing teeth and washing hands]
💡 Tip: Early to bed, early to rise! Brush in morning & night.
❓ Quick Check: How many times should we brush? (Twice)
2. Clean Habits
Good actions we do daily to stay clean.
Always cover your mouth when sneezing. Use a handkerchief. Do not bite your nails. Wear clean clothes.
🇮🇳 Example: Meena always uses a hanky when she sneezes "Achhoo!".
[Image: Girl using handkerchief while sneezing]
💡 Tip: Dirty nails = Tummy ache!
❓ Quick Check: What do we use for a runny nose? (Handkerchief)
3. Clean Surroundings
Keeping the area around us neat and tidy.
We must not throw garbage on the road. Use a dustbin. Do not let water collect in tyres or pots.
🇮🇳 Example: The Swachh Bharat Abhiyan teaches us to keep India clean.
[Image: Child throwing wrapper in dustbin]
💡 Tip: Dry waste (Blue bin), Wet waste (Green bin).
❓ Quick Check: Where do we throw chocolate wrappers? (Dustbin)
4. Diseases Caused by Dirt
Sickness that comes from germs in dirty places.
Dirt brings flies and mosquitoes. Flies sit on poop and then on our food. This causes diseases like Cholera and Typhoid.
🇮🇳 Example: Eating uncovered street food can make your tummy hurt.
[Image: Flies sitting on uncovered sweets]
💡 Tip: No dirt = No Flies = No Disease.
❓ Quick Check: Who carries germs from dirt to food? (Housefly)
5. Prevention of Diseases
Stopping diseases before they happen.
Drink boiled or filtered water. Eat fresh food. Keep food covered. Vaccinations help us fight germs.
🇮🇳 Example: Mother boils milk to kill germs before drinking.
[Image: Covered food and boiled water glass]
💡 Tip: Prevention is better than Cure!
❓ Quick Check: What kills germs in water? (Boiling)
6. Safety at Home
Staying safe inside the house.
Do not play with fire or matchsticks. Do not touch electric switches with wet hands. Do not run on wet floors.
🇮🇳 Example: Rahul does not touch the hot iron box.
[Image: Do not touch electric socket]
💡 Tip: Wet hands + Switch = Shock! (Danger)
❓ Quick Check: Should we play with sharp knives? (No)
7. Safety at School
Rules to follow in the classroom and playground.
Do not jump on desks. Do not push friends on the stairs. Wait for your turn on the swing.
🇮🇳 Example: The class walks in a line quietly to the library.
[Image: Kids walking in a line]
💡 Tip: No pushing, No rushing!
❓ Quick Check: Is it good to run on stairs? (No)
8. Road Safety Rules
Rules to stay safe on the road.
Always walk on the footpath. Cross at the Zebra Crossing. Look Right, then Left, then Right again before crossing.
🇮🇳 Example: Red light says Stop. Green light says Go.
[Image: Zebra Crossing and Traffic Light]
💡 Tip: Zebra Crossing is for walking people (Pedestrians).
❓ Quick Check: Which light means STOP? (Red)
9. First Aid
The immediate help given to an injured person.
If someone gets hurt, do not panic. Inform an adult. Wash the wound with water and apply antiseptic lotion.
🇮🇳 Example: Teacher puts a Band-Aid on Amit's knee when he fell.
[Image: First Aid Box]
💡 Tip: Keep a First Aid box ready at home.
❓ Quick Check: What do we apply on a small cut? (Antiseptic)
10. Emergency Situations
Dangerous times when we need urgent help.
If there is a fire, call 101. If there is a thief, call 100. If someone is very sick, call 102 (Ambulance).
🇮🇳 Example: We dial 100 for Police in India.
[Image: Police Car and Ambulance]
💡 Tip: Remember 100 (Police), 101 (Fire), 102 (Ambulance).
❓ Quick Check: Who do we call for fire? (Fire Brigade)
