🐾 Safari Notes

1. Animals Around Us 🏠🌲

Welcome to the animal world! We see animals everywhere.

  • Pet Animals: We keep them at home for fun and love.
    Ex: Dog 🐕, Cat 🐈, Rabbit 🐇.
  • Farm Animals: They are useful to us. They give us milk, wool, or carry loads.
    Ex: Cow 🐄 (Milk), Sheep 🐑 (Wool), Horse 🐎 (Riding).
  • Wild Animals: They live in the jungle. They find their own food.
    Ex: Lion 🦁, Tiger 🐯, Elephant 🐘, Bear 🐻.

2. Where do they live? (Homes) 🏡

Just like we have houses, animals have special homes too!

  • Land Animals: Live on ground. (Dog, Camel 🐪, Zebra).
  • Water Animals: Live in rivers and seas. They have fins to swim. (Fish 🐟, Octopus 🐙, Whale).
  • Birds: They have wings and feathers to fly. They live in Nests. (Parrot 🦜, Sparrow, Eagle 🦅).

Specific Homes:
🦁 Lion lives in a Den.
🐄 Cow lives in a Shed.
🐕 Dog lives in a Kennel.
🐎 Horse lives in a Stable.

3. Animal Families 👨‍👩‍👦

Animals have cute babies! Let's learn their names:

  • Dog ➡ Puppy 🐶
  • Cat ➡ Kitten 🐱
  • Cow ➡ Calf 🐮
  • Lion ➡ Cub 🦁
  • Sheep ➡ Lamb 🐑
  • Hen ➡ Chick 🐥

4. Sounds & Food 🔊🍖

Animal Sounds:
Dog says "Woof Woof" (Barks).
Cat says "Meow".
Lion "Roars".
Cow "Moos".

Food Habits:
🌿 Plant Eaters: Eat grass and leaves (Cow, Goat, Elephant).
🍖 Flesh Eaters: Eat other animals (Lion, Tiger, Wolf).
🌰 Grain Eaters: Birds eat grains and nuts (Hen, Squirrel).

5. Special Features ✨

Some animals have magic parts!

  • Elephant: Has a long Trunk.
  • Camel: Has a Hump to store fat.
  • Fish: Have Gills to breathe underwater.
  • Birds: Have a Beak to eat (no teeth!).
  • Zebra: Has black and white Stripes.
