Class Notes 📏
1. Measurement Basics

Measurement is finding a number that shows the size or amount of something. We use Standard Units so everyone gets the same result.

💡 TIP: Standard units prevent confusion!
Check: Is a 'handspan' a standard unit? (No)
2. Length (How long?)

We measure length in Metric Units:
• Millimetre (mm): Very small (thickness of a coin).
• Centimetre (cm): Small (width of a finger).
• Metre (m): Medium (length of a saree).
• Kilometre (km): Long distance (Home to School).

💡 TIP: 10 mm = 1 cm | 100 cm = 1 m | 1000 m = 1 km
3. Mass (How heavy?)

Mass is the weight of an object.
• Gram (g): Light things (Mustard seeds, gold).
• Kilogram (kg): Heavy things (Rice bag, your weight).

💡 TIP: Kilo means 1000. So 1 kg = 1000 g.
4. Capacity (How much liquid?)

Capacity is the amount of liquid a container holds.
• Millilitre (ml): Small drops (Medicine spoon).
• Litre (l): Large amount (Milk packet, bucket).

💡 TIP: 1 Litre = 1000 Millilitres.
5. Unit Conversions

Rule: Big to Small → Multiply (x)
Rule: Small to Big → Divide (÷)
Example: 5 m to cm → 5 x 100 = 500 cm.
Example: 3000 g to kg → 3000 ÷ 1000 = 3 kg.

Check: 2 km = ? m (2000 m)
6. Mixed Unit Problems

When adding or subtracting different units, always group them.
Example: 2m 40cm + 1m 50cm.
Add cm: 40+50=90. Add m: 2+1=3. Result: 3m 90cm.

💡 TIP: If cm > 100, carry over to m!
7. Time (12h vs 24h)

12-Hour Clock: Uses AM (Morning) and PM (Afternoon/Night).
24-Hour Clock: Used in Railways. Add 12 to PM time.
Ex: 1 PM = 13:00 Hours. 10 PM = 22:00 Hours.

💡 TIP: 00:00 is Midnight. 12:00 is Noon.
8. Calendar Calculations

1 Year = 365 Days (366 in Leap Year).
Leap year happens every 4 years (divisible by 4).
Months with 31 days: Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Oct, Dec.

Check: Is 2024 a leap year? (Yes)
9. Speed

Speed tells how fast something moves.
Formula: Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Unit: km/h or m/s.
If a car travels 100km in 2 hours, Speed = 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h.

💡 TIP: S = D / T
10. Advanced Problems

Read the problem twice. Note down units. Convert to same unit before solving.
Example: Add 500m to 2km.
Step 1: Convert 2km to 2000m.
Step 2: 2000 + 500 = 2500m.
