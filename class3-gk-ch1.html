My Study Notes 📖

🍽️ What Do Animals Eat?

Animals are grouped by the food they eat:

1. Herbivores: Animals that eat ONLY plants (grass, leaves, fruits).

Examples: Cow, Goat, Elephant, Giraffe.

2. Carnivores: Animals that eat ONLY flesh of other animals.

Examples: Lion, Tiger, Crocodile, Eagle.

3. Omnivores: Animals that eat BOTH plants and animals.

Examples: Bear, Crow, Human, Dog.

💡 Tip: 'Herb' means plant. 'C'arnivore cuts meat!

[Image: A Cow eating grass vs Lion eating meat]

❓ Quick Check: Is a Tiger a Herbivore? (No!)

✨ Amazing Animal Features

Animals have special powers to survive!

Camouflage: Blending with surroundings to hide from enemies.

Example: Chameleon changes color.

Hibernation: A long, deep sleep during winter to save energy.

Example: Polar Bears, Frogs.

Migration: Moving from one place to another for food or weather.

Example: Siberian Crane flies thousands of kilometers.

[Image: Chameleon changing color on a leaf]
🏠 Animal Homes (Habitats)

Just like we have houses, animals have special names for their homes.

  • 🦁 Lion lives in a Den.
  • 🐰 Rabbit lives in a Burrow.
  • 🐮 Cow is kept in a Shed.
  • 🐴 Horse is kept in a Stable.
  • 🐕 Dog lives in a Kennel.

💡 Tip: Horses are 'Stable' and strong!

🌍 Saving Animals

Some animals are disappearing.

Extinct Animals: Animals that are gone forever. We cannot see them anymore.

Examples: Dinosaurs, Dodo Bird.

Endangered Animals: Animals that are very few in number. They might disappear if we don't protect them.

Examples: Tiger, Panda, Rhino.

[Image: A Dodo bird sketch]

❓ Quick Check: Are Dinosaurs alive today? (No, they are extinct!)

Important Questions 🌟

Q1. Which animal is known as the 'Ship of the Desert'?

Ans: Camel

Because it can walk easily on sand and live without water for days.

Q2. Name a flightless bird that is now extinct.

Ans: Dodo

The Dodo lived in Mauritius and could not fly.

Q3. Where does a Rabbit live?

Ans: Burrow

Rabbits dig holes in the ground called burrows to stay safe.

Q4. What is the winter sleep of animals called?

Ans: Hibernation

Animals like bears sleep all winter to save energy.

Q5. Which animal uses camouflage?
