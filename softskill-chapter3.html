3.1 BASIC: Foundations of Leadership

Leadership vs. Management

Definition: Management focuses on processes, systems, and "doing things right." Leadership focuses on people, vision, and "doing the right thing."

[Diagram: Manager (Head/Logic) vs Leader (Heart/Vision)]

Beginner: A manager counts value; a leader creates value.

Advanced: In flat organizations, the distinction blurs. A "Leader-Manager" is now the gold standard.

Indian Example: Ratan Tata (Visionary Leader) vs. a typical bank branch manager (Operational excellence).

Emotional Intelligence (Daniel Goleman)

Framework: 1. Self-Awareness 2. Self-Regulation 3. Motivation 4. Empathy 5. Social Skills.

Intermediate: IQ gets you hired; EQ gets you promoted. Empathy is the ability to understand and share the feelings of another.

Expert Insight: "Amygdala Hijack" prevents rational thinking. Leaders must pause (6 seconds) to re-engage the prefrontal cortex.

3.2 MEDIUM: Styles & Dynamics

Leadership Styles

  • Transformational: Inspires change (e.g., Steve Jobs).
  • Transactional: Rewards/Punishments (e.g., Sales targets).
  • Situational: Adapts to team maturity (Hersey-Blanchard).
  • Servant: Serves the team first (e.g., Mahatma Gandhi).
Case Study (Success): Satya Nadella shifting Microsoft from "Know-it-all" to "Learn-it-all" culture (Growth Mindset/Servant Leadership).

Team Dynamics & Psychological Safety

Concept: Google's Project Aristotle found "Psychological Safety" (feeling safe to take risks) is the #1 predictor of high-performing teams.

Motivation Theories: Maslow's Hierarchy (Survival to Self-Actualization) vs. Herzberg’s Two-Factor (Hygiene vs. Motivators).

3.3 ADVANCED: High-Stakes Psychology

Emotional Regulation in Crisis

Cognitive Reappraisal: Reinterpreting a negative event to reduce emotional impact. "This is not a disaster; it's a pivot point."

Conflict Resolution: Focus on interests, not positions. Separate the person from the problem.

Technique: The "Third Story" - Step out of your story and their story to tell a neutral observer's story.
3.4 EXPERT: Future of Leadership

Leadership in AI Era

Challenge: Managing hybrid teams of Humans + AI agents. Leaders must optimize for creativity and ethics, leaving data processing to AI.

Trend: Burnout Epidemic. "Emotional Fatigue" is the new silent killer. Leaders must act as "Energy Officers."

Tools: 360-degree feedback platforms and Sentiment Analysis dashboards.

Assignments & Case Studies

Assignment 1: The Shadowing Log

Shadow a leader (or senior peer) for 2 days. Record:

  • 3 instances of decision making.
  • 1 instance of conflict resolution.
  • Map their style to the Hersey-Blanchard model.

Case Study: The Toxic High Performer

Scenario: 'Alex' is your top sales rep but belittles the support team. Morale is dropping.

Task: Design a feedback conversation using the SBI (Situation-Behavior-Impact) model. Decide if you will retain or fire Alex if behavior doesn't change.
