📘 Safety Notes

1. Road Safety 🚗🛑

The road is busy with cars and buses. We must be safe.
🚦 Red Light: Stop!
🟢 Green Light: Go!
🚶‍♂️ Always walk on the Footpath.
🦓 Cross the road only at the Zebra Crossing.
Story: Timmy ran on the road and fell. The policeman told him, "Always hold an adult's hand!"

❓ Activity: Draw a Zebra Crossing in your mind (Black & White stripes).

2. Stranger Danger 👀🚫

A Stranger is someone you do not know.
🚫 Never take candy or toys from a stranger.
🚫 Never go anywhere with a stranger.
✅ If a stranger talks to you, run to your parents or teacher immediately.

3. Safe Play Habits ⚽🏃‍♂️

Playing is fun, but we must play safely.
✅ Play in the park or playground.
🚫 Do not play on the road or near stairs.
🚫 Do not push your friends while playing. It can hurt them.

4. Not Touching Sharp Objects 🔪✋

Some things in the house are sharp and dangerous.
🚫 Knives, Scissors, Blades, Needles.
Do not touch them! They can cut your skin and cause blood to come out.
Only adults should use these tools.

5. Fire Safety 🔥🧯

Fire is hot and can burn us.
🚫 Do not play with matches or lighters.
🚫 Stay away from the gas stove in the kitchen.
✅ If you see a fire, tell an adult immediately. Do not hide!

6. Saying NO to Wrong Things ❌✋

If someone asks you to do something dangerous or bad, say NO!
Example: "Come, let's break this window." -> Say NO!
Example: "Let's steal this chocolate." -> Say NO!
Be brave and make good choices.

7. Asking for Help 🙋‍♂️

It is okay to ask for help if you are scared or hurt.
✅ Tell your Parents.
✅ Tell your Teachers.
✅ If you are lost, tell a Policeman.
Never be afraid to speak up!

8. Good Touch & Bad Touch 🤲❌

Your body belongs to you.
✅ Good Touch: Makes you feel safe (Like Mom's hug, Dad's pat on head).
🚫 Bad Touch: Makes you feel sad, scared, or uncomfortable.
If someone gives you a bad touch, shout NO, run away, and tell your parents!
