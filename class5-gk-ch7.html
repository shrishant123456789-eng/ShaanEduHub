Key Points to Remember
  • Plants: Flowering vs Non-flowering.
  • Animals: Vertebrates (Bones) vs Invertebrates (No bones).
  • Habitat: Where an organism lives (Aquatic, Terrestrial).
  • Food Chain: Producer ➔ Consumer ➔ Decomposer.
  • Extinct: Gone forever (Dodo). Endangered: In danger (Tiger).
  • Sanctuary: Protects animals. National Park: Protects whole ecosystem.
  • 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.
