1. My Amazing Body 🧍‍♂️

Hello friend! Our body is like a wonderful machine. It has many parts.

External Body Parts: These are parts we can see outside.

  • Head: To wear a hat! 🧢
  • Hands: To hold things and clap. 👏
  • Legs: To run, jump, and dance. 🏃‍♂️
  • Neck: Helps us move our head. 🧣

[Image: External Body Parts of a Child]

2. Internal Organs (Inside Us) 🫀

Some parts are hidden inside our body. They work day and night!

  • Brain 🧠: It is inside our head. It helps us think and learn.
  • Heart ❤️: It is inside our chest. It pumps blood. It goes dhak-dhak!
  • Lungs 🫁: They help us to breathe air.
  • Stomach: It digests the tasty food we eat. 🥪

[Image: Simple Diagram of Brain Heart Lungs Stomach]

3. Five Sense Organs 👀👂👃👅✋

We have 5 magic windows to the world!

  • Eyes 👀: We SEE beautiful colors.
  • Ears 👂: We HEAR music and sounds.
  • Nose 👃: We SMELL flowers (and yummy pizza!).
  • Tongue 👅: We TASTE sweet, sour, and salty food.
  • Skin ✋: We FEEL if something is hot or cold, soft or hard.

[Image: The Five Sense Organs Chart]

4. Cleanliness & Hygiene 🛁

To keep germs away, we must keep our body clean!

  • Brush teeth twice a day (Morning & Night). 🪥
  • Take a bath every day with soap. 🧼
  • Trim nails regularly. 💅
  • Wash hands before and after eating. 👐

[Image: Child Brushing Teeth and Washing Hands]

5. Healthy Food & Exercise 🥗🧘‍♀️

Good Food: Fruits 🍎, Vegetables 🥦, Milk 🥛, and Dal make us strong.
Junk Food: Too many chocolates 🍫 and chips are bad for health.

Exercise: Playing outside, running, and doing simple Yoga keeps us fit and happy! 😊

6. Safety & First Aid 🚑

Be Safe: Do not play with sharp scissors ✂️ or fire 🔥. Do not run on the road.
First Aid: If you get a small cut, clean it and put a bandage 🩹. Always call an adult for help!
