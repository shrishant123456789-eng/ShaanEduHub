📖 CHAPTER NOTES

1. Self-Introduction

Definition: Telling others basic facts about yourself.

Purpose: To help people know who you are and build confidence.

Method: Name → Class → Hobbies → Ambition.

Example: "Hello! I am Aarav. I study in Class 4 at Modern School. I love playing cricket and want to be a pilot."

Common Mistake: Speaking too softly or looking at the ground.

Tip: Always smile! 😊

[Image: Child introducing self]

Quick Check: What is the first thing you say in an intro? (Answer: Name/Greeting)

2. Conversation Practice

Definition: Talking between two or more people.

Purpose: To share ideas and feelings.

Method: Listen first → Respond politely → Ask a question back.

Example: "How was your day?" "It was great! I learned about space. How was yours?"

Common Mistake: Interrupting when others are talking.

Tip: Make eye contact.

[Image: Two friends talking]

Quick Check: Should you interrupt a speaker? (No)

3. Dialogue Writing & Speaking

Definition: Writing down a conversation before saying it.

Purpose: To plan what to say in different situations.

Method: Use speaker names and colons (e.g., Mother: ... Rahul: ...).

Common Mistake: Forgetting question marks.

[Image: Script writing]

4. Show & Tell

Definition: Showing an object and talking about it.

Purpose: To describe things clearly.

Method: Introduction of object → Features → Why it is special.

Example: "This is my tiffin box. It is blue and has stickers."

[Image: Child holding a toy]

5. Role Play

Definition: Acting out a character or situation.

Purpose: To practice real-life communication.

Method: Understand your role → Use appropriate voice → Stay in character.

[Image: Children in costumes]

6. Telephone Conversation (Polite)

Definition: Speaking to someone over a phone.

Purpose: To communicate when someone is far away.

Method: Say "Hello" → Identify yourself → State purpose → Say "Goodbye".

[Image: Using a phone]

7. Listening Comprehension

Definition: Understanding what is being said.

Purpose: To gather information accurately.

Method: Stop talking → Focus on the speaker → Visualize the story.

[Image: Child listening carefully]

8. Following Instructions

Definition: Doing exactly what you are told.

Purpose: To complete tasks correctly and safely.

Method: Listen to the whole step → Repeat in mind → Perform.

[Image: Recipe/Manual steps]

9. Group Discussion (Basic)

Definition: Sharing ideas in a small group.

Purpose: To learn teamwork and problem-solving.

Method: Wait for your turn → Say "I think..." or "I agree with..."

[Image: Group of kids in circle]

10. Presentation Skills

Definition: Giving a short talk on a topic.

Purpose: To share knowledge with an audience.

Method: Clear opening → 3 main points → Summary.

[Image: Poster presentation]

11. Speech Practice (1-2 Minutes)

Definition: A formal talk delivered to many people.

Purpose: To persuade or inspire others.

Method: Hook the audience → Explain topic → Call to action.

[Image: Child at a podium]
