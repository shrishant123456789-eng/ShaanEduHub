🖥️ Parts of a Computer

Let's remember our computer friends!
• Monitor: Looks like a TV 📺. Shows pictures.
• CPU: The brain 🧠. Does all the thinking.
• Keyboard: Has many keys to type ⌨️.
• Mouse: Helps to point and click 🖱️.
• Printer: Prints work on paper 🖨️.

👉 Can you point to the CPU in your mind?
⌨️ Keyboard & Mouse Magic

Keyboard:
• Alphabet Keys (A-Z) to type names.
• Number Keys (0-9) to type age.
• Enter Key ↵ to go to next line.
• Spacebar is the longest key!

Mouse:
• Left Click to select 👆.
• Double Click to open.
• Scroll wheel moves page up/down.

🎨 Fun with Paint

We love drawing! Remember the tools?
• Pencil ✏️: Free drawing.
• Eraser 🧼: Rubs out mistakes.
• Fill Color 🪣: Fills color in shapes.
• Brush 🖌️: Paints thick lines.
"Drawing makes us creative!"

🛡️ Be Safe & Smart

• Sit straight on your chair 🪑.
• Don't eat or drink near computer 🍔🚫.
• Don't touch wires ⚡.
• Ask parents before going online 🌐.
• Keep passwords secret 🔐.

👉 Show a thumbs up if you follow rules! 👍
