⚡ Quick Revision

  • Plants give us Oxygen, Food, and Medicine.
  • Roots hold the plant; Leaves make food (Photosynthesis).
  • Herbs = Small (Tulsi). Shrubs = Bushy (Rose). Trees = Big (Mango).
  • Climbers need support (Money Plant). Creepers crawl (Watermelon).
  • Photosynthesis needs: Sunlight + Water + CO2.
  • Medicinal plants: Neem, Tulsi, Aloe Vera, Turmeric.
  • Van Mahotsav is the festival of trees.
  • We must not cut trees (Deforestation) but plant more (Afforestation).
