1. Festivals & Days 🎉

India is a land of festivals!

  • Independence Day: 15th August 🇮🇳
  • Republic Day: 26th January 🇮🇳
  • Gandhi Jayanti: 2nd October (Birthday of Bapu).
  • Diwali: Festival of Lights. 🪔
  • Holi: Festival of Colors. 🎨
  • Eid: A festival of brotherhood. 🌙
  • Christmas: 25th December (Santa brings gifts). 🎅

2. Amazing Animal World 🦒🐳

Some animals hold world records!

  • Blue Whale: The largest animal in the world. (Lives in ocean).
  • Giraffe: The tallest animal. (Has a long neck).
  • Cheetah: The fastest land animal. 🐆
  • Ostrich: The largest bird (It cannot fly, but runs fast). 🐦

4. Brain Teasers & Riddles 🧩

Can you guess who am I?
Riddle: I have a face but no eyes. I have hands but no legs.
Answer: A Clock! ⏰

Riddle: I have to be broken before you can eat me.
Answer: An Egg! 🥚

5. Logos & Brands 🍏Ⓜ️

We see pictures on products. These are called Logos.

  • Apple: A picture of an apple with a bite. (Phones/Computers).
  • McDonald's: A big yellow 'M'. (Burgers/Fries).
  • Google: Colorful letter 'G'. (Internet).
