1. India: Location & Neighbors

Definition: India is a vast country located in the continent of Asia (Southern part).

Basic: It is the 7th largest country in the world by area.

Advanced: It lies entirely in the Northern Hemisphere. It has 9 neighbors: Pakistan, Afghanistan (NW), China, Nepal, Bhutan (N), Myanmar, Bangladesh (E), Sri Lanka, Maldives (S - Sea neighbors).

[Map of India showing Neighbors]
💡 Tip: "My Neighbor Likes Mangoes" can help remember neighbors!

Daily Life: When we look at a world map, India looks like a kite flying below China.

2. States & Union Territories

Fact: India has 28 States and 8 Union Territories (UTs).

Key Capitals: New Delhi (Capital of India), Mumbai (Maharashtra), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Kolkata (West Bengal).

Advanced: Rajasthan is the largest state. Goa is the smallest state. Ladakh is the largest UT.

[Map showing States & UTs]
💡 Tip: 28 + 8 = 36 entities to remember!

3. Physical Features

Diversity: India has Mountains (Himalayas), Plains (Northern Plains), Deserts (Thar), Plateaus (Deccan), and Islands (Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep).

[Diagram of Mountains, Rivers, Desert]

Daily Life: People in hills wear woolens; people near sea eat fish and rice.

4. National Symbols

  • Animal: Royal Bengal Tiger (Strength)
  • Bird: Peacock (Grace)
  • Flower: Lotus (Purity)
  • Anthem: Jana Gana Mana (by Rabindranath Tagore)
  • Song: Vande Mataram (by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee)
[Collage of National Symbols]

5. Important Dates

Independence Day: 15th August (1947)

Republic Day: 26th January (1950 - Constitution started)

Gandhi Jayanti: 2nd October

6. Civics: Constitution & Govt

Constitution: The supreme book of laws. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is the Father of the Constitution.

Rights & Duties: We have rights (freedom, equality) but also duties (respect flag, keep country clean).

Government: Parliament makes laws. President is Head of State. Prime Minister is Head of Government.
