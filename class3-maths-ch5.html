1. Division as Sharing (बांटना) 🍫

Imagine you have 12 chocolates and 3 friends (Riya, Amit, and You). How many will each get?

We distribute them equally one by one.

🍫🍫🍫🍫 (You) | 🍫🍫🍫🍫 (Riya) | 🍫🍫🍫🍫 (Amit)

12 ÷ 3 = 4. Each gets 4 chocolates! Equal sharing is called Division.

2. Division as Grouping 📦

We have 10 balls. We want to put 2 balls in each box. How many boxes do we need?

⚽⚽ | ⚽⚽ | ⚽⚽ | ⚽⚽ | ⚽⚽

We made 5 groups. So, 10 ÷ 2 = 5.

Difference: In sharing, we know the groups (people). In grouping, we find the number of groups!

3. Division Facts 📝

Division is the opposite of Multiplication!

If 3 × 4 = 12, then:

  • 12 ÷ 3 = 4
  • 12 ÷ 4 = 3
Any number ÷ 1 = Same Number (5 ÷ 1 = 5)
Any number ÷ Itself = 1 (5 ÷ 5 = 1)
0 ÷ Any number = 0 (0 ÷ 5 = 0)

4. Divide 2-Digit Numbers (No Remainder) 🔢

Let's divide 48 ÷ 2.

Step 1: Divide Tens. 4 tens ÷ 2 = 2 tens.

Step 2: Divide Ones. 8 ones ÷ 2 = 4 ones.

48 ÷ 2 = 24

7. Word Problems (Story Maths) 📖

Look for keywords like: Share equally, Each, Groups.

Example: 20 pencils are put into 4 boxes equally. How many in each box?

Think 🤔: We need to split 20 into 4 parts.

Solve: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 Pencils.
