Quick Revision ⚡
  • Ratio: Comparison of two quantities (a : b).
  • Proportion: Two ratios are equal (a : b :: c : d).
  • Percentage: Fraction with denominator 100 (%).
  • Simplest Form: Divide both terms by HCF.
  • Unitary Method: Find value of 1, then find value of many.
  • To %: Multiply fraction/decimal by 100.
  • % of Quantity: (Rate/100) × Total.
