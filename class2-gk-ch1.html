📚 Study Notes (पढ़ाई)

1. All About Me (मेरा परिचय) 🙋‍♂️🙋‍♀️

Every person is special! To introduce yourself, you tell people about:

  • My Name: What people call you. (मेरा नाम)
  • My Age: How many years old you are. (मेरी उम्र)
  • Gender: Are you a Boy 👦 or a Girl 👧?
  • Birthday: The special day you were born! 🎂

[Image: Boy and Girl introducing themselves]

2. My Family Members (मेरे परिवार के सदस्य) 👨‍👩‍👦

People who live with us and love us are our family.

  • Father (Papa): He works hard and cares for us. 👔
  • Mother (Mummy): She loves us and looks after the home. 👗
  • Brother (Bhai): A boy sibling. 🧢
  • Sister (Behan): A girl sibling. 🎀

[Image: A happy family photo]

3. Types of Family (परिवार के प्रकार) 🏠

Families can be different sizes:

1. Small Family (Nuclear Family):
Only Father, Mother, and Children live together.
(छोटा परिवार: माता-पिता और बच्चे)

2. Big Family (Joint Family):
Grandparents, Parents, Uncle, Aunt, and Cousins all live together.
(बड़ा परिवार: दादा-दादी, चाचा-चाची सब साथ)

[Image: Comparison of Small vs Big Family]

4. Family Relations (रिश्ते) 🤝

How are we connected?

  • Grandfather (Dada/Nana): Father's or Mother's father. 👴
  • Grandmother (Dadi/Nani): Father's or Mother's mother. 👵
  • Uncle (Chacha/Mama): Father's or Mother's brother. 🧔
  • Aunt (Chachi/Mami): Uncle's wife or parent's sister. 👩‍🦱
  • Cousins: Uncle and Aunt's children. 👫

5. Hobbies & Habits (शौक और आदतें) 🎨🦷

Hobbies: Things we love to do in free time.
Examples: Painting 🖌️, Dancing 💃, Playing Cricket 🏏.

Good Habits (अच्छी आदतें):

  • Brushing teeth twice a day 🦷
  • Respecting elders 🙏
  • Sharing toys 🧸
Bad Habits (बुरी आदतें):
  • Fighting 👊
  • Biting nails 💅
  • Watching too much TV 📺

6. My Address & Routine 🗺️⏰

Address: The place where your house is located. You should know your house number and city.

Daily Routine:
☀️ Morning: Wake up, Brush, Bath, School.
🌤️ Afternoon: Lunch, Homework, Rest.
⛅ Evening: Play time!
🌙 Night: Dinner, Sleep.
