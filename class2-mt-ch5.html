📘 Study Notes

1. Personal Cleanliness 🧍‍♂️✨

Personal Cleanliness means keeping our own body clean.
🌟 Why? Germs live on dirty bodies and make us sick.
✅ Take a bath every day with soap and water. 🚿
✅ Trim your nails regularly. 💅
✅ Comb your hair neatly. 💇‍♂️
Story: Sonu played in the mud but didn't bathe. He got itchy rashes. Monu bathed daily and stayed healthy!

❓ Activity: Check your nails now. Are they long or short?

2. Clean Clothes 👕

We must always wear clean and washed clothes.
🚫 Dirty clothes smell bad and have germs.
✅ Change your clothes after coming back from school or play.
✅ Always wear a clean uniform to school. It makes you look smart! 😎

3. Clean Surroundings 🏠🌳

Surroundings mean the area around us.
If our house is clean but the road outside is dirty, mosquitoes will come. 🦟
✅ Keep your room tidy.
✅ Do not throw wrappers on the street.
Clean surroundings = Happy & Healthy Life! 🌈

4. Using Dustbin 🚮

The Dustbin is the house for garbage!
🍌 Throw fruit peels, pencil shavings, and wrappers ONLY in the dustbin.
🚫 Never throw trash on the floor or out of the window.
Use Green Bin for wet waste and Blue Bin for dry waste.

5. Keeping School Clean 🏫

School is our second home.
✅ Do not write on desks or walls. 🖍️🚫
✅ Pick up any paper lying on the floor.
✅ Flush the toilet after use. 🚽
A clean school is a beautiful school!

6. Washing Hands 🧼🤲

Our hands touch many things and get germs.
Wash hands:
1️⃣ Before and after eating food. 🍽️
2️⃣ After using the toilet. 🚽
3️⃣ After playing with pets. 🐶
Use soap and rub for 20 seconds! ⏲️

[attachment_1](attachment)

7. Brushing Teeth Daily 🪥

Brush your teeth twice a day.
☀️ Once in the morning.
🌙 Once at night before sleeping.
If we don't brush, germs will make holes (cavities) in our teeth. Ouch! 🦷💥
Clean teeth give you a sparkling smile! 😁

8. Clean Habits 🏠🏫

Let's promise to follow these habits:
✅ Cover mouth while sneezing or coughing. 🤧
✅ Keep shoes in the shoe rack. 👟
✅ Keep books and toys in their place. 📚
Being clean makes us disciplined and good children.
