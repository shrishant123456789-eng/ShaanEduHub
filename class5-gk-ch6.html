📝 Chapter Notes

1. Human Body Systems

Definition: Groups of organs working together to perform specific functions.

Image: Diagram of Skeleton & Digestive System
  • Skeletal System: 206 bones. Gives shape and support.
  • Muscular System: Helps in movement.
  • Digestive System: Breaks down food (Stomach, Intestines).
  • Circulatory System: Heart pumps blood.
  • Nervous System: Brain controls everything.
💡 Tip: "S.M.D.C.N" - Skeletal, Muscular, Digestive, Circulatory, Nervous.

Check: Which organ pumps blood? (Heart)

2. Nutrients & Balanced Diet

Nutrients: Substances in food that help us grow.

Image: Plate with Dal, Roti, Sabzi, Curd
  • Carbohydrates: Energy giving (Rice, Roti, Sugar).
  • Proteins: Body building (Dal, Eggs, Milk).
  • Fats: Energy bank (Ghee, Oil).
  • Vitamins & Minerals: Protective food (Fruits, Green Vegetables).

Indian Example: A "Thali" with rice, dal, sabzi, and curd is a balanced diet.

3. Diseases & Prevention

Disease: When the body does not work properly.

Image: Doctor giving vaccine
  • Communicable: Spreads from person to person (Cold, Flu, COVID-19).
  • Non-communicable: Does not spread (Diabetes, Heart issues).
  • Deficiency Diseases: Lack of vitamins (Night blindness - Vit A, Scurvy - Vit C).
💡 Tip: C for Citrus, C for Curing Scurvy.
4. First Aid Basics

Definition: Immediate help given to an injured person before a doctor arrives.

Image: First Aid Box with Red Cross
  • Cuts: Wash with water, apply antiseptic.
  • Burns: Put under cold running water.
  • Nosebleed: Pinch nose, lean forward slightly.

Indian Example: Using Turmeric (Haldi) on small cuts is a traditional antiseptic.

5. Mental Health Awareness

Definition: Keeping our mind happy and healthy.

Image: Happy Brain Emoji
  • Emotions: It is okay to feel sad or angry.
  • Sharing: Talk to parents or teachers if you are worried.
  • Stress: Too much worry. Deep breathing helps.

Tip: Good sleep (8-10 hours) is food for the brain.

6. Physical Fitness & Yoga

Fitness: Ability to do daily work without getting tired easily.

Image: Child doing Surya Namaskar
  • Yoga: Ancient Indian practice for body and mind.
  • Surya Namaskar: Complete body exercise.
  • Pranayama: Breathing exercises to calm the mind.
7. Healthy Habits & Hygiene

Hygiene: Practices to stay clean and healthy.

Image: Washing Hands
  • Brush teeth twice a day.
  • Bath daily.
  • Trim nails regularly.
  • Wash hands before and after meals.

Posture: Sit straight to keep the backbone healthy.

8. Safety Rules

Safety: Staying away from danger.

Image: Traffic Lights
  • Home: Do not play with matchsticks or sockets.
  • School: Do not run on stairs.
  • Road: Use Zebra Crossing. Red light means STOP.
