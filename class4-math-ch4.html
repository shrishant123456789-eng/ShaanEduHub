📝 My Study Notes

1. Understanding Factors

A Factor is a number that divides another number completely without leaving a remainder.

Example: Factors of 12

1 × 12 = 12

2 × 6 = 12

3 × 4 = 12

So, Factors of 12 are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

💡 Remember: 1 is a factor of EVERY number. The number itself is the biggest factor.

2. Understanding Multiples

A Multiple is the result of multiplying a number by an integer. Think of "Multiplication Tables"!

Example: Multiples of 5

5 × 1 = 5

5 × 2 = 10

5 × 3 = 15

Multiples: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25...

Multiples never end (Infinite)!

3. Odd and Even Numbers

  • Even Numbers: Numbers that have 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 in the ones place. They can be made into pairs.
  • Odd Numbers: Numbers that have 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 in the ones place. They always leave one alone when paired.

4. Prime and Composite Numbers

Prime Numbers: Numbers that have EXACTLY two factors: 1 and the number itself.

Examples: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13.

Composite Numbers: Numbers that have MORE THAN two factors.

Examples: 4, 6, 8, 9, 10.

⚠️ Special Note: 1 is neither Prime nor Composite! It is a unique number.

5. Sieve of Eratosthenes

A fun way to find all Prime Numbers between 1 and 100.

  1. Write numbers 1 to 100.
  2. Cross out 1.
  3. Circle 2 (Prime) and cross all multiples of 2.
  4. Circle 3 (Prime) and cross all multiples of 3.
  5. Circle 5 (Prime) and cross all multiples of 5.
  6. Continue this until you finish.
[Image Concept: Grid 1-100 with crossed out numbers]

6. Highest Common Factor (HCF)

The largest factor that is common to two or more numbers.

Find HCF of 8 and 12:

Factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8

Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Common Factors: 1, 2, 4

Highest Common Factor: 4

7. Lowest Common Multiple (LCM)

The smallest multiple that is common to two or more numbers.

Find LCM of 3 and 4:

Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18...

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20...

Lowest Common Multiple: 12

8. Prime Factorization (Factor Tree)

Breaking a number down into only Prime Factors using a "Tree".

[Concept: Tree for 12]
12 -> 2 x 6
6 -> 2 x 3
Result: 2 x 2 x 3

We keep splitting composite numbers until we only have Primes left.
