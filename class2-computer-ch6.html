🖱️ What is a Mouse?

A computer mouse is a small Input Device.
It looks like a real mouse with a long tail (wire).
We use it to point at things and click on the screen.
"Aman moves the mouse to open his favorite game." 🎮

⚙️ Parts of a Mouse

A mouse has 3 main parts:

Left
Right

1. Left Button: Used for clicking.
2. Right Button: Used for options.
3. Scroll Wheel: Used to go Up and Down.

👆 Click (Single Click)

Pressing the Left Button once is called a Click.
It makes a "Click" sound.
We use it to Select an item on the screen.

✌️ Double Click

Pressing the Left Button two times quickly is called Double Click.
Click-Click!
We use it to Open a file or a game.

👉 Right Click

Pressing the Right Button once is called Right Click.
It opens a list of choices (Menu) on the screen.
It helps us see what we can do with an item.

🚚 Drag and Drop

1. Click and Hold the left button.
2. Move the mouse.
3. Release the button.
This is used to Move objects from one place to another. 🖼️
