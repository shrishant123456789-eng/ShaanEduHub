Class 2 • Chapter 10 • Smart & Safe Rules
✅ Do's and ❌ Don'ts

Meet Happy Computer! He stays happy when we follow rules.
• Wash hands before touching him 🧼.
• Sit properly on your chair 🪑.
• Be gentle with the mouse and keyboard.
• Don't eat or drink near him 🍔🚫.

✅ Clean Hands
❌ Sticky Fingers
🧼 Clean Hands Magic

Why clean hands?
Dirt and oil from food make keys sticky.
Sticky keys stop working!
"Clean hands make the computer smile 😃"

🪑 Sit Like a King

Your body needs care too!
• Sit Straight.
• Keep feet on the floor.
• Keep eyes level with the screen.
"Your back says thank you when you sit straight!" 🧍‍♂️

⚡ The Sleeping Snakes (Wires)

There are many wires behind the computer.
NEVER pull or touch them.
Wires carry electricity ⚡.
Ask your teacher or parent if a wire is loose.
"Stay safe, stay away from wires!"

⏰ Watch the Clock

Using the computer for too long can hurt your eyes 👀.
• Take breaks.
• Play outside too! ⚽
• Follow the time set by parents.

🌐 Stranger Danger

The internet connects us to the world.
But be careful!
• Never talk to strangers online 🚫.
• Never tell your name or address to strangers.
• Always ask parents before opening a new website.
