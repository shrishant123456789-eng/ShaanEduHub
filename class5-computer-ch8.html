Chapter 8: Cyber Security and Ethics

Class 5-8 Level | Exam Oriented Module

1. Cyber Crime Types

Definition: Any illegal activity done using a computer and the internet is called Cyber Crime.

  • Phishing: Tricking people into giving passwords via fake emails.
  • Identity Theft: Stealing someone's personal details to pretend to be them.
  • Cyber Bullying: Sending mean or threatening messages online.
Exam Tip: "Phishing" sounds like "Fishing" - catching a victim with bait (fake email).

2. Hacking (Ethical Overview)

Hacking: Finding weaknesses in a computer system.

Ethical Hacking (White Hat): Done with permission to fix security holes and protect systems.

Unethical Hacking (Black Hat): Done without permission to steal or destroy data.

⚠️ WARNING: Hacking without permission is a serious crime punishable by law. Never try it.
Difference: Ethical hackers help companies; Criminal hackers harm them.

3. Malware

Malware (Malicious Software): Software designed to damage computers.

Type How it works
Virus Attaches to a file/program. Spreads when you open that file.
Worm Self-replicating. Travels across networks without human help.
Trojan Horse Looks like a useful program (e.g., a game) but has a virus hidden inside.
Exam Tip: Trojan Horses do NOT replicate themselves.

4. Antivirus Working

Definition: Software used to prevent, detect, and remove malware.

Steps: Scan ➝ Detect ➝ Quarantine/Delete.

Examples: Norton, McAfee, QuickHeal, Windows Defender.

Always keep your Antivirus UPDATED to catch new viruses.

5. Firewall

Concept: A security guard between your computer and the Internet.

It checks incoming and outgoing network traffic and blocks unauthorized access.

Analogy: Firewall is like a Gatekeeper at your school gate.

6. Cyber Laws

Laws created to punish cyber criminals and protect citizens.

IT Act 2000 (India): The primary law in India dealing with cyber crime and electronic commerce.

Why needed? To stop online fraud, hacking, and stealing data.

7. Digital Footprint

The trail of data you leave behind when you use the internet.

  • Active: Things you post intentionally (Photos on FB, Tweets).
  • Passive: Data collected without you knowing (IP address, search history).
"Think before you post" because footprints are permanent.

8. Online Privacy & Safety

  • Passwords: Use Strong passwords (Mix of Uppercase, Lowercase, Numbers, Symbols). Never share them.
  • Privacy: Keep social media accounts 'Private'.
  • Stranger Danger: Do not accept friend requests from strangers.
