2.1 Resume & CV Engineering

The ATS (Applicant Tracking System)

An ATS is robot software used by 99% of big companies (TCS, Infosys, Google) to filter resumes before a human sees them. It scans for keywords.

Diagram: Resume -> ATS Scanner -> Reject/Accept Bin
Real Life Example: Imagine a security guard at a wedding hall who only lets people in if their invitation card has a specific "QR Code". The ATS is that guard. Your "Keywords" are the QR Code.
Tip: ATS reads left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Avoid columns and graphics.
Structure & Action Verbs

Start bullet points with Action Verbs (Created, Managed, Developed). Use the XYZ formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]".

Bad: "I worked on sales."
Good: "Generated ₹5 Lakh revenue (Y) by acquiring 50 new clients (X) using cold calling strategies (Z)."

2.2 High-Impact Cover Letters

The 4-Paragraph Structure
  1. The Hook: Who you are and why you are writing.
  2. The Value: Specific examples of your work.
  3. The Bridge: Why this specific company?
  4. The Ask: Call to action (Interview request).
Context: Applying to Zomato? Mention how you admire their recent quick-commerce logistics and how your supply chain project aligns with it.

2.3 LinkedIn & Digital Identity

Headline Optimization

Formula: [Role] | [Key Skill 1] | [Key Skill 2] | [Unique Value Proposition]

Example: "Java Developer | Spring Boot Specialist | AWS Certified | Building scalable fintech solutions for Rural India"

2.4 Personal Branding Strategy

Brand Positioning

Your brand is what people say about you when you are not in the room. Move from "Generalist" to "Specialist".

Case: Instead of being just a "Content Writer", position yourself as a "B2B SaaS Technical Writer".
Matrix: Generalist (Low Value) vs Specialist (High Value)
