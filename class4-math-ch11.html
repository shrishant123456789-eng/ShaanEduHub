Chapter 11: Time and Calendar

Class 5 • Mathematics

"Time is the most precious gift. Spend it wisely!" ⏳

My Study Notes 📝

1. Reading Time to the Exact Minute
  • The clock has 60 small marks. Each mark is 1 minute.
  • The numbers 1 to 12 show hours. The gap between two numbers is 5 minutes.
  • To read exact time:
    • Look at the Hour hand (Short). It tells the hour.
    • Look at the Minute hand (Long). Multiply the number it has passed by 5, then add the small lines.
Example: Hour hand is past 4. Minute hand is on the 3rd small line after 2.
2 stands for 10 mins. + 3 small lines = 13 mins.
Time is 4:13.
❓ Check: If the minute hand is on the 2nd dot after 7, how many minutes have passed? (Ans: 37 mins)
2. Use of A.M. and P.M.
A day has 24 hours, but our normal clock shows only 12. So we divide the day into two parts:
  • A.M. (Ante Meridiem): From 12:00 Midnight to 12:00 Noon. (Morning time).
  • P.M. (Post Meridiem): From 12:00 Noon to 12:00 Midnight. (Afternoon, Evening, Night).
Breakfast at 8:00 A.M.
Dinner at 8:00 P.M.
Note: 12:00 Noon and 12:00 Midnight do not have AM or PM usually, but we say 12 Noon or 12 Midnight.
💡 Tip: A comes before P in alphabet. So A.M. comes first (Morning).
3. Reading the Calendar
  • Year: Has 12 months. Total 365 days (366 in Leap Year).
  • Month: Has 30 or 31 days. February has 28 or 29.
  • Week: Has 7 days (Monday to Sunday).

Days in Months Trick (Knuckles): Jan(31), Feb(28/29), Mar(31), Apr(30), May(31), Jun(30), Jul(31), Aug(31), Sep(30), Oct(31), Nov(30), Dec(31).
If today is Monday, 5th Jan. The next Monday will be 5 + 7 = 12th Jan.
4. The 24-Hour Clock Format
Used in railways, airlines, and military to avoid AM/PM confusion.
  • The day starts at 00:00 (Midnight).
  • From 1 AM to 12 Noon, time is same as 12-hour clock (just remove AM).
  • From 1 PM onwards, we add 12 to the hours.
1:00 PM → 1 + 12 = 13:00 hours.
8:30 PM → 8 + 12 = 20:30 hours.
12 Midnight → 00:00 hours (Start of new day).
5. Conversion of Time
Standard Units:
  • 1 Day = 24 Hours
  • 1 Hour = 60 Minutes
  • 1 Minute = 60 Seconds

Rules:
  • Big Unit to Small Unit → Multiply (Days to Hours: ×24).
  • Small Unit to Big Unit → Divide (Minutes to Hours: ÷60).
Convert 3 days into hours:
3 × 24 = 72 hours.
Convert 2 hours 10 mins into mins:
(2 × 60) + 10 = 120 + 10 = 130 minutes.
6. Calculating Time Duration
Elapsed time is the duration between a Start Time and an End Time.
  • Method 1: Count forward on a clock.
  • Method 2: Subtraction (End Time - Start Time). *Be careful crossing 12:00*.
School starts at 8:00 AM and closes at 2:00 PM.
8 AM to 12 Noon = 4 hours.
12 Noon to 2 PM = 2 hours.
Total Duration = 4 + 2 = 6 hours.
7. Concept of Leap Year
  • An ordinary year has 365 days.
  • The Earth takes approx 365 ¼ days to revolve around Sun.
  • These ¼ days add up (¼ + ¼ + ¼ + ¼ = 1) every 4 years to make an extra day.
  • A Leap Year has 366 days. The extra day is added to February (29 days).
  • Check: If a year is divisible by 4, it is a Leap Year. (For century years like 1900, 2000, must divide by 400).
2024 ÷ 4 = 506 (No remainder) → Leap Year.
2025 ÷ 4 = Remainder 1 → Not a Leap Year.
