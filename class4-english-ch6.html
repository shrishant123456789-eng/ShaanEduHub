My Study Notes 📝

⚡ Flash Rules ⚡

  • Capital Letters: Start of sentence, Names (Rahul), Places (India), 'I'.
  • Full Stop (.): End of a telling sentence.
  • Question Mark (?): End of an asking sentence.
  • Exclamation Mark (!): Strong feelings (Joy, Shock).
  • Comma (,): Separates items in a list.
  • " ": Shows exactly what someone said.
  • 's: Shows belonging (Ravi's bat) or short forms (It's).
