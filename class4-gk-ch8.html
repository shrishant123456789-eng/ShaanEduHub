Class Notes 📚
1. Continents & Oceans

Continent: A very large landmass. There are 7 continents.

List: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.

Ocean: A huge body of saltwater. There are 5 oceans.

List: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic.

Example: We live in Asia (if in India). The Pacific is the largest ocean.
Tip: Asia is the biggest continent. Australia is the smallest.
[Image: World Map showing 7 Continents]
❓ Check: Which is the smallest continent? (Ans: Australia)
2. Important Countries & Capitals

Every country has a main city called a Capital where the government works.

India 🇮🇳 → New Delhi

USA 🇺🇸 → Washington D.C.

United Kingdom 🇬🇧 → London

France 🇫🇷 → Paris

Japan 🇯🇵 → Tokyo

Example: If you visit the Eiffel Tower, you are in Paris, the capital of France.
Tip: Remember 'D' for Delhi, 'T' for Tokyo.
[Image: Flags of different countries]
❓ Check: What is the capital of Japan? (Ans: Tokyo)
3. World Famous Monuments

Monuments are old buildings or statues that are special to history.

• Taj Mahal (India) - Symbol of love.

• Eiffel Tower (France) - Iron tower in Paris.

• Statue of Liberty (USA) - Gift from France.

• Great Wall of China (China) - Longest wall.

Example: Millions of people visit the Taj Mahal every year.
Tip: Pyramids are in Egypt (Triangle shaped tombs).
[Image: Collage of Taj Mahal & Eiffel Tower]
❓ Check: Where is the Great Wall located? (Ans: China)
4. Maps & Globe

Map: A drawing of the earth on a flat paper.

Globe: A round model of the earth.

Maps show more details. Globes show the true shape.

Directions: North (Top), South (Bottom), East (Right), West (Left).

Example: Sailors use maps and compasses to find their way in the sea.
Tip: NEWS = North East West South.
[Image: A Globe and a Flat Map]
❓ Check: Is a map flat or round? (Ans: Flat)
5. Natural Wonders

Beautiful places made by nature, not humans.

• Mt. Everest: Highest mountain (Nepal/China).

• Grand Canyon: Deep valley (USA).

• Great Barrier Reef: Largest coral reef (Australia).

Example: The Northern Lights are colorful lights in the sky (Nature's magic).
Tip: 'Natural' means made by God/Nature.
[Image: Mt Everest]
❓ Check: Name the highest mountain. (Ans: Mt. Everest)
6. Different Cultures

Culture is how people live: their food, clothes, language, and festivals.

Japan: Eat Sushi, wear Kimono.

Italy: Famous for Pizza and Pasta.

India: Many festivals like Diwali, Holi.

Example: Saying 'Namaste' is Indian culture. Saying 'Hello' is Western culture.
Tip: Respect everyone's culture. Variety is beautiful.
[Image: Kids in different traditional dresses]
❓ Check: Kimono is a dress from which country? (Ans: Japan)
7. International Organizations (UNO)

UNO: United Nations Organization.

Formed after big wars to keep peace in the world.

It helps countries talk instead of fight. It also helps poor children (UNICEF).

Example: If two countries are angry, the UN tries to make them friends.
Tip: UN = Peace Keeper.
[Image: UN Logo (Blue flag with olive branches)]
❓ Check: What is the main goal of UNO? (Ans: Peace)
8. World Environment Awareness

Our Earth is getting dirty (Pollution) and hot (Global Warming).

We must use the 3 Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.

Plastic is bad for oceans. Trees are good for air.

Example: Using a cloth bag instead of plastic saves the earth.
Tip: Plant a tree on your birthday!
[Image: Earth with a sad face vs happy face]
❓ Check: Should we use single-use plastic? (Ans: No)
9. Global Citizenship

A Global Citizen is someone who cares about the whole world, not just their own country.

We share one planet. We must help everyone.

Values: Kindness, Peace, Responsibility.

Example: Helping during a disaster in another country is global citizenship.
Tip: Think Global, Act Local.
[Image: Kids holding hands around the globe]
❓ Check: Do global citizens care only about their city? (Ans: No)
