Numbers & Number System

  • Place value helps identify the position of digits
  • Indian system uses Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, Lakhs, Crores
  • Prime numbers have only two factors: 1 and itself
  • HCF is the highest common factor; LCM is the lowest common multiple

Operations on Whole Numbers

  • BODMAS: Brackets, Of, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction
  • Long division is used for dividing by 2-digit numbers
  • Estimation helps in quick calculations

Fractions

  • Proper fraction: numerator < denominator
  • Improper fraction: numerator ≥ denominator
  • Like fractions have the same denominator
  • Always simplify fractions to lowest terms

Decimals

  • Decimal point separates whole and fractional parts
  • Align decimal points when adding or subtracting
  • Money calculations use decimals (₹1.50 = 1 rupee 50 paise)

Geometry

  • Acute angle: less than 90°
  • Right angle: exactly 90°
  • Obtuse angle: between 90° and 180°
  • Parallel lines never meet

Measurement

  • 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 cm = 10 mm
  • 1 kg = 1000 g
  • 1 l = 1000 ml
  • 24-hour clock: 2:00 PM = 14:00 hours
