My Body Notebook 📝

🫀 Internal Organs

These organs are inside our body. We cannot see them, but they work 24/7!

1. Brain: It is inside our head. It controls our body and helps us think.

💡 Tip: Brain is the 'Computer' of our body.

2. Heart: It is on the left side of our chest. It pumps blood to all parts.

💡 Tip: Heart is the 'Engine' or 'Pump'.

3. Lungs: Two air bags in our chest. They help us breathe air in and out.

4. Stomach: It is like a bag. It digests (mixes and breaks down) the food we eat.

5. Kidneys: Bean-shaped organs. They clean our blood and remove waste (urine).

👀 The 5 Sense Organs

These organs help us know the world around us.

  • 👁️ Eyes: To SEE (Sight).
  • 👂 Ears: To HEAR (Sound).
  • 👃 Nose: To SMELL (Scents).
  • 👅 Tongue: To TASTE (Sweet, Sour, Salty, Bitter).
  • ✋ Skin: To FEEL (Hot, Cold, Soft, Hard).

💡 Tip: We have 2 eyes, 2 ears, 1 nose, 1 tongue, but skin is everywhere!

[Image: Face showing eyes, nose, ears, mouth]
🚿 Hygiene & Good Habits

Keeping our body clean is called Hygiene.

Brushing: Brush teeth twice a day (Morning & Night).

Bathing: Take a bath every day to remove dirt and germs.

Hand Wash: Wash hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet.

Nails: Trim nails regularly so germs don't hide there.

🍎 Nutrition: Healthy vs Junk

Food gives us energy to play and study.

Healthy Food: Makes us strong.

Examples: Fruits, Vegetables, Milk, Eggs, Dal, Roti.

Junk Food: Tastes good but is bad for health if eaten too much.

Examples: Burger, Pizza, Chips, Soda, Candy.
