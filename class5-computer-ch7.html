📘 Study Notes

1. Computer Network

Definition: A group of computers connected together to share information and resources (like printers or files).
🇮🇳 Life Example: Like a joint family living in one house sharing one TV and one fridge!
💡 Tip: Network = Net (Mesh) + Work. Working together in a mesh.

2. Types of Network

LAN (Local Area Network): Small area. Connects computers in a room, building, or school.

MAN (Metropolitan Area Network): Covers a city.

WAN (Wide Area Network): Covers the whole world (Internet).

🇮🇳 Life Example:
  • LAN: Intercom in your apartment society.
  • MAN: Cable TV network in Mumbai.
  • WAN: Railway booking system across India.

3. Internet vs Intranet

Internet: Public network. Anyone can access it (like a public park).
Intranet: Private network. Only employees of a company can access it (like a private home).
💡 Tip: Internet = International. Intranet = Internal.

4. Client - Server Model

Server: Powerful computer that gives information (Waiter).

Client: Computer that asks for information (Customer).

5. IP Address

Unique address of a computer on the network. Like 192.168.1.5.
🇮🇳 Life Example: Just like every house in Delhi has a unique address/pincode so postman can find it, every computer needs an IP address so data can find it.

6. WWW & Web Tools

WWW (World Wide Web): Huge collection of websites stored on the Internet.

Web Browser: Software to open websites (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

Search Engine: Website to search for information (Google, Bing).

💡 Analogy: Internet is the Road. Browser is the Car. Search Engine is the Map.

7. URL (Uniform Resource Locator)

It is the address of a website.

https://www.google.com

Protocol :// Subdomain . DomainName . Type

❓ Important Questions (Exam)

  • 1. What is a computer network?
    A set of connected computers that share data and devices.
  • 2. Full form of LAN?
    Local Area Network.
  • 3. Full form of WAN?
    Wide Area Network.
  • 4. What is a Server?
    A main computer that provides services to other computers.
  • 5. Give an example of a Web Browser.
    Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox.
  • 6. What is a Client?
    A computer that requests services from a server.
  • 7. What is an IP Address?
    A unique number assigned to every computer on a network.
  • 8. Difference between LAN and WAN?
    LAN is for small areas (rooms); WAN is for large areas (countries).
  • 9. What is an ISP?
    Internet Service Provider (e.g., Airtel, Jio).
  • 10. What is a URL?
    Uniform Resource Locator (Website Address).
  • 11. Who owns the Internet?
    No single person owns the Internet.
  • 12. What connects a LAN?
    Usually cables or Wi-Fi.
  • 13. Example of Search Engine?
    Google, Bing, Yahoo.
  • 14. What is Intranet?
    A private network used inside an organization.
  • 15. What is the web page format?
    HTML (HyperText Markup Language).
  • 16. Full form of MAN?
    Metropolitan Area Network.
  • 17. Is the Internet a WAN?
    Yes, it is the largest WAN.
  • 18. What device connects to ISP?
    Modem / Router.
  • 19. What is the Homepage?
    The first page of a website.
  • 20. Can we print via network?
    Yes, using a shared printer.
  • 21. Full form of WWW?
    World Wide Web.
  • 22. What is Netiquette?
    Good manners to follow on the Internet.
  • 23. What is Hyperlink?
    A link that takes you to another page when clicked.
  • 24. Protocol in https://google.com?
    https (HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure).
  • 25. Wireless LAN is called?
    WLAN or Wi-Fi.
  • 26. Which is faster, LAN or WAN?
    LAN is typically faster.
  • 27. What is downloading?
    Getting files from the Internet to your computer.
  • 28. What is uploading?
    Sending files from your computer to the Internet.
  • 29. Meaning of 'Com' in .com?
    Commercial.
  • 30. Meaning of 'Edu' in .edu?
    Education.
