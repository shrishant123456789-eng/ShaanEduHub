📚 Food Stories

1. Why Do We Eat? 😋

Just like a car needs petrol to run, our body needs Food to work and play! Food gives us energy. It helps us grow tall and strong.

We have three main food friends:

  • Energy Givers: Rice, Wheat, Sugar, Butter. (They make us run fast! 🏃)
  • Body Builders: Pulses, Eggs, Milk. (They make muscles! 💪)
  • Protectors: Fruits and Vegetables. (They fight germs! 🛡️)

[Image: Chart showing Energy, Body-building, and Protective foods]

2. Fruits & Vegetables 🍇🥕

Nature's candy!
Fruits: Apples 🍎, Bananas 🍌, Mangoes 🥭. They are sweet and juicy. Most fruits have seeds inside.
Vegetables: Spinach 🥬, Potatoes 🥔, Carrots 🥕. We usually cook them to make tasty dishes. Green leafy vegetables keep our eyes sharp!

[Image: A colorful basket of fruits and vegetables]

3. Grains, Pulses & Milk 🌾🥛

  • Cereals (Grains): Wheat (for chapati), Rice, Corn 🌽. These fill our tummy.
  • Pulses (Dal): Kidney beans (Rajma), Gram. They have protein.
  • Milk Products: Milk gives us strong bones 🦴. We make Curd, Cheese, Butter, and Ice-cream 🍦 from milk.

[Image: Wheat sacks, Rice bowl, and Milk bottle]

4. Healthy vs. Junk Food 🍔❌

Healthy Food: Home-cooked food, fruits, salad, dal-rice. It makes us happy and smart. 😊

Junk Food: Burgers, chips, cold drinks, too many chocolates. They taste good but make our tummy sad and lazy. 😴 We should eat them very rarely!

[Image: Healthy Thali vs Burger and Fries]

5. Eating Habits & Water 💧🍽️

To be a super-kid, follow these rules:

  • Balanced Diet: Eat a little bit of everything (Roti, Dal, Sabzi, Salad).
  • Water: Drink 6-8 glasses of water every day. It cleans our body.
  • Raw vs Cooked: We eat cucumber raw (salad). We cook potato and rice.
  • Food Sources: Plants give us fruits/veg. Animals give us milk/eggs/meat. 🐔🐮
  • Good Manners: Wash hands before eating. Chew food properly. Don't talk while eating! 🤫
