My India Notebook 📖

🐯 National Symbols

Every country has symbols that make it special. Here are ours:

Flag (Tiranga): It has three colors - Saffron (Top), White (Middle), Green (Bottom) with a blue Ashoka Chakra.

Animal: Royal Bengal Tiger. It stands for strength.

Bird: Peacock. It is very beautiful and colorful.

Flower: Lotus. It grows in water and is very pretty.

💡 Tip: The 'T' in Tiger stands for Terror (to enemies) and Strength!

[Image: Collage of Tiger, Peacock, Lotus, and Flag]
🗺️ States & Capitals

India is a very big country!

  • It has 28 States and 8 Union Territories.
  • New Delhi is the Capital of India.
  • Rajasthan is the largest state.
  • Goa is the smallest state.

💡 Tip: Goa is small like a 'Go' button!

[Image: Map of India showing states]

👨‍💼 Leaders & Heroes

Important people who lead and protect our country.

President: The Head of the Country. Known as the First Citizen of India.

Prime Minister: The Head of the Government who runs the country.

Freedom Fighters: Heroes who fought for India's freedom.

Examples: Mahatma Gandhi (Father of Nation), Bhagat Singh, Subhash Chandra Bose.

🎶 Anthem & Song

We sing these with pride!

National Anthem: "Jana Gana Mana". Written by Rabindranath Tagore. We stand still when singing it.

National Song: "Vande Mataram". Written by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.

Q1. What is the National Animal of India?
