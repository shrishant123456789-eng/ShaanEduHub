1. Global Sports 🌍

Sports played by many countries together are called Global Sports. The biggest one is the Olympics, which happens every 4 years. We also have FIFA for Football and the ICC World Cup for Cricket.

Picture of Olympic Rings (Blue, Yellow, Black, Green, Red)
💡 Tip: Olympics = 5 Rings = 5 Continents!
Q: How often are Olympics held? (Ans: Every 4 years)

2. Sports Terms 🎾

Every sport has a special name for its playground and rules. For example, we play Cricket on a Pitch, but we play Badminton or Tennis on a Court. To win in Football, you must score a Goal.

Drawing of a Cricket Pitch and a Football Goalpost
💡 Tip: In Chess, the word "Checkmate" means the game is over!
Q: Where is Tennis played? (Ans: On a Court)

3. Famous Players 🌟

India and the world have great athletes. Virat Kohli is a famous Cricket star. Lionel Messi is a legend in Football. India's PV Sindhu has won many medals in Badminton.

Sketch of a Cricket Bat and a Football with a Golden Shoe
💡 Tip: Kohli = Cricket, Messi = Football, Sindhu = Badminton!
Q: Which sport does Lionel Messi play? (Ans: Football)

4. World of Music 🎵

Music is made using instruments. They are divided into groups:

  • String: Have wires (Guitar, Sitar, Violin).
  • Wind: You blow air into them (Flute, Trumpet).
  • Percussion: You hit them (Drums, Tabla).
Picture of a Flute, a Guitar, and a Drum
💡 Tip: Wind = Air, String = Wire, Percussion = Beat!
Q: Is a Drum a Wind or Percussion instrument? (Ans: Percussion)
