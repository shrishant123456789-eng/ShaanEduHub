1. Domestic, Wild & Pet Animals

Definition: Animals are divided based on where they live.

  • Wild Animals: Live in forests/jungle naturally. (e.g., Lion, Tiger, Elephant).
  • Domestic Animals: Kept by humans for work or food. (e.g., Cow, Horse, Sheep).
  • Pet Animals: Kept at home for company. (e.g., Dog, Cat, Parrot).
🖼️ Picture: Lion (Jungle) vs Cow (Farm)
💡 Tip: Wild = Free in forest. Domestic = Useful to us.
2. Herbivores, Carnivores & Omnivores

Definition: Grouping animals by what they eat.

  • Herbivores: Eat ONLY plants/grass. (Cow, Deer, Rabbit).
  • Carnivores: Eat ONLY flesh/meat of others. (Lion, Wolf).
  • Omnivores: Eat BOTH plants and meat. (Bear, Crow, Human).
🖼️ Picture: Grass (Leaf) | Meat (Bone) | Both
💡 Tip: 'H'erbivore = 'H'erbs (Plants).
3. Birds: Features & Types

Features: Beak (no teeth), Wings (to fly), Feathers (warmth).

  • Flying Birds: Pigeon, Sparrow, Eagle.
  • Flightless Birds: Cannot fly due to heavy body. (Ostrich, Penguin, Kiwi).
  • Aquatic Birds: Webbed feet to swim. (Duck, Swan).
🖼️ Picture: Ostrich running vs Eagle flying
💡 Tip: Penguins are birds but they swim well!
4. Animal Homes & Shelters

Natural vs Man-made homes:

  • Lion: Den (Natural)
  • Rabbit: Burrow (Natural)
  • Bird: Nest (Built by self)
  • Spider: Web (Built by self)
  • Cow: Shed (Man-made)
  • Horse: Stable (Man-made)
  • Dog: Kennel (Man-made)
🖼️ Picture: A cozy Shed and a Spider Web
5. Sounds & Movements
  • Sounds: Lion (Roars), Dog (Barks), Cat (Meows), Horse (Neighs), Elephant (Trumpets), Snake (Hisses).
  • Movements: Snakes (Slither/Crawl), Kangaroos (Hop), Fish (Swim), Birds (Fly/Walk).
🖼️ Picture: Kangaroo hopping
6. Young Ones of Animals

Babies have special names!

  • Cow → Calf
  • Dog → Puppy
  • Cat → Kitten
  • Lion → Cub
  • Sheep → Lamb
  • Horse → Foal
  • Hen → Chick
  • Duck → Duckling
🖼️ Picture: Mother hen with chicks
7. Eating Habits (How they eat)
  • Chewing the Cud: Cows/Buffaloes swallow food and chew it later.
  • Gnawing: Rabbits/Rats have sharp front teeth to nibble nuts.
  • Swallowing Whole: Snakes/Frogs have no chewing teeth.
  • Lapping: Cats/Dogs drink liquid with tongue.
  • Sucking: Butterflies use a tube (proboscis) for nectar.
🖼️ Picture: Butterfly on a flower
8. Endangered Animals

What does it mean? Animals that are very few in number and might disappear forever.

Examples: Tiger, Giant Panda, Rhinoceros.

Extinct (Gone forever): Dodo, Dinosaurs.

🖼️ Picture: Save the Tiger poster
9. Care & Protection

We must be kind to animals.

  • Give fresh water and food.
  • Take sick pets to a Veterinary Doctor (Vet).
  • Do not tease animals in the zoo.
  • Do not destroy their forest homes.
🖼️ Picture: Child feeding a dog
10. Human-Animal Relationship

Animals help us in many ways:

  • Food: Milk (Cow), Eggs (Hen), Honey (Bee).
  • Clothing: Wool (Sheep), Silk (Silkworm).
  • Work: Donkey/Camel carry loads. Oxen plough fields.
  • Safety: Dogs guard our homes.
🖼️ Picture: Sheep giving wool
