📝 Detailed Study Notes

1. What is a Circle?

A circle is a simple closed curve. It is NOT a polygon because it has no straight sides.

Every point on the circle is at the same distance from a fixed point inside it called the Center.

Center (Red Dot)

2. Parts of a Circle

  • Center (O): The fixed point in the middle.
  • Circumference: The boundary or the length of the circle border.
  • Radius (r): A straight line from the Center to ANY point on the boundary. Plural is Radii.
  • Chord: A straight line joining ANY two points on the circle.
  • Diameter (d): A Chord that passes through the Center. It is the LONGEST chord.

3. The Magic Formula (Radius & Diameter)

The Diameter is basically two Radii put together in a straight line.

Diameter = 2 × Radius

Radius = Diameter ÷ 2

Example: If Radius is 5cm, Diameter is 10cm.

4. Drawing a Circle

We use a tool called a Compass.

  1. Fix a sharp pencil in the compass.
  2. Measure the radius on a ruler (stretch the compass arms).
  3. Place the metal tip on paper (This becomes the Center).
  4. Rotate the pencil arm around to draw the circle.
💡 Tip: Don't move the metal tip while drawing, or your circle will be wobbly!

5. Advanced Terms

  • Arc: Any part of the circumference.
  • Semicircle: Exactly half of a circle (divided by diameter).
  • Interior: The space inside the boundary.
  • Exterior: The space outside the boundary.

6. Concentric Circles

Circles that have the SAME Center but DIFFERENT Radii.

Target Board / Archery Board pattern. (Small circle inside a big circle).

Examples: Ripples in water, Target board.

🧠 Advanced Word Problems (Thinking Time)

Problem 1: The Bicycle Wheel

The spokes of a bicycle wheel connect the center axle to the rim. If a spoke is 30 cm long, what is the Diameter of the wheel? (Assuming spokes go from center to edge)

Ans: 60 cm.

The spoke acts as the Radius (Center to Edge).
Diameter = 2 × Radius.
Diameter = 2 × 30 = 60 cm.

Problem 2: The Pizza Cut

Rahul cuts a circular pizza into two equal halves by cutting straight through the center. What is this line of cut called geometrically?

Ans: Diameter.

A straight line passing through the center connecting two points on the boundary is the Diameter.

Problem 3: The Garden Fence

A circular garden has a diameter of 10 meters. The gardener wants to put a fence along the RADIUS only (from center to edge) to divide flowers. How long is the fence?

Ans: 5 meters.

Radius = Diameter ÷ 2.
Radius = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 meters.

Problem 4: Concentric Tracks

Two running tracks are circular. They share the same center point. The inner track has a radius of 50m. The outer track has a radius of 60m. Are these Concentric Circles?

Ans: Yes.

Circles with the same center but different radii are called Concentric Circles.

Problem 5: The Longest Stick

You have a circular swimming pool with a radius of 4 meters. What is the longest straight stick you can float on the water without it touching the edges (or just touching)?

Ans: 8 meters.

The longest straight line inside a circle is the Diameter.
D = 2 × 4 = 8 meters.

Problem 6: Clock Hands

Imagine the second hand of a clock is the radius. If the tip of the second hand touches the edge of the clock face, and the hand is 7 cm long, how wide is the clock face?

Ans: 14 cm wide.

The "width" of a circle is its Diameter.
Radius = 7 cm.
Diameter = 14 cm.

Problem 7: The Bangle Test

Riya wants to draw a circle but lost her compass. She uses a bangle. Can she easily find the exact center point just by tracing? Why or why not?

Ans: No, not easily.

Tracing a bangle gives the boundary (Circumference), but it doesn't mark the Center. A compass leaves a pinhole at the center.

Problem 8: Chord vs Diameter

Draw a circle. Draw a line connecting two points on the boundary NOT passing through the center. Draw another passing THROUGH the center. Which one is longer?

Ans: The one passing through the center (Diameter).

The Diameter is the longest chord of the circle.

Problem 9: The Goat Tether

A goat is tied to a pole with a rope of length 3 meters. If the goat walks keeping the rope tight, what shape does it trace? What is the radius?

Ans: Circle. Radius is 3m.

The pole is the center. The rope is the radius. The path is the circumference.

Problem 10: Radius Math

Circle A has a radius of 10cm. Circle B has a diameter of 10cm. Which circle is bigger?

Ans: Circle A is bigger.

Circle A: Radius = 10cm.
Circle B: Diameter = 10cm -> Radius = 5cm.
10cm > 5cm.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Center: The middle point.
  • Radius: Center to Edge.
  • Diameter: Edge to Edge through Center.
  • Chord: Edge to Edge (anywhere).
  • Diameter = 2 × Radius.
  • Radius = Diameter ÷ 2.
  • Concentric Circles: Same center, different sizes.
  • Circumference: The boundary line.
