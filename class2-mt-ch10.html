📘 Nature Notes

1. Loving Plants & Trees 🌳💚

Trees are our best friends. They give us:
🌟 Fresh Air (Oxygen) to breathe.
🌟 Cool Shade when it is hot.
🌟 Yummy Fruits like mangoes and apples.
We must love and protect them. Never pluck flowers unnecessarily.

❓ Activity: What is the name of your favorite tree?

2. Watering Plants 💧🌱

Plants get thirsty just like us!
✅ Give water to plants every morning.
✅ Watch them grow big and strong.
If we don't water them, they will dry up and feel sad. Let's make our garden happy!

3. Not Harming Animals 🐶🚫

Animals have feelings. They feel pain if we hurt them.
❌ Do not throw stones at dogs or cats.
❌ Do not pull their tails.
✅ Be gentle. You can pet them softly if they are friendly.
Animals are God's beautiful creation.

4. Feeding Birds 🐦🍚

Birds fly all day and get hungry.
✅ Put some grains (rice/seeds) on your roof or balcony.
✅ Keep a bowl of fresh water for them to drink.
Seeing birds eat happily will make you smile! 😊

5. Saving Trees 🌳❌🪓

Paper is made from trees.
🚫 Do not waste paper. Write on both sides.
🚫 Do not cut trees.
If we save paper, we save trees. Trees are homes for birds and squirrels. 🐿️

6. Saving Water 💧🚰

Water is very precious. We cannot live without it.
✅ Close the tap while brushing your teeth.
✅ Repair leaking taps.
✅ Use a bucket to bathe instead of a shower.
Every drop counts!

7. Keeping Environment Clean 🧹🌍

Our Earth is our home.
✅ Throw wrappers in the dustbin.
✅ Do not spit on the road.
A clean environment keeps diseases away and makes nature look beautiful.

8. Reduce Waste Habit ♻️🗑️

Reduce Waste means creating less garbage.
✅ Use cloth bags instead of plastic bags.
✅ Use a reusable water bottle.
Plastic is bad for nature. Let's say NO to plastic!
