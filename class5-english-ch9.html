Quick Revision ⚡

  • Formal letters are for official work; Informal for family.
  • Emails must have a clear 'Subject' line.
  • Notices are always put inside a box.
  • Messages are short and include Date, Time, and Name.
  • Tone in formal writing must be polite and direct.
  • Date format: 12 October 2023 (not 12/10/23 in formal).
  • Salutations: 'Respected Sir/Madam' for Formal, 'Dear' for Informal.
