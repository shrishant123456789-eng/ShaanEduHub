Quick Revision ⚡

  • Always greet politely based on the time of day.
  • Use "Could you..." or "May I..." for requests.
  • When introducing, say "Hello, I am..." or "Meet my friend...".
  • Give reasons for your opinions: "I think... because...".
  • Speak clearly and use pauses; don't rush.
  • Watch out for silent letters (e.g., 'K' in Knife).
  • Maintain eye contact (in real life) and smile!
