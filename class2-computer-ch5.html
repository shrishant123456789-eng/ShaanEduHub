⌨️ What is a Keyboard?

A keyboard is like a magic slate! It is an Input Device.
It has many small buttons called Keys.
We use it to write letters, numbers, and words on the computer.
Example: "Riya types her name using the keyboard." 😊

🔤 Alphabet Keys

These keys have letters from A to Z.
We use them to type words and sentences.
"Cat", "Dog", "My Name" - all are typed using Alphabet keys!

🔢 Number Keys

These keys show numbers from 0 to 9.
We use them to type our age, phone number, or do math sums.
Can you find the number 7 on your keyboard?

✨ Special Keys

Spacebar: The longest key! It gives a space between words.
(Hello[space]World)

Enter Key: ↵ Moves the cursor to the next line. Like pressing "Go"!

Backspace: ← Erases mistakes from the left side. It works like an eraser.

Caps Lock: Helps us type BIG (Capital) letters like ABC.

⬆️⬇️ Arrow Keys

There are 4 arrow keys.
⬆️ Up Arrow: Moves cursor up.
⬇️ Down Arrow: Moves cursor down.
⬅️ Left Arrow: Moves cursor left.
➡️ Right Arrow: Moves cursor right.

🧘 Correct Posture

• Sit straight on your chair.
• Keep your feet flat on the floor.
• Keep your hands soft on the keyboard.
• Don't hit the keys hard! Be gentle. 🌸
