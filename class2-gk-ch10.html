📘 Fun & Play Notes

1. Types of Games 🎲🏏

Playing games makes us happy and strong!
Indoor Games: Played inside the house.

  • Examples: Ludo 🎲, Chess ♟️, Carrom, Snakes & Ladders.
Outdoor Games: Played outside in the park or field.
  • Examples: Cricket 🏏, Football ⚽, Hockey 🏑, Tennis 🎾.

2. Sports & Equipment 🏆

National Game: Hockey 🏑 is the National Game of India.
Sports Persons: People who play sports for the country are called sports persons.
Equipment: Things we need to play.

  • Cricket needs a Bat and Ball.
  • Badminton needs a Racket and Shuttlecock.
  • Football needs a big Ball.

3. Fitness & Health 💪

Why should we play?

  • Games keep our body fit and strong.
  • Running and swimming are great exercises. 🏊‍♂️
  • Yoga: Helps us stay calm and flexible. 🧘‍♀️
  • Playing together teaches us to be good friends (Teamwork).

4. Music & Dance 🎸💃

Music and Dance are great fun!
Music Instruments:

  • Guitar: Has strings. 🎸
  • Piano: Has black and white keys. 🎹
  • Flute: We blow air into it. 🎶
  • Drum: We beat it with sticks. 🥁
Dance: Moving our body to music. India has many dance forms like Kathak and Bharatanatyam.

5. TV & Entertainment 📺

Television (TV) shows us cartoons, movies, and news.

  • We can learn new things from TV.
  • Good Habit: Do not watch TV for too long. It can hurt your eyes. 👀
  • Play outside more than watching TV!
