📝 My Smart Notes
1. Important National Days 🇮🇳

Definition: Special days celebrated by everyone in our country India.

Examples: Independence Day (15th August), Republic Day (26th January).

[Image: Indian Flag flying high on Red Fort]

Indian Context: On Republic Day, we see a grand parade on TV from New Delhi.

💡 Tip: Republic Day = R = Rajpath (Parade). Independence Day = I = India Free.
❓ Check: When is Gandhi Jayanti? (Ans: 2nd Oct)
2. International Days 🌍

Definition: Days celebrated by many countries around the world together.

Examples: Earth Day (22 April), Environment Day (5 June).

[Image: Kids planting a tree for Earth Day]

Indian Context: Schools in India celebrate Yoga Day (21 June) with morning exercises.

💡 Tip: Enviornment has 'E' (5th letter) -> 5th June!
❓ Check: Why do we celebrate Earth Day? (Ans: To save our planet)
3. Current Events 📰

Definition: Important things happening right now in India and the world.

Explanation: New discoveries, sports wins, or new leaders.

[Image: Drawing of Chandrayaan-3 Rocket]

Indian Context: Chandrayaan-3 landing on the moon made India very proud!

💡 Tip: Read newspaper headlines daily!
❓ Check: Which rover went to the moon? (Ans: Pragyan)
4. Great Discoveries 🔬

Definition: Finding something new that helps humans.

Examples: The Wheel (transport), Fire (cooking), Computer (work).

[Image: An old wheel vs a modern car tyre]

Indian Context: Zero (0) was introduced to the world by Aryabhata from India.

💡 Tip: Wheel makes the world 'roll' faster.
❓ Check: Who invented the bulb? (Ans: Thomas Edison)
5. Logical Reasoning 🧠

Definition: Using your brain to find the correct answer, not just memory.

Example: If A=1, B=2, then C=? (Ans: 3)

[Image: A pattern of Shapes: Circle, Square, Circle, Square...]

Indian Context: Like solving puzzles in the Sunday newspaper.

💡 Tip: Look for the pattern or the rule hidden in the question.
❓ Check: 2, 4, 6, __? (Ans: 8)
6. Odd One Out 🍎

Definition: Finding the item that is different from the rest of the group.

Example: Apple, Banana, Potato, Mango. (Potato is a vegetable, others are fruits).

[Image: 3 Balls and 1 Bat]

Indian Context: Like finding a Cricket ball in a basket of Tennis balls.

💡 Tip: Find what the other 3 have in common first.
❓ Check: Car, Bus, Truck, Ship? (Ans: Ship - goes on water)
7. Classification 📂

Definition: Grouping things based on their features.

Example: Herbivores (eat plants) vs Carnivores (eat meat).

[Image: Group of animals split into two sides]

Indian Context: Sorting coins into ₹1, ₹2, and ₹5 piles.

💡 Tip: Ask "Which family does this belong to?"
❓ Check: Is a table Living or Non-Living? (Ans: Non-Living)
8. Sequencing 🔢

Definition: Putting things in the correct order of time or size.

Example: Seed → Plant → Tree.

[Image: Baby -> Boy -> Man -> Old Man]

Indian Context: Morning Assembly → Classes → Lunch Break → Home.

💡 Tip: Think "What happens first?"
❓ Check: Monday, Tuesday, ____? (Ans: Wednesday)
9. Analogy ⚖️

Definition: Finding a relationship pair similar to another pair.

Example: Foot is to Shoe as Hand is to Glove.

[Image: Doctor:Stethoscope :: Painter:Brush]

Indian Context: Bat is to Cricket as Racket is to Badminton.

💡 Tip: Make a sentence. "Shoe covers Foot, so Glove covers Hand."
❓ Check: Bird:Fly :: Fish:____? (Ans: Swim)
10. Fun GK & Riddles 🧩

Definition: Solving puzzles and identifying famous logos or pictures.

Example: I have a face but no eyes, hands but no arms. What am I? (A Clock).

[Image: Olympic Rings Logo]

Indian Context: The 'Swachh Bharat' logo is Gandhi Ji's glasses.

💡 Tip: Read the riddle slowly, the answer is often simple!
❓ Check: Symbol with 4 rings? (Ans: Audi Car)
