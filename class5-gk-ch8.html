📖 Study Notes

1. National & International Sports
🏏 ⚽ 🏑

Sports played within a country are National Sports (e.g., Kabaddi in India). Sports played between countries are International Sports (e.g., Cricket World Cup).

Fact: Field Hockey is widely considered India's National Game because we won 8 Olympic Gold medals in it!

Check: Cricket originated in England but is loved in India.

2. Famous Sportspersons
🥇 🏃

India: Sachin Tendulkar (Cricket), PV Sindhu (Badminton), Neeraj Chopra (Javelin), Mary Kom (Boxing).

World: Lionel Messi (Football), Usain Bolt (Running), Roger Federer (Tennis).

Memory Tip: Neeraj Chopra threw the Javelin like a rocket to win Gold! 🚀
3. Sports Awards & Trophies
🏆 🎖️
  • Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna: Highest sports honour in India.
  • Arjuna Award: For outstanding performance.
  • Dronacharya Award: For coaches (Gurus).
Trophy Examples: Ranji Trophy (Cricket), Durand Cup (Football).
4. Olympic Games Awareness
🔵🟡⚫🟢🔴

Held every 4 years. The logo has 5 rings representing 5 continents united by sports.

Motto: Citius, Altius, Fortius (Faster, Higher, Stronger).
5. Sports Terminology
🗣️ 📋
  • LBW: Leg Before Wicket (Cricket).
  • Checkmate: Winning move in Chess.
  • Deuce: A tie score in Tennis/Badminton.
  • Hattrick: 3 successes in a row.
6. Records & Achievements
📊 📈

Records are benchmarks set by athletes. Usain Bolt is the fastest man (100m in 9.58s).

India: First Individual Olympic Gold - Abhinav Bindra (Shooting, 2008).
7. Yoga & Traditional Games
🧘 🛖

Yoga: Ancient Indian practice for mind and body. "Asanas" are poses. International Yoga Day: June 21.

Traditional Games: Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, Gilli-Danda, Chess (Chaturanga).

8. Sportsmanship & Teamwork
🤝 ❤️

Sportsmanship: Playing fair, respecting opponents, and accepting defeat with a smile.

Tip: You win some, you lose some. The fun is in playing!
