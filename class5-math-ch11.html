📘 Class Notes: Money Matters

1. Indian Currency System 🇮🇳

The currency of India is the Indian Rupee. The symbol is ₹.

[Image: Notes of ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹500, ₹2000 and coins]
Basics:
• 1 Rupee = 100 Paise
• We write 50 rupees and 25 paise as ₹50.25.

2. Conversion of Money 🔄

To change Rupees to Paise, multiply by 100. To change Paise to Rupees, divide by 100.

[Image: Diagram showing x100 and ÷100 flow]
💡 Tip: Simply add two zeros to convert ₹ to P. Remove two decimals to convert P to ₹.
₹5 = 5 × 100 = 500 paise.
750 paise = 750 ÷ 100 = ₹7.50

3. Addition & Subtraction ➕➖

Always align the dots (decimals) in a straight line.

[Image: Column addition of money with dots aligned]
Add ₹25.50 and ₹10.25:
  25.50
+ 10.25
_______
  ₹35.75

4. Multiplication & Division ✖️➗

Used to find total cost of many items (Multiply) or cost of one item (Divide).

[Image: 1 Chocolate = ₹10, 5 Chocolates = ?]

Unitary Method: Find value of 1 first, then find value of many.

5. Bills & Invoices 🧾

A bill shows the Item, Rate (Price per item), Quantity, and Amount.

[Image: A sample shop bill table]
Amount = Rate × Quantity.
Total = Sum of all amounts.

6. Profit & Loss 📈

Cost Price (CP): Price at which you buy.
Selling Price (SP): Price at which you sell.

[Image: Scales weighing CP and SP]
• SP > CP = Profit 😊 (Profit = SP - CP)
• CP > SP = Loss 😞 (Loss = CP - SP)

7. Simple Budgeting 📝

Budgeting is planning how to spend your money. Income must be greater than Expenses.

[Image: Pie chart of Allowance: Snacks, Books, Saving]

8. Savings & Spending 🐷

Needs: Things you must have (Food).
Wants: Things you wish for (Toys).

[Image: Piggy bank vs Toy store]

Saving helps in emergencies.

9. Banking Awareness 🏦

Bank: A safe place for money.
Account: Your personal folder in a bank.
ATM: Machine to take cash out.

[Image: Bank building and ATM card]

10. Advanced Word Problems 🧠

Read the problem twice. Identify if you need +, -, ×, or ÷.

[Image: Thinking kid with math symbols]
"Rohan bought a pen for ₹10 and sold it for ₹12. Find profit."
CP=10, SP=12. SP > CP.
Profit = 12 - 10 = ₹2.
