My Measurement Notes 📝

1. Standard Units of Measurement

Measurement helps us find out how long, how heavy, or how much a container holds.

  • Length: Measures distance or height.
  • Weight (Mass): Measures how heavy an object is.
  • Capacity: Measures how much liquid a container holds.
[Image: A ruler, a weighing scale, and a measuring jug]
Standard Units:
• Length: Meter (m)
• Weight: Gram (g)
• Capacity: Liter (l)
💡 Tip: 'Kilo' is the biggest, 'Milli' is the smallest in our daily usage!
❓ What unit would you use to measure a pencil? (cm)
2. Units of Length in Detail

Let's understand the family of Length.

  • Kilometer (km): For long distances (e.g., Delhi to Mumbai).
  • Meter (m): Standard unit (e.g., Length of a saree, height of a room).
  • Centimeter (cm): Small lengths (e.g., Pencil, Notebook).
  • Millimeter (mm): Very tiny lengths (e.g., Thickness of a coin).
Relationship:
1 km = 1000 m
1 m = 100 cm
1 cm = 10 mm
💡 Remember: King Henry Died Mother Didn't Cry Much (km, hm, dam, m, dm, cm, mm).
❓ How many meters in 2 km? (2000 m)
3. Measuring with a Ruler

To measure accurately, we use a ruler (scale).

  • Step 1: Place the '0' mark of the ruler at one end of the object.
  • Step 2: Look at the other end. The number it touches is the length in cm.
  • Step 3: Count the small lines after the number for millimeters.
[Image: A pencil placed on a ruler starting at 0 and ending at 5.5]
If an eraser ends at 3 big marks and 4 small lines, it is 3 cm 4 mm or 3.4 cm.
💡 Error Alert: Never start measuring from the edge of the ruler. Always start from the '0' line!
❓ If a line ends at 5 small lines after 2 cm, length is? (2.5 cm)
4. Weight and Capacity

Just like length, weight and capacity have their own families.

  • Weight: Kilogram (kg) for heavy things (Rice bag), Gram (g) for light things (Gold, Spices).
  • Capacity: Liter (l) for large amounts (Bucket of water), Milliliter (ml) for small amounts (Medicine spoon).
Relationships:
1 kg = 1000 g
1 l = 1000 ml
💡 Tip: Think of a 1-liter water bottle. 1000 tiny drops (ml) make that bottle full!
❓ 5 kg potatoes = ? grams (5000 g)
5. Conversion: Big to Small

When going from a larger unit to a smaller unit, we MULTIPLY.

  • km → m: Multiply by 1000
  • m → cm: Multiply by 100
  • kg → g: Multiply by 1000
  • l → ml: Multiply by 1000
Example: Convert 5 m to cm.
Since 1 m = 100 cm,
5 m = 5 × 100 = 500 cm.
💡 Tip: Big to Small? Multiply All!
❓ 3 l to ml? (3000 ml)
6. Conversion: Small to Big

When going from a smaller unit to a larger unit, we DIVIDE.

  • m → km: Divide by 1000
  • cm → m: Divide by 100
  • g → kg: Divide by 1000
  • ml → l: Divide by 1000
Example: Convert 4000 g to kg.
Since 1000 g = 1 kg,
4000 ÷ 1000 = 4 kg.
💡 Tip: Small to Big? Divide is the gig!
❓ 500 cm to m? (5 m)
7. Operations with Measures

We add and subtract measures just like normal numbers, but we must align the units correctly.

  • Step 1: Make columns for units (e.g., m and cm).
  • Step 2: Write numbers in correct columns.
  • Step 3: Add or subtract starting from the smaller unit.
  • Step 4: Carry over or borrow if needed.
Add: 12m 45cm + 5m 60cm
cm: 45 + 60 = 105 (105 cm = 1 m 05 cm)
m: 12 + 5 + 1 (carry) = 18 m
Ans: 18 m 05 cm
💡 Check: Always check if the smaller unit exceeds its limit (like cm > 100) and convert it!
❓ 2kg + 500g = ? (2kg 500g or 2.5kg)
8. Estimation & Word Problems

Sometimes we don't need exact numbers. We estimate or round off.

  • Estimation: Is the door about 2 meters or 20 meters? (2 meters).
  • Rounding: 18 cm is close to 20 cm. 102 km is close to 100 km.
Word Problem Logic:
• "Total length" -> Addition.
• "How much left?" -> Subtraction.
• "Cut into pieces" -> Division.
💡 Tip: Read the unit in the question carefully. If one is in 'm' and other in 'km', convert first!
❓ Estimate length of a bus: 10m or 10cm? (10m)

Important Questions 🌟

1. What is the standard unit of length?
Answer: Meter (m)
It is the base unit from which km and cm are derived.
2. Which unit is used to measure distance between two cities?
Answer: Kilometer (km)
Meters are too small for large distances.
3. How many centimeters make 1 meter?
Answer: 100 cm
Centi means 1/100.
4. Convert 5 kg into grams.
Answer: 5000 g
1 kg = 1000 g, so 5 × 1000 = 5000.
5. Which is greater: 1500 m or 1 km?
Answer: 1500 m
1 km = 1000 m, so 1500 m > 1000 m.
6. A spoon holds about 5 ___ of syrup. (Unit?)
Answer: ml (milliliters)
Small capacities are measured in ml.
7. Convert 7000 ml to liters.
Answer: 7 l
7000 ÷ 1000 = 7.
8. Add: 2m 40cm + 1m 20cm.
Answer: 3m 60cm
Add meters with meters, cm with cm.
9. How many grams in half a kilogram?
Answer: 500 g
1000 ÷ 2 = 500.
10. Measurement of liquid is called?
Answer: Capacity
Capacity refers to volume of liquid.
11. The thickness of a coin is measured in?
Answer: Millimeters (mm)
It is very small, smaller than a cm.
12. 12 km = _____ m.
Answer: 12000 m
Multiply by 1000.
13. Convert 350 cm to meters.
Answer: 3.5 m or 3m 50cm
Divide by 100.
14. What instrument measures weight?
Answer: Weighing balance / Scale
It compares mass.
15. Subtract: 10 kg - 2 kg 500 g.
Answer: 7 kg 500 g
Regroup 10kg to 9kg 1000g, then subtract.
16. What is the short form of Milliliter?
Answer: ml
Milli + Liter.
17. Is a cricket pitch measured in kg or m?
Answer: m (meters)
Pitch is a length.
18. 8 m 5 cm is equal to ___ cm.
Answer: 805 cm
800 cm + 5 cm.
19. How many mm in 10 cm?
Answer: 100 mm
10 × 10 = 100.
20. The standard unit of mass is?
Answer: Gram (g) / Kilogram (kg)
In metric system, gram is base, kg is standard SI.
21. Round 48 cm to nearest 10.
Answer: 50 cm
48 is closer to 50 than 40.
22. To convert m to cm, we move decimal point to?
Answer: Right by 2 places
Multiplication by 100.
23. 1000 liters = 1 _____?
Answer: Kiloliter (kl)
Though rarely used in daily life, kilo = 1000.
24. 3/4 kg is equal to?
Answer: 750 g
0.75 × 1000.
25. A car tank holds 30 ___ of petrol.
Answer: liters
Capacity of tank.
26. 2500 g = ___ kg ___ g.
Answer: 2 kg 500 g
Divide by 1000, quotient is kg, remainder is g.
27. Length of a saree is usually?
Answer: 5 to 6 meters
Estimation logic.
28. 50 mm + 5 cm = ___ cm.
Answer: 10 cm
50 mm = 5 cm. 5 + 5 = 10.
29. Which is heavier: 1 kg cotton or 1 kg iron?
Answer: Both are equal
Weight is same (1 kg), only volume differs.
30. 9 m - 50 cm = ?
Answer: 8 m 50 cm
900 cm - 50 cm = 850 cm.
