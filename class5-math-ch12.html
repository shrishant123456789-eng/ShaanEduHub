📘 Notebook: Concepts & Tricks

1. Mental Calculation Strategies 🧠

Basic: Break numbers to make them easy. E.g., 24 + 26 becomes (20+20) + (4+6) = 40 + 10 = 50.

Advanced: Compensation method. To do 48 + 35, think 50 + 35 = 85, then subtract 2 = 83.

[IMAGE: Numbers splitting diagram]
💡 Tip: Look for "Friendly Numbers" that end in 0 or 5!

2. Estimation Tricks 📊

Rounding helps us guess the answer quickly.

Example: 198 + 304 is approx 200 + 300 = 500.

[IMAGE: Number line showing rounding]
💡 Tip: 5 or more? Round Up. 4 or less? Round Down.

3. Fast Calculation Techniques ⚡

Multiplying by 10, 100, 1000 is easy. Just add zeros!

To multiply by 5: Multiply by 10 and then halve it. E.g., 12 x 5 -> 120 / 2 = 60.

[IMAGE: Speed math symbols x10 x100]

4. Multi-step Word Problems 📝

Real life isn't just one step. RPSC Method:

  • Read carefully.
  • Plan (Add? Subtract?).
  • Solve.
  • Check your answer.
