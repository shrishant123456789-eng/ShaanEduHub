Class Notes & Concepts
1. Main Verbs & Helping Verbs

A Verb is a word that shows action or state of being.

🏃‍♂️ ➕ 🆘
  • Main Verb: Shows the actual action. (Play, Eat, Sleep)
  • Helping Verb: Helps the main verb show time. (Is, Am, Are, Was, Were, Has, Have)
Example: Rahul is (Helping) playing (Main).
💡 Helping verbs are like assistants; they support the Boss (Main Verb)!
2. Action vs State Verbs

Not all verbs show movement.

🤔 vs ⛹️
  • Action Verb: Physical activity (Run, Jump, Dance).
  • State Verb: Feeling, thought, or possession (Know, Love, Have, Own).
I run fast (Action).
I know him (State).
3. The Three Tenses

Tense tells us the TIME of action.

🕒
  • Present: Happening now or habit. (I eat.)
  • Past: Finished action. (I ate.)
  • Future: Will happen later. (I will eat.)

Continuous Tense: Shows ongoing action (Ing form).

I am eating (Present Cont.) | I was eating (Past Cont.)
4. Spoken English Usage

Using correct tenses in daily life.

🗣️
  • For Habits: Use Simple Present. "I wake up at 6 AM."
  • For Completed Tasks: Use Simple Past. "I finished my homework."
💡 Don't say "I am going to school everyday." Say "I go to school everyday."

6. Subject-Verb Agreement

The verb must agree with the subject.

🤝
  • Singular Subject: Verb + s/es. (He plays.)
  • Plural Subject (or I/You): Base Verb. (They play. I play.)
Wrong: The boys plays.
Correct: The boys play.
7. Listening to Stories

Identify tenses in stories.

👂

"Once upon a time, a lion lived (Past) in a jungle. He was (Past) very strong."

8. Error Detection

Fixing spoken sentences.

❌ ➡️ ✅
Wrong: She do not know.
Correct: She does not know. (She is singular).

Quick Revision

  • Present Simple: Habits (He walks).
  • Past Simple: Finished (He walked).
  • Future Simple: Prediction (He will walk).
