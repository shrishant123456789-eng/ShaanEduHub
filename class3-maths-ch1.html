Chapter 1: Numbers & Number Sense

1. Welcome to the World of Numbers! 🌍

Hello, Math Explorer! 🕵️‍♂️ Today we will learn about big numbers. You already know numbers up to 999. Now let's jump higher!

When we add 1 to 999, we get 1000 (One Thousand). 🏆

Smallest 4-digit number = 1000
Largest 4-digit number = 9999

2. Place Value House 🏠

Numbers live in a house called the Place Value Chart. Let's look at the number 5,432.

  • Th (Thousands): 5000
  • H (Hundreds): 400
  • T (Tens): 30
  • O (Ones): 2

Face Value: The face value is just the digit itself! (Face value of 5 is just 5).

3. Expanded Form 💥

Let's stretch the number like a rubber band!

3,526 = 3000 + 500 + 20 + 6

We write it as: Three thousand five hundred twenty-six.

4. Comparing Numbers 🐊

The hungry crocodile always eats the bigger number! 🐊

  • > Greater Than (Big Mouth opens left)
  • < Less Than (Big Mouth opens right)
  • = Equal To (Same numbers)

Example: 4500 > 3200

5. Skip Counting & Patterns 🚀

Jumping numbers is fun!

  • Skip by 2s: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10...
  • Skip by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25...
  • Skip by 10s: 10, 20, 30, 40...

6. Odd & Even Numbers 🍎🍏

Even Numbers: Can be paired up. End with 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. (Like a pair of shoes 👟👟)

Odd Numbers: Cannot be paired completely. End with 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. (One is left alone 👟)

7. Rounding Off 🎯

Making numbers simple!

  • Nearest 10: If unit digit is 0-4, go down. If 5-9, go up!
    Ex: 73 → 70, 78 → 80
  • Nearest 100: Look at tens place.
    Ex: 340 → 300, 360 → 400
