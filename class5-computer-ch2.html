Chapter 2: CPU – Deep Study

📘 My Class Notes

1. Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU)

[ DIAGRAM: A cartoon brain doing math ]
Definition: The part of the CPU that does all the calculations and makes decisions.
Basic: It's the calculator inside the computer.
Advanced: It has two parts - Arithmetic (Maths: +, -, *, /) and Logic (Decisions: Is A > B? Yes/No).
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Like the shopkeeper at the Kirana store who quickly totals your bill (Maths) and decides if he has enough change (Logic).
🧠 Tip: ALU = "All Logic Used" or "Arithmetic & Logic Unit".

2. Control Unit (CU)

[ DIAGRAM: A traffic police officer ]
Definition: The manager of the CPU that directs the flow of data.
Basic: It tells other parts what to do.
Advanced: It fetches instructions from memory, decodes them, and tells the ALU or Memory what action to take. It does not calculate; it commands.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Like the Traffic Police Havaldar at a busy chowk. He doesn't drive the cars, but he tells them when to stop and when to go.
🧠 Tip: CU = "Commander Unit".

3. Registers

[ DIAGRAM: Sticky notes near CPU ]
Definition: Tiny, super-fast memory locations inside the CPU.
Basic: Scratchpad for the CPU.
Advanced: They hold data temporarily that the CPU is working on *right now*. They are much faster than RAM but store very little data.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Holding money in your hand (Register) vs keeping it in the bank (Hard Disk). Hand is faster!

4. Role of CPU in Multitasking

[ DIAGRAM: Juggler juggling balls ]
Concept: CPU switches between tasks so fast it looks like they are happening at the same time.
How: It spends a tiny fraction of a second on Music, then Game, then Download, then back to Music.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Like your mother cooking roti, checking your homework, and talking on the phone. She switches attention quickly!

5. Clock Speed

[ DIAGRAM: A clock ticking fast ]
Definition: The speed at which a CPU works. Measured in Hertz (Hz) or Gigahertz (GHz).
Higher GHz = Faster Computer. 3.0 GHz means 3 billion cycles per second!

6. CPU vs Processor

Usually used for the same thing.
Strictly: CPU is the logical unit (brain function). Processor is the physical chip (hardware).
Note: In Class 5, we treat them as the same!

7. Importance of CPU

The CPU determines the Performance of the computer. A weak CPU makes a slow computer, no matter how much RAM you have.
