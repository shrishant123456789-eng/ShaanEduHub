Handwritten Notes

1. Modes of Transport

Transport means moving people or goods from one place to another. We use different vehicles based on where we travel!

  • Land Transport: Roads and Tracks (Car, Cycle, Train).
  • Water Transport: Rivers and Oceans (Boat, Ship).
  • Air Transport: The fastest way! (Aeroplane, Helicopter).
[Image: A collage of Car, Boat, and Plane]
💡 Tip: L.A.W. stands for Land, Air, and Water!
❓ Q: Which is the fastest mode? (Ans: Air)

2. Special Vehicles

Some vehicles have special jobs. They help us in emergencies or take us to unique places.

  • Ambulance: Carries sick people to the hospital.
  • Fire Engine: Red truck that puts out fires.
  • Submarine: Travels underwater!
  • Hot Air Balloon: Used for fun and sight-seeing in the air.
[Image: Red Fire Engine with Ladder]
💡 Tip: Red means help! Look for red on fire engines and ambulance lights.
❓ Q: Which vehicle goes under the sea? (Ans: Submarine)

3. Communication

Communication is sharing information, ideas, or feelings with others.

  • Letters: The old way of sending messages through the post office.
  • Mobile Phones: Instant talking and texting anywhere.
  • Email: Letters sent through the internet.
  • Satellite: Helps in TV signals and long-distance calls.
[Image: A Smartphone and an Envelope]
💡 Tip: "Communicate" means to "connect"!
❓ Q: How do we send a digital letter? (Ans: Email)

4. Road Safety

Safety is important so we don't get hurt. Always follow the rules on the road!

  • Traffic Lights: Red (Stop), Yellow (Wait), Green (Go).
  • Zebra Crossing: White stripes on road for walking across.
  • Footpath: Always walk here, not in the middle of the road.
[Image: Traffic Light with 3 colors]
💡 Tip: Walk like a Zebra only on the stripes!
❓ Q: What does yellow light mean? (Ans: Wait/Get Ready)
