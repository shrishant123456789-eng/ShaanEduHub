1. The 3 Magic Words ✨

Good children use three magic words. They work like magic! 🪄

  • Please 🙏: Say 'Please' when you want something.
    Example: "Please give me water."
  • Sorry 😔: Say 'Sorry' when you make a mistake.
    Example: "I am sorry I broke the toy."
  • Thank You 🤗: Say 'Thank You' when someone helps you.
    Example: "Thank you for the chocolate."

❓ Activity: If your friend gives you a pencil, what will you say?

2. Greeting Elders (Namaste) 🙏🌅

When we meet elders (parents, teachers, grandparents), we must greet them.
☀️ In the morning, say "Good Morning".
🌙 At night, say "Good Night".
🙏 Or simply say "Namaste" with folded hands.
This shows respect (izzat).

3. Using Polite Words 😊🗣️

Always speak softly and sweetly.
❌ Rude: "Give me that book!" (Bad manner)
✅ Polite: "May I have that book, please?" (Good manner)
Never shout or use bad words. Polite children are loved by everyone! ❤️

4. Listening to Others 👂

When someone is speaking, we must LISTEN carefully.
🚫 Do not talk in between.
Wait for them to finish.
If your teacher is speaking, look at her and listen. This is a very good habit.

5. Waiting for Your Turn ⏳

Do not push or rush!
🏃‍♂️ On the swing? Wait for your turn.
🚌 Boarding the bus? Stand in a line (queue).
💧 Drinking water? Wait behind your friend.
Waiting shows patience (sabra).

6. Respecting Rules 📜

Rules keep us safe and happy.
School Rules: Come on time, wear clean uniform.
Home Rules: Keep toys back after playing.
Good children always follow the rules.

7. Table Manners 🍽️

How to eat food nicely?

  1. Wash hands before and after eating. 🧼
  2. Chew food with your mouth closed. 🤐
  3. Do not talk while eating.
  4. Do not waste food. Take only what you can eat. 🍛

8. Classroom Manners 🎒


🙋‍♂️ Raise your hand if you want to speak.
🗑️ Throw waste paper in the dustbin. Keep class clean.
🤫 Keep quiet when the teacher is teaching.
🤝 Share things with friends.
