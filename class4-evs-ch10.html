1. Forms of Energy

What is Energy?

Energy is the ability to do work. We need energy to run, play, cook, and move things.
[Image: A glowing sun, a burning fire, and a ringing bell]

There are different forms of energy:

  • Light Energy: Helps us to see things. (Source: Sun, Bulb)
  • Heat Energy: Makes things warm. (Source: Fire, Sun)
  • Sound Energy: Produced when things vibrate. (Source: Bell, Drums)
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Sunlight dries our clothes (Heat). The school bell rings (Sound).
💡 Tip: LHS = Light, Heat, Sound!

Quick Check: Which energy helps us read a book? (Answer: Light)

2. Sources of Energy

Where does energy come from?

Anything that gives us energy is a source of energy. The Sun is the main source of energy on Earth.
[Image: Sun, Windmill, and a Coal heap]
  • Renewable Sources: Never finish. Nature gives them back. (Sun, Wind, Water)
  • Non-Renewable Sources: Limited. They will finish one day. (Coal, Petrol, Diesel)
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Solar panels on roofs use Renewable energy. Scooters use Petrol (Non-renewable).
💡 Tip: Re-new-able = New again and again!

Quick Check: Is wind renewable? (Answer: Yes)

3. Force - Push & Pull

What is Force?

A push or a pull acting on an object is called Force.
[Image: Boy pushing a swing, Girl pulling a toy cart]
  • Push: Moving something away from you.
  • Pull: Moving something towards you.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Pushing a car to start it. Pulling a bucket of water from a well.
💡 Tip: Push = Away, Pull = Near.

Quick Check: Kicking a football is push or pull? (Answer: Push)

4. Effects of Force

What can Force do?

Force cannot be seen, but its effects can be seen.
[Image: Clay being molded, Moving ball being stopped]
  1. It can move a stationary object.
  2. It can stop a moving object.
  3. It can change the direction of a moving object.
  4. It can change the shape of an object.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: A mother rolls dough (changes shape). A goalkeeper catches the ball (stops movement).
💡 Tip: Force is a Changer! (Changes speed, shape, direction).

Quick Check: Squeezing a lemon changes what? (Answer: Shape)

5. Simple Machines

Making Work Easy

Simple machines are tools that make our work easier and faster with less effort.
[Image: Seesaw, Pulley on well, Ramp, Cycle wheel]

Types of Simple Machines:

  • Lever: A rigid rod that moves on a point (fulcrum). Example: Seesaw, Spoon, Scissors.
  • Pulley: A wheel with a grooved rim and a rope. Example: Drawing water from a well.
  • Inclined Plane: A sloping surface. Example: A slide in the park, a ramp in a hospital.
  • Wheel & Axle: A wheel attached to a rod (axle). Example: Steering wheel, Bicycle pedals.
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Using a spoon to open a tin lid (Lever).
💡 Tip: L-P-I-W (Lever, Pulley, Inclined, Wheel).

Quick Check: What machine is a slide? (Answer: Inclined Plane)

6. Uses in Daily Life

Machines Around Us

We use simple machines every day without noticing.
[Image: Scissors cutting paper, Screwdriver turning screw]
  • Scissors: Lever (helps cut cloth).
  • Flag Pole: Pulley (helps hoist the flag).
  • Stairs: Inclined Plane (helps climb up).
  • Doorknob: Wheel & Axle (helps open door).
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: A vegetable vendor pushing a cart uses wheels (Wheel & Axle).
💡 Tip: Tools = Friends that help us work!

Quick Check: What machine is in a bicycle? (Answer: Wheel & Axle)

7. Safety While Using Machines

Be Careful!

Machines are helpful but can be dangerous if not used carefully.
[Image: Child staying away from a fan, Handling scissors carefully]
  • Do not touch moving parts of a machine (like a fan).
  • Handle sharp machines (scissors, knife) with care.
  • Do not play with electrical switches.
  • Maintain machines regularly (oiling cycles).
🇮🇳 Daily Life Example: Never put your finger inside a table fan grill.
💡 Tip: Safety First, Work Next!

Quick Check: Should we touch a moving fan? (Answer: No)
