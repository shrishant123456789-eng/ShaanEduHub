1. The Clock 🕒

A clock tells us the time. It has numbers from 1 to 12.

It has two hands:

  • Short Hand: Hour Hand (tells hours). 🟥
  • Long Hand: Minute Hand (tells minutes). 🟦

When the Long Hand is at 12, it is O'clock.
-> 3 O'clock.

When the Long Hand is at 6, it is Half Past.
-> Half past 3.

2. Days of the Week 📅

There are 7 days in a week.

  1. Monday (Start of school week) 🎒
  2. Tuesday
  3. Wednesday
  4. Thursday
  5. Friday
  6. Saturday (Weekend!) 🏏
  7. Sunday (Holiday!) 🎈

3. Months of the Year 🗓️

There are 12 months in a year.

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

❄️ January is cold. ☀️ May is hot.

4. AM and PM ☀️🌙

AM (Morning): From night till lunch time.
Example: Wake up, go to school. ☀️

PM (Afternoon/Night): From lunch time till night.
Example: Play, Dinner, Sleep. 🌙

Step-by-Step Examples

Example 1: Reading Time

Question: The Short hand is at 4. The Long hand is at 12. What time is it?

Thinking: Long hand at 12 means O'clock. Short hand at 4 means 4.

Answer: 4:00 or 4 O'clock. ✅

Example 2: Days

Question: If today is Monday, what day is tomorrow?

Thinking: Look at the list: Monday -> Tuesday.

Answer: Tuesday. ✅

Example 3: AM or PM?

Question: You eat breakfast at 8:00. Is it AM or PM?

Answer: Breakfast is in the morning. So, it is 8:00 AM. ✅
