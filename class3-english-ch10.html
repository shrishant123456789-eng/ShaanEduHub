📝 Chapter Notes

1. Paragraph Writing

Definition: Writing a group of sentences about one main idea or topic.

A paragraph is like a small family of sentences that stick together! They all talk about the same thing. The first sentence is called the topic sentence – it tells us what the paragraph is about. The other sentences give more details. We leave a line before starting a new paragraph.

Topic: My Pet Dog
Paragraph: I have a pet dog. His name is Tommy. He is brown in color. He likes to play with me. I love my dog very much.
💡 Memory Tip: One paragraph = One topic! When topic changes, new paragraph begins!

Quick Check

How many main ideas in one paragraph? (One/Many)

2. Story Writing (With Pictures)

Definition: Creating a story by looking at pictures and using imagination to connect events.

Pictures help us write stories! Look at each picture carefully. Find out who is in the picture, what they are doing, and what happens next. Connect the pictures with words. A story has a beginning (introduction), middle (action), and end (conclusion).

Picture Story:
1. A boy finds a puppy (Beginning)
2. He takes care of it (Middle)
3. They become friends (End)
💡 Memory Tip: Picture + Imagination = Story! Look, Think, Write!
[Image: Three-panel picture story sequence]

Quick Check

A story needs how many parts? (3/2)

3. Story Sequencing

Definition: Arranging events in the correct order – what happened first, next, and last.

Everything happens in order! First we wake up, then brush teeth, then eat breakfast. When writing, we must put events in the right sequence. Use words like: First, Then, Next, After that, Finally. This makes the story clear and easy to understand.

Sequence Words:
• First – I woke up
• Then – I brushed my teeth
• Next – I ate breakfast
• Finally – I went to school
💡 Memory Tip: First-Next-Last! Like climbing stairs – one step at a time!
[Image: Numbered sequence steps or flow chart]

Quick Check

Which word comes first: Finally or First?

4. Creative Writing (Imagination)

Definition: Using your imagination to create new stories, characters, and worlds.

Creative writing means making things up! You can write about a flying car, a talking tree, or a trip to the moon. Use descriptive words (adjectives) to make it interesting. Imagine you are in the story – what do you see, hear, and feel?

Creative Start:
"Once upon a time, there was a magical pencil. Whatever it drew became real! One day, a little girl named Priya found it under her bed..."
💡 Memory Tip: Let your imagination fly like a bird! No limits in creative writing!
[Image: Thought bubble with imaginative elements]

Quick Check

5. Writing About Myself

Definition: Writing a short paragraph introducing yourself – your name, age, class, likes, and family.

This is your introduction! Write your name, how old you are, which class you study in, what you like to do, and a little about your family. Use simple present tense. Start with "My name is..." or "I am..."

Example:
My name is Rahul. I am eight years old. I study in Class 3. I like to play cricket and read books. I live with my parents and my sister. I am a happy boy.
💡 Memory Tip: Name-Age-Class-Likes-Family! NACLF - Remember "Nacl" like salt!
[Image: Child introducing themselves]

Quick Check

What tense do we use for "Myself"? (Present/Past)

6. Writing About My Family

Definition: Writing about family members, their names, what they do, and why you love them.

Family is special! Write about your mother, father, brothers, sisters, grandparents. Mention what work they do (teacher, doctor, farmer, homemaker). Write about things you do together. Use words like loving, caring, helpful.

Example:
There are five members in my family. My father is a teacher. My mother is a homemaker. I have one brother. My grandparents live with us. We eat dinner together every day. I love my family.
💡 Memory Tip: Family is a gift that lasts forever! Count, Describe, Love!
[Image: Family members labeled]

Quick Check

How many family members should we write about? (All/Only parents)

7. Writing About My School

Definition: Describing your school building, classrooms, teachers, friends, and activities.

Your school is your second home! Write the name of your school, what it looks like (big building, playground, garden), your favorite subject, your class teacher's name, and what you do during assembly or break time. Mention games you play.

Example:
The name of my school is Delhi Public School. It is a big building with twenty classrooms. We have a large playground. My class teacher is Mrs. Sharma. I like Mathematics. During break, I play with my friends. I love my school.
💡 Memory Tip: School = Building + Teachers + Friends + Games! BTF Game!
[Image: School building with labeled parts]

Quick Check

What should we mention about school facilities? (Playground/Library/Both)

8. Writing About My Favorite Things

Definition: Writing about your favorite toy, food, animal, game, or hobby and why you like it.

Everyone has favorites! Choose one thing you really like. Describe it (color, size, look). Tell why it is special to you. When did you get it? How do you feel when you play with it or eat it? Use words like favorite, special, love, enjoy.

Example:
My favorite toy is my teddy bear. Its name is Bruno. It is brown and soft. I sleep with it every night. It makes me feel happy and safe. I got it on my birthday. I love my teddy bear.
💡 Memory Tip: What-Look-Why-Feel! Describe your favorite completely!
[Image: Favorite things collage - toy, food, animal]

Quick Check

Should we say why we like something? (Yes/No)

9. Short Notice / Message Writing (Intro)

Definition: Writing short, clear information for others to read quickly.

A notice or message is short and to the point! It tells Who, What, When, Where. Write the date at the top. Use bullet points or short sentences. Example: "To: Mother, From: Priya, Date: 20/01/2026, Message: I have gone to play atRadha's house. Will return by 5 PM."

Format:
To: [Name]
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Message: [Short information]
💡 Memory Tip: Short and Sweet! Who-What-When-Where!

🎯 Important Questions

1. What is a paragraph?

Answer: A group of sentences about one main topic.
All sentences in a paragraph stick to one subject.

2. Name the three parts of a story.

Answer: Beginning, Middle, and End.
Introduction, action/problem, and conclusion.

3. What is story sequencing?

Answer: Putting events in correct order (First, Next, Last).
Helps readers follow the story logically.

4. Which word shows the first event?

Answer: First or Once upon a time.
Sequence starters indicate beginning.

5. Can we write imaginary things in creative writing?

Answer: Yes.
Creative writing allows imagination and fantasy.

6. What should you write in "Myself"?

Answer: Name, age, class, likes, family.
Basic personal information introduction.

7. What tense is used for "My Family"?

Answer: Simple Present Tense.
Current situation description.

8. What does a notice include?

Answer: To, From, Date, Message.
Format for short written communication.

9. Why do we use pictures for story writing?

Answer: They give us ideas and sequence.
Visual aids help imagination.

10. Write one sequence word.

Answer: First, Then, Next, Finally, After that.
Any time-order connective.

11. What is the first sentence of a paragraph called?

Answer: Topic sentence.
It introduces the main idea.

12. Should a notice be long?

Answer: No, it should be short and clear.
Notices are brief communications.

13. What should you write about school?

Answer: Name, building, teachers, activities.
Description of educational environment.

14. What makes creative writing special?

Answer: Imagination and descriptive words.
Uses adjectives and fantasy elements.

15. Write about your favorite toy in two lines.

Answer: My favorite toy is a car. It is red and fast.
Any valid description with reason.

16. What does "Finally" mean in a story?

Answer: The last event or end.
Indicates conclusion of sequence.

17. How many topics in one paragraph?

Answer: One topic only.
Paragraph unity principle.

18. What should we write after finishing a paragraph?

Answer: Full stop and leave a line.
Formatting for next paragraph.

19. Name two things you can write about your family.

Answer: Names, occupations, activities together.
Family description elements.

20. What is the purpose of "Myself" writing?

Answer: To introduce oneself to others.
Self-introduction format.

21. Arrange: Finally/First/Then

Answer: First, Then, Finally.
Correct chronological order.

22. What does "To" mean in a notice?

Answer: The person who will read it.
Addressee in message format.

23. Can a story have pictures only?

Answer: No, it needs words too.
Picture story requires text.

24. What color is your favorite food?

Answer: Any valid color description.
Personal preference examples accepted.

25. What is the best title for a paragraph about dogs?

Answer: My Pet Dog / Dogs.
Relevant heading to topic.

26. Should a story be true or imaginary?

Answer: Can be either.
Stories can be real or fictional.

27. Write a sentence about school using "big".

Answer: My school is big.
Descriptive sentence construction.

28. What comes first: From or To?

Answer: To.
Notice format order.

29. What helps you write creatively?

Answer: Imagination, books, pictures.
Creative writing inspiration sources.

30. Write one line about your mother.

Answer: My mother is kind and loving.
Any descriptive sentence valid.
