Culture Notebook 📖

🪔 Festivals of India

We celebrate many festivals together with love.

Diwali: The festival of lights. We light diyas and eat sweets.

Eid: Celebrated after Ramadan (month of fasting). A sweet dish called 'Sewaiyan' is made.

Christmas: Birthday of Jesus Christ (25th Dec). We decorate trees and get gifts.

Harvest Festivals: Thanking nature for food.

Pongal (Tamil Nadu), Onam (Kerala - Snake Boat Race).

💡 Tip: Onam has an 'O' like a boat!

🏰 Famous Monuments

Old and beautiful buildings built by kings.

Taj Mahal: Located in Agra. It is made of white marble. Built by Shah Jahan.

Red Fort: Located in New Delhi. The Prime Minister hoists the flag here on Independence Day.

Qutub Minar: A very tall brick minaret in Delhi.

Gateway of India: A famous landmark in Mumbai.

💃 Dances of India

India has two types of dances: Classical and Folk.

Classical Dances:

  • Kathak: Famous in North India. Tells stories.
  • Bharatanatyam: Famous in Tamil Nadu. Uses hand signs (Mudras).

Folk Dances:

  • Bhangra: Energetic dance from Punjab (Dhol music).
  • Garba: Dance with sticks (Dandiya) from Gujarat.

💡 Tip: Bhangra goes Balle Balle!

🗣️ Languages

Different states speak different languages.

  • Punjab: Punjabi
  • Bengal: Bengali
  • Maharashtra: Marathi
  • Tamil Nadu: Tamil
  • Kerala: Malayalam

Hindi is widely spoken across India.
