Class Notes & Concepts
1. Introduction to Decimals

A decimal is a way of writing a number that is not whole. It represents a part of a whole, just like a fraction.

Example: 1.5 is one and a half. The dot (.) is called the decimal point.

Daily Life: Price tags! ₹10.50 means 10 rupees and 50 paise.

💡 Memory Tip: Think of the decimal point as a separator between Whole Numbers (Kings) and Fractions (Commoners).

1 Whole + 0.5 Half = 1.5
🌕 + 🌗 = 1.5
2. Place Value of Decimals

After the decimal point, the values get smaller by 10 times.

Order: Tenths (1/10) → Hundredths (1/100) → Thousandths (1/1000).

Ones.TenthsHundredths
5.23

5.23 = 5 + 2/10 + 3/100

3. Conversion: Fractions to Decimals

To convert, divide the numerator by the denominator OR make the denominator 10, 100, 1000.

Example: 7/10 = 0.7 | 5/100 = 0.05

3/2 = 1.5

1/4 = 0.25

Method: 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25

4. Comparing Decimals

Always compare the whole number part first. If they are equal, compare the tenths, then hundredths.

Example: 5.6 vs 5.4 -> 5 is same. 6 > 4, so 5.6 > 5.4

⚠️ Trap: 0.5 is NOT smaller than 0.09. 0.5 = 0.50, which is bigger than 0.09.

Compare: 3.40 _ 3.04
Answer: 3.40 > 3.04
5. Ordering Decimals

Ascending: Smallest to Biggest. Descending: Biggest to Smallest.

Trick: Add zeros to make them the same length.

Example: 0.2, 0.02, 0.22 -> Change to 0.20, 0.02, 0.22 -> Order: 0.02, 0.20, 0.22

Ascending: 1.1, 1.01, 1.11
Correct: 1.01, 1.10, 1.11
6. Addition of Decimals

Golden Rule: ALIGN THE DOTS!

Write numbers one below the other so decimal points are in a straight line.

  12.50
+  3.45
-------
  15.95
7. Subtraction of Decimals

Align the dots. Fill empty spaces with Zeros.

Example: 5 - 2.4 -> Write as 5.0 - 2.4

  5.0
-  2.4
-------
  2.6
8. Decimal Multiplication

1. Ignore point, multiply normally.

2. Count total decimal places in question.

3. Put point in answer.

0.2 x 0.3 = ?
2 x 3 = 6
Total places = 1 + 1 = 2
Answer = 0.06
9. Decimal Division

When dividing by 10, 100, 1000, move the decimal point to the LEFT.

34.5 ÷ 10 = 3.45 (Jump 1 left)

← Left for Division
Right for Multiplication →
10. Money & Decimals

1 Rupee = 100 Paise.

To convert Paise to Rupees, put a dot before 2 digits.

50 paise = ₹0.50 | 5 paise = ₹0.05

💰 ₹5.75 = 5 Rupees + 75 Paise
11. Measurement

1 cm = 10 mm (0.1 cm = 1 mm)

1 m = 100 cm (0.01 m = 1 cm)

1 kg = 1000 g (0.001 kg = 1 g)

📏 2 kg 500 g = 2.500 kg = 2.5 kg
12. Advanced Word Problems

Read carefully. Identify if you need sum (total), difference (how much left), product (cost of many), or division (share).

"Total cost" -> Add
"Change back" -> Subtract
