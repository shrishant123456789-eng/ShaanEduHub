🖥️ Main Parts of a Computer

A computer has four main parts that work together like a team. Let's learn about each one!

1. Monitor 🖥️

What is it? Monitor is like a TV screen. It shows us pictures, letters, and videos.

What does it do? We can see everything on the monitor. When we type, draw, or play games, we see it all on the monitor screen.

Real-life example: Just like you watch cartoons on TV, you see your work on a computer monitor!

2. CPU (Central Processing Unit) 🧠

What is it? CPU is the brain of the computer. It is usually inside a box called the system unit or CPU cabinet.

What does it do? CPU thinks and does all the work. It follows our instructions and makes the computer work.

Real-life example: Like our brain helps us think and solve problems, CPU helps the computer think and work!

3. Keyboard ⌨️

What is it? Keyboard has many buttons called keys. Each key has a letter, number, or symbol on it.

What does it do? We use the keyboard to type letters, numbers, and words. It helps us write on the computer.

Real-life example: Just like you write in your notebook with a pencil, you type on a computer using a keyboard!

4. Mouse 🖱️

What is it? Mouse is a small device that fits in our hand. It has buttons on top and sometimes a wheel in the middle.

What does it do? We use the mouse to point, click, and select things on the screen. It helps us move around on the computer.

Real-life example: Like you point at things with your finger, the mouse helps you point at things on the computer screen!

💡 Remember: Monitor, CPU, Keyboard, and Mouse are the four main parts of a computer. They all work together to make the computer useful!

📥 Input Devices

Input devices are parts that help us give information to the computer. We use them to tell the computer what to do.

1. Keyboard ⌨️ (Input Device)

Keyboard is an input device because we use it to put information into the computer. We type letters, numbers, and words using the keyboard.

Example: When you type your name, the keyboard sends that information to the computer.

2. Mouse 🖱️ (Input Device)

Mouse is also an input device. We use it to click, select, and move things on the screen. The mouse tells the computer what we want to do.

Example: When you click on a game icon with the mouse, it tells the computer to open that game.

3. Microphone 🎤 (Input Device)

What is it? Microphone is a device that we use to record our voice or talk to the computer.

What does it do? Microphone takes our voice and sends it to the computer. The computer can then save our voice or listen to our commands.

Example: When you sing a song and record it on the computer, you use a microphone!

💡 Remember INPUT: IN means going inside. Input devices put information INTO the computer. Keyboard, Mouse, and Microphone are input devices!

📤 Output Devices

Output devices are parts that show or give us information from the computer. They help us see, hear, or get results from the computer.

1. Monitor 🖥️ (Output Device)

Monitor is an output device because it shows us what the computer is doing. It displays pictures, words, and videos that come OUT from the computer.

Example: When you draw a picture, the monitor shows you that picture on the screen.

2. Printer 🖨️ (Output Device)

What is it? Printer is a machine that prints things on paper.

What does it do? Printer takes pictures or text from the computer and prints them on paper. We can then hold the paper in our hands.

Example: When your teacher prints your homework from the computer, a printer is used!

3. Speakers 🔊 (Output Device)

What is it? Speakers are devices that make sounds and music.

What does it do? Speakers let us hear music, songs, voices, and sounds that come OUT from the computer.

Example: When you listen to a song on the computer, you hear it through speakers!

💡 Remember OUTPUT: OUT means coming outside. Output devices bring information OUT of the computer. Monitor, Printer, and Speakers are output devices!

💾 Storage Devices

Storage devices are parts that save and keep our information safe. They remember everything even when the computer is turned off.

1. Hard Disk 💿

What is it? Hard disk is inside the computer. It is like a big cupboard where the computer keeps all its files, photos, and programs.

What does it do? Hard disk stores a lot of information. It keeps everything safe even when we turn off the computer.

Example: All your photos, games, and homework are saved in the hard disk!

2. Pen Drive 📀

What is it? Pen drive is a small device that we can carry in our pocket. It is also called a USB drive or flash drive.

What does it do? Pen drive stores files, photos, and videos. We can plug it into the computer, save our work, take it out, and carry it anywhere.

Example: If you want to take your project from school to home, you can save it in a pen drive and carry it!

💡 Remember STORAGE: Storage devices are like bags and cupboards. They keep our things safe! Hard Disk and Pen Drive are storage devices!

🎯 Quick Summary

Main Parts: Monitor, CPU, Keyboard, Mouse

Input Devices (Give information TO computer): Keyboard, Mouse, Microphone

Output Devices (Get information FROM computer): Monitor, Printer, Speakers

Storage Devices (Save information): Hard Disk, Pen Drive

Answer: Keyboard, Mouse, Microphone

Q9. Name three output devices.

Answer: Keyboard is an input device.

Q13. What does a printer do?
Answer: Printer prints text or pictures from the computer on paper.
Q14. What are speakers used for?
Answer: Speakers are used to hear sounds, music, and voices from the computer.

Answer: Storage devices are parts that save and keep our information safe.

Answer: Hard Disk, Pen Drive

Answer: Hard disk is a storage device inside the computer that stores a lot of information like files, photos, and programs.
Q18. What is a pen drive?
Answer: Pen drive is a small storage device that we can carry in our pocket to save and carry files.
Q19. Where is the CPU located?
Answer: CPU is located inside the system unit or CPU cabinet.
Q20. Why is CPU called the brain of the computer?
Answer: CPU is called the brain of the computer because it thinks and does all the work, just like our brain helps us think.

Answer: No, hard disk is inside the computer. We can carry a pen drive in our pocket.
Q22. Write one difference between input and output devices.
Answer: Input devices give information TO the computer, while output devices give information FROM the computer to us.

Answer: When we click the mouse, it tells the computer to select or open something on the screen.
Q24. True or False: Monitor is an input device.
Answer: False (Monitor is an output device)
