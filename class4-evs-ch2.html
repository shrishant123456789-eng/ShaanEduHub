⚡ Quick Revision

  • Plants are classified into Herbs, Shrubs, Trees, Climbers, and Creepers based on stems.
  • Roots anchor the plant and absorb water. Tap roots have one main root; fibrous roots are messy.
  • Stems carry water and food. They support branches and leaves.
  • Leaves are the 'Kitchen of the Plant'. They make food using sunlight (Photosynthesis).
  • Flowers turn into fruits. Fruits have seeds. Seeds grow into new plants.
  • Seed dispersal happens via Wind, Water, Animals, and Explosion.
  • Plants adapt to their surroundings: Cactus (Desert), Lotus (Water), Pine (Mountains).
