Class 2 • Chapter 1 • Introduction
🤖 What is a Computer?

Hello kids! A computer is a Smart Machine. It runs on electricity. It makes our work easy and very fast!

⚡ Why is it Smart?

1. It works very fast.
2. It never gets tired.
3. It never makes mistakes.
4. It can remember many things.

🏥 Places & Uses

Computers are used everywhere! Let's see where:

🏠 Home: Playing games, watching cartoons, doing homework.
🏫 School: Teaching, keeping records of students.
🏥 Hospital: Keeping details of patients, helping doctors.
🏦 Bank: Keeping money safe, ATM machines.
🏢 Office: Typing letters, sending emails.
🛒 Shop: Making bills for things we buy.

🆚 Human vs Computer

Humans (Us): We have feelings. We get tired. We can make mistakes.

Computers: No feelings. Never tired. Zero mistakes. But, a computer cannot think on its own! It needs YOU to tell it what to do.
