1. Indoor & Outdoor Games

🖼️ Image: Kids playing Ludo (Indoor) & Cricket (Outdoor)

Definition: Indoor games are played inside a house or building. Outdoor games are played in open fields or parks.

Examples:
  • Indoor: Ludo, Chess, Carrom, Snakes & Ladders.
  • Outdoor: Cricket, Football, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi.
🇮🇳 India Life: In rainy season, we play Carrom at home. On sunny days, we play Cricket in the park.
💡 Memory Tip: 'In' means Inside (Room). 'Out' means Outside (Sun).
❓ Quick Check: Is Football an indoor game? (Answer: No)

2. National & International Sports

🖼️ Image: Hockey Stick (National) & Football (International)

Definition: A National Sport is the main sport of a country. International Sports are played by many countries together.

Examples:
  • Field Hockey is considered the National Sport of India.
  • Football and Tennis are played all over the world.
🇮🇳 India Life: We cheer for 'Team India' in the Cricket World Cup against other countries.
💡 Memory Tip: 'Inter' means between. International = Between nations.
❓ Quick Check: What is India's national sport? (Answer: Field Hockey)

3. Sports Equipment

🖼️ Image: Helmet, Bat, Ball, Shuttlecock

Definition: The tools or things we need to play a game are called Sports Equipment.

Examples:
  • Cricket needs: Bat, Ball, Stumps.
  • Badminton needs: Racquet, Shuttlecock.
  • Safety: Helmets and knee pads protect us from injury.
🇮🇳 India Life: Before playing gully cricket, we check if the bat is strong and ball is taped.
💡 Memory Tip: Equipment = Tools for play. No tool = No game!
❓ Quick Check: What do we hit in Badminton? (Answer: Shuttlecock)

4. Famous Sportspersons

🖼️ Image: Silhouette of Virat Kohli & P.V. Sindhu

Definition: People who play sports professionally and make their country proud are Sportspersons.

Famous Names:
  • India: Virat Kohli (Cricket), P.V. Sindhu (Badminton), Neeraj Chopra (Javelin).
  • World: Lionel Messi (Football), Usain Bolt (Running).
🇮🇳 India Life: We see photos of Neeraj Chopra holding his Gold Medal in newspapers.
💡 Memory Tip: Neeraj threw the Spear (Javelin) very far!
❓ Quick Check: Which game does P.V. Sindhu play? (Answer: Badminton)

5. Importance of Games & Fitness

🖼️ Image: Kids stretching and running

Definition: Playing games keeps our body strong (Physical Fitness) and mind happy (Mental Health).

Benefits:
  • Makes muscles and bones strong.
  • Teaches teamwork and discipline.
  • Keeps us away from laziness.
🇮🇳 India Life: PT period in school helps us stretch and feel fresh for studies.
💡 Memory Tip: Fit Body = Sharp Mind. 🧠💪
❓ Quick Check: Do games teach discipline? (Answer: Yes)

6. Olympic Games

🖼️ Image: Five Olympic Rings

Definition: The Olympics is the world's biggest sports competition held every 4 years.

Key Points:
  • Symbol: 5 interlaced rings (Blue, Yellow, Black, Green, Red).
  • Values: Friendship, Respect, Excellence.
  • Athletes from almost every country participate.
🇮🇳 India Life: We wait for 4 years to see India win medals at the Olympics.
💡 Memory Tip: 5 Rings = 5 Continents joined together.
❓ Quick Check: How often are Olympics held? (Answer: Every 4 years)

7. Music Instruments

🖼️ Image: Tabla, Sitar, Guitar

Definition: Objects created to make musical sounds are Musical Instruments.

Types:
  • Indian: Tabla, Sitar, Flute (Bansuri), Harmonium.
  • Western: Guitar, Piano, Violin, Drums.
🇮🇳 India Life: In school assembly, someone plays the Harmonium or Drums during prayers.
💡 Memory Tip: Tabla = Tap with hands. Flute = Blow air.
❓ Quick Check: Is Tabla an Indian instrument? (Answer: Yes)

8. Dance Forms

🖼️ Image: Dancer in Bharatanatyam pose

Definition: Moving the body to music to express feelings is called Dance.

Examples:
  • Classical (India): Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu), Kathak (North India), Kathakali (Kerala).
  • Folk (India): Bhangra (Punjab), Garba (Gujarat).
🇮🇳 India Life: We dance Garba during Navratri festivals.
💡 Memory Tip: Bhangra = Balle Balle (Punjab)!
❓ Quick Check: Which state is Bhangra from? (Answer: Punjab)

9. Art, Painting & Creativity

🖼️ Image: Paintbrush and Palette

Definition: Using imagination to create something beautiful like drawings or paintings is Art.

Details:
  • Tools: Pencil, colors, paper, canvas.
  • Creativity helps us solve problems in new ways.
  • Folk Art: Madhubani (Bihar), Warli (Maharashtra).
🇮🇳 India Life: Making Rangoli on Diwali is a beautiful form of art.
💡 Memory Tip: Art is freedom of thought on paper.
❓ Quick Check: Is Rangoli an art form? (Answer: Yes)

10. Entertainment – Good & Bad Use

🖼️ Image: Book vs Mobile Phone

Definition: Activities we do for fun and relaxation are Entertainment.

Balance is Key:
  • Good: Reading stories, playing board games, watching educational shows.
  • Bad: Watching TV for hours, playing mobile games all day (hurts eyes).
🇮🇳 India Life: Watching a movie with family on Sunday is good fun, but not every day!
💡 Memory Tip: Too much of anything is bad. Balance screen time with play time.
❓ Quick Check: Is playing mobile games for 5 hours good? (Answer: No)
