Chapter 5 Notes

1. External Body Parts

Definition: Parts of the body that we can see from outside.

Explanation: Our body has many parts. The parts like head, neck, hands, and legs are outside. We can touch them.

Real-life Example: When you wave to a friend, you use your hand (External Part).

Memory Tip: External = Exit (Outside).

Quick Check: Is the heart an external part? (No)
2. Internal Organs

Definition: Organs inside our body that we cannot see.

[attachment_0](attachment)

Explanation: These organs work all the time, even when we sleep. The main ones are Brain, Heart, Lungs, and Stomach.

Real-life Example: You feel your heart beating after running (Internal Organ).

Memory Tip: Internal = In (Inside).

Quick Check: Can you see your stomach organ? (No)
3. Functions of Body Parts

Definition: The work that each body part does.

  • Brain: Helps us think and remember.
  • Heart: Pumps blood to all parts.
  • Lungs: Help us breathe.
  • Stomach: Digests the food we eat.

Real-life Example: Your brain tells your hand to pick up a pencil.

Memory Tip: Brain = Boss of the body.

4. Sense Organs

Definition: Organs that help us know about the world around us.

[attachment_1](attachment)

Explanation: We have FIVE sense organs: Eyes, Ears, Nose, Tongue, and Skin.

Real-life Example: Seeing a rainbow with eyes.

Memory Tip: Count fingers on one hand = 5 Sense Organs.

5. Functions of Sense Organs
  • Eyes: To see shapes and colors.
  • Ears: To hear sounds and music.
  • Nose: To smell flowers or food.
  • Tongue: To taste sweet, sour, salty, bitter.
  • Skin: To feel hot, cold, soft, or hard.

Real-life Example: Tasting ice cream (Tongue) and feeling it is cold (Skin).

6. Taking Care of Our Body

Definition: Keeping body clean and safe.

  • Brush teeth twice a day.
  • Take a bath daily.
  • Trim nails regularly.
  • Wash hands before eating.

Real-life Example: Washing hands prevents germs.

Memory Tip: Clean Body = Happy Body.

7. Exercise and Physical Activity

Definition: Moving the body to keep it strong.

[attachment_2](attachment)

Explanation: Playing outdoor games, running, swimming, and cycling are good exercises.

Real-life Example: Playing tag with friends in the park.

Memory Tip: Move more to stay fit.

8. Good Posture

Definition: The correct way to sit, stand, and walk.

[attachment_3](attachment)

Explanation: Keep your back straight. Do not bend or slouch.

Real-life Example: Sitting straight on the chair in class.

Memory Tip: Stand tall like a tree.

9. Common Illnesses

Definition: When the body is not feeling well.

Explanation: Fever, cold, cough, and stomach ache are common. We should rest and see a doctor.

Real-life Example: Getting a cold after playing in rain.

Memory Tip: Rest helps us heal.

10. Keeping Body Fit and Active

Definition: Having energy to do work and play.

[attachment_4](attachment)
  • Eat healthy food (fruits, vegetables).
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Sleep for 8-9 hours.

Real-life Example: Eating an apple instead of chips.

Memory Tip: Eat Well + Sleep Well + Play Well.
