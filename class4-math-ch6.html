📝 My Study Notes

1. What are Decimals?

Decimals are another way to write fractions. They use a dot called the Decimal Point.

The decimal point separates the Whole Number part from the Fractional part.

35.4

35 = Whole Part

. = Decimal Point

4 = Decimal Part

2. Understanding Tenths (1/10)

When we divide one whole into 10 equal parts, each part is called one-tenth.

  • Fraction: 1/10
  • Decimal: 0.1

4/10 = 0.4

9/10 = 0.9

3. Understanding Hundredths (1/100)

When we divide one whole into 100 equal parts, each part is called one-hundredth.

  • Fraction: 1/100
  • Decimal: 0.01

5/100 = 0.05

25/100 = 0.25

💡 Tip: If there is 1 zero in denominator, put point before 1 digit. If 2 zeros, put point before 2 digits!

4. Decimal Place Value Chart

As we move Right from the decimal point, values get smaller.

Tens (10) Ones (1) Point (.) Tenths (1/10) Hundredths (1/100)
2 5 . 6 8

Number: 25.68

Read as: "Twenty-five point six eight" (Read decimal digits separately!).

5. Expanded Form

Writing a decimal as the sum of the value of its digits.

Example: 4.25

= 4 (Ones) + 2 (Tenths) + 5 (Hundredths)

= 4 + 2/10 + 5/100

= 4 + 0.2 + 0.05

6. Conversions

Decimal to Fraction: Write the number without the point. Denominator is 1 followed by zeros equal to decimal places.

0.5 = 5/10

0.07 = 7/100

Fraction to Decimal:

7/10 = 0.7

13/100 = 0.13

5/100 = 0.05 (Add zero if digits are fewer!)

7. Comparing Decimals

  1. Compare Whole number parts. (5.2 > 4.9)
  2. If whole parts are same, compare Tenths. (3.5 > 3.2)
  3. If tenths are same, compare Hundredths. (1.25 > 1.22)
⚡ Trick: Add zeros to make them look same length. 0.5 vs 0.45 -> 0.50 vs 0.45. Now 50 > 45!

8. Money and Measurement

100 Paise = 1 Rupee. So, 1 Paise = 1/100 Rupee = ₹0.01.

50 Paise = ₹0.50

5 Rupees 25 Paise = ₹5.25

Same for meters and cm (100cm = 1m)!
