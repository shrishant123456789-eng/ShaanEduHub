💻 Uses of Computer

Class 2 • Chapter 4 • Fun Learning
📚

In Education

Computers are very smart!
• Teachers use them in Schools to teach.
• Students use them at Home for homework.
• You can take Online Classes too!
"Ravi studies math on his computer." 🎓

🎨

Drawing & Painting

Do you like colors?
• We can draw and paint on a computer.
• We use the Mouse to draw lines and shapes.
• No messy hands, just fun! 🖍️

🎮

Playing Games

This is the most fun part!
• We can play Car Racing, Puzzles, and Chess.
• Games help us think fast.
• Remember: Don't play for too long! ⏳

🎬

Watching Videos

Movie time!
• You can watch Cartoons like Tom & Jerry.
• You can watch learning videos and stories.
• It works like a small TV! 📺

📝

Writing (Typing)

Writing on a computer is called Typing.
• We use the Keyboard to type letters and stories.
• We can type homework and print it out.
• Handwriting can be messy, but typing is always clean! ✨

🎵

Listening to Music

Let's dance!
• Computers can play Songs and Rhymes.
• You can listen using Speakers or Headphones.
• It makes us feel happy! 🎧
