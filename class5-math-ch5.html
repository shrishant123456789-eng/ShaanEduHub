Class Notes 📖
1. Basic Geometric Terms

Geometry starts with points. A Point is an exact location. A Line goes on forever in both directions. A Ray starts at one point and goes forever in one direction. A Line Segment is part of a line with two end points.

💡 TIP: Ray sounds like 'Sun Ray' -> Starts from Sun, goes forever!
Check: Can you measure the length of a Line? (No)
2. Types of Lines

Parallel Lines: Never meet, like railway tracks.
Intersecting Lines: Cross each other like an 'X'.
Perpendicular Lines: Cross at exactly 90 degrees (L shape).

💡 TIP: Perpendicular lines make a Perfect corner (90°).
Check: The letter 'H' has what kind of lines? (Parallel & Perpendicular)
3. Types of Angles

An angle is made when two rays meet. Measured in degrees (°).

  • Acute: Less than 90° (Cute small mouth)
  • Right: Exactly 90° (L shape)
  • Obtuse: More than 90°, less than 180° (Wide open)
  • Straight: Exactly 180° (Flat line)
  • Reflex: More than 180°
💡 TIP: 'Acute' is 'A Cute' little angle.
4. Polygons & Triangles

A Polygon is a closed shape with straight sides. A Triangle has 3 sides.

By Sides: Equilateral (all equal), Isosceles (2 equal), Scalene (none equal).

💡 TIP: Tri = 3, Quad = 4, Penta = 5.
5. Symmetry

Symmetry means one half is the mirror image of the other. The line that divides them is the Line of Symmetry.

Check: Does a circle have symmetry? (Yes, infinite!)

Quick Revision ⚡
  • Point: Exact location (.)
  • Line: No endpoints (<->)
  • Ray: 1 endpoint (•->)
  • Segment: 2 endpoints (•-•)
  • Acute: < 90°
  • Right: = 90°
  • Obtuse: > 90° and < 180°
  • Straight: = 180°
  • Reflex: > 180°
  • Triangle: 3 sides, Sum of angles = 180°
  • Quadrilateral: 4 sides, Sum of angles = 360°
