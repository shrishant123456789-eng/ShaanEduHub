📝 Quick Revision Notes

1. Logical Reasoning

Definition: Using your brain to find patterns, sequences, and logic in questions.

Example: If A = 1, B = 2, then C = ?
Answer: 3 (Because we are counting alphabet positions).
💡 Tip: Always look for what changes and what stays the same!

2. Odd One Out

Definition: Finding the item that is different from the rest of the group.

Example: Car, Bus, Truck, Airplane.
Answer: Airplane (Others run on roads, Airplane flies).

3. Mixed Bag Knowledge

Definition: General facts about science, geography, sports, and India.

Fact: The largest organ in our body is Skin.
Fact: Cricket team has 11 players.

4. Case Studies

Definition: A short story situation where you must decide the right action.

Situation: You find a lost wallet.
Action: Give it to a teacher or police, do not keep it.
