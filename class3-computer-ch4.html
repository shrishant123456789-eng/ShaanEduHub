🖥️ Class 3 - Computer

Chapter 4: Uses of Computer

📘 Let's Read & Learn

🏫 Computer at School
Computers help teachers and students in many ways at school. Teachers use computers to make timetables and keep student records safely. They also use it to show colorful pictures and videos while teaching. Students use computers to do their homework, draw nice pictures, and learn new things through fun games. In the computer lab, everyone gets a chance to practice typing and explore educational programs.
🏠 Computer at Home
At home, computers are like helpful friends! We use them to do our homework and projects. We can watch funny cartoons and educational videos on the computer. Many kids love to play games on computers during free time. We can also listen to music, see family photos, and video call our relatives who live far away. It makes learning at home fun and easy!
🏥 Computer in Hospitals
Doctors and nurses use computers to take good care of patients. Computers keep all patient records safe and organized. Special machines connected to computers help doctors see inside our body through scanning. This helps them find out what is making someone sick. Computers also help make bills when patients buy medicines and help in the pharmacy to count tablets correctly.
🏦 Computer in Banks
Banks use computers to keep everyone's money records safe. When you put money in the bank, the computer remembers exactly how much you have. ATM machines are special computers that give you money even when the bank is closed. computers help print passbooks and send money quickly from one place to another. They make banking fast and safe!
🛒 Computer in Shops
Shopkeepers use computers to make bills when you buy things. A barcode scanner is connected to the computer - it reads lines on products to know the price quickly. Computers help shop owners know how many items are left in the shop (stock). They also help calculate the total money to pay back as change.
🎮 Computer for Entertainment
Computers are great for fun and play! We can play exciting video games with colorful graphics. We can watch movies and cartoons on the computer screen. Many people use computers to listen to their favorite songs and music. It helps us relax and enjoy our free time with family and friends.
📚 Computer for Learning
Learning becomes super interesting with computers! We can watch educational videos about animals, space, and history. There are special learning games that teach us math, English, and science while we play. We can read e-books and see 3D models of difficult concepts. Online classes help us learn from home with the best teachers from anywhere in the world!
