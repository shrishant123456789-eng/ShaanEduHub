Chapter 10: Perimeter and Area

Class 5 • Mathematics

"Math is the measuring tape of the universe!" 📏

My Study Notes 📝

1. What is Perimeter?
Perimeter is the total distance around a closed shape. Think of it as the length of a fence needed to surround a garden.
Example: If you walk along the boundary of a park, the total distance you walk is the Perimeter.
💡 Tip: Peri = Around, Meter = Measure. Measuring Around!
2. Finding Perimeter
To find the perimeter of ANY polygon (shape with straight sides), just add the lengths of all its sides.
Triangle with sides 3cm, 4cm, 5cm.
Perimeter = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cm.
❓ Check: What is the perimeter of a shape with sides 2m, 2m, 2m? (Ans: 6m)
3. Perimeter of a Square
A square has 4 equal sides. Instead of adding (Side + Side + Side + Side), we use multiplication.
Formula: Perimeter = 4 × Side
If side = 5cm, P = 4 × 5 = 20cm.
4. Perimeter of a Rectangle
A rectangle has length (l) and breadth (b). Opposite sides are equal.
Formula: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)
or P = 2 × (l + b)
If l=4cm, b=3cm → P = 2 × (4+3) = 2 × 7 = 14cm.
5. What is Area?
Area is the amount of surface a shape covers. We measure it in "square units" (like sq cm, sq m).
Imagine covering the floor with square tiles. The number of tiles is the Area.
💡 Tip: Perimeter is the FENCE. Area is the GRASS inside.
6. Area of a Square
Since a square has equal length and width (side), we multiply the side by itself.
Formula: Area = Side × Side
If side = 6cm, Area = 6 × 6 = 36 sq cm.
7. Area of a Rectangle
To find how much space a rectangle covers, multiply its Length by its Breadth.
Formula: Area = Length × Breadth
If l=10m, b=5m, Area = 10 × 5 = 50 sq m.
8. Area vs Perimeter
  • Perimeter: Fencing, Framing a photo, Running around a track. (Unit: m, cm)
  • Area: Painting a wall, Tiling a floor, Spreading a carpet. (Unit: sq m, sq cm)

Important Questions 🌟

1. Find the perimeter of a square with side 8 cm.
Ans: 32 cm
Formula: 4 × side = 4 × 8 = 32.
2. Find the area of a rectangle with length 12m and breadth 5m.
Ans: 60 sq m
Formula: l × b = 12 × 5 = 60.
3. Which unit is used for Area?
Ans: Square units (sq cm, sq m)
Because area is 2-dimensional (length × width).
4. The perimeter of a square is 20 cm. What is its side?
Ans: 5 cm
Side = Perimeter ÷ 4 = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
5. Find the cost of fencing a square park of side 10m at ₹5 per meter.
Ans: ₹200
Perimeter = 40m. Cost = 40 × 5 = 200.
6. A rectangle has Area 50 sq cm and length 10 cm. Find breadth.
Ans: 5 cm
Breadth = Area ÷ Length = 50 ÷ 10 = 5.
7. Find the perimeter of a triangle with sides 5cm, 6cm, 7cm.
Ans: 18 cm
Sum of all sides: 5 + 6 + 7 = 18.
8. Is the area of a square with side 4cm equal to its perimeter?
Ans: Yes (Numerically)
Area = 4×4=16. Perimeter = 4×4=16. Value is same, units differ.
9. Which shape has a larger area: Square (side 5) or Rectangle (4x6)?
Ans: Square
Sq Area=25. Rect Area=24. 25 > 24.
10. What is the formula for the perimeter of a rectangle?
Ans: 2 × (l + b)
11. A carpet covers the floor. Are we talking about Area or Perimeter?
Ans: Area
12. Find the missing side of a rectangle if Perimeter=20 and Length=6.
Ans: 4
2(6+b)=20 -> 6+b=10 -> b=4.
13. Convert 5 sq m to sq cm.
Ans: 50,000 sq cm
1 sq m = 10,000 sq cm.
14. Length of a room is 5m, breadth is 4m. Find carpet area.
Ans: 20 sq m
15. Length of boundary of a closed figure is called?
Ans: Perimeter
16. Area of square is 81 sq cm. What is the side?
Ans: 9 cm
9 × 9 = 81.
17. How many cm make 1 meter?
Ans: 100 cm
18. Perimeter of regular pentagon (5 sides) of side 3cm?
Ans: 15 cm
5 sides × 3cm = 15cm.
19. If length is doubled, what happens to the perimeter of a square?
Ans: It doubles
20. Cost of tiling a floor of 10 sq m at ₹10 per sq m?
Ans: ₹100
10 × 10 = 100.
21. A wire forms a square of side 10cm. If bent into rectangle length 12cm, find breadth.
Ans: 8 cm
Perimeter stays 40. 2(12+b)=40 -> 12+b=20 -> b=8.
22. Perimeter of an equilateral triangle side 10cm?
Ans: 30 cm
3 × 10 = 30.
23. Can two shapes have same area but different perimeter?
Ans: Yes
24. What is the perimeter of a rectangle length 15cm breadth 0cm?
Ans: 30 cm
Though it's a line, formula says 2(15+0)=30.
25. Side of square is 12m. Find Area.
Ans: 144 sq m
26. Total length of boundary of circle is called?
Ans: Circumference
(Special name for circle perimeter).
27. Area of a postcard is measured in?
Ans: sq cm
28. Area of a playground is measured in?
Ans: sq m or hectares
29. Length=4, Breadth=3. Find sum of Area and Perimeter.
Ans: 26
A=12, P=14. Sum = 26.
30. Does perimeter change if we cut a corner of a square?
Ans: Usually stays same or increases
The boundary path shifts inwards but length often remains same for simple corner cuts.
