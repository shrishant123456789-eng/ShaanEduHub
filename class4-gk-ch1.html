📖 Chapter Notes

TOPIC 1: Introduction of Self
🖼️ Image: A child introducing themselves
Definition: Introducing yourself means telling others who you are.
Explanation: It includes your full name, age, where you live (address), school name, and hobbies. It helps people know you better.
🇮🇳 Example: "Namaste! My name is Aarav. I am 9 years old. I live in Jaipur."
💡 Tip: Always speak loudly and smile when introducing yourself!
❓ Quick Check: What comes first in an introduction? (Answer: Your Name)
TOPIC 2: Family Members & Relations
🖼️ Image: Family Tree Chart
Definition: People related to us by birth or marriage are our family.
Explanation: Parents, grandparents, brothers (siblings), sisters, uncles, and aunts make a family.
🇮🇳 Example: Your father's father is your 'Dada ji' (Paternal Grandfather). Your mother's brother is your 'Mama ji' (Maternal Uncle).
💡 Tip: Paternal = Papa's side, Maternal = Mummy's side.
❓ Quick Check: Who is your father's mother? (Answer: Grandmother / Dadi ji)
TOPIC 3: Types of Families
🖼️ Image: Nuclear vs Joint Family
Definition: Families can be big or small based on who lives together.
Types:
1. Nuclear Family: Only parents and children living together (Small).
2. Joint Family: Grandparents, parents, uncles, aunts, and cousins living together (Big).
🇮🇳 Example: In cities, many people live in Nuclear families. In villages, Joint families are common.
💡 Tip: Joint = Joined together (Many people). Nuclear = Nucleus (Small core).
❓ Quick Check: Which family type has grandparents living with you? (Answer: Joint Family)
TOPIC 4: Good Manners
🖼️ Image: Child saying Thank You
Definition: Polite behavior towards others.
Explanation: Using magic words helps us show respect.
🇮🇳 Example: Touching elders' feet (Charan Sparsh) or saying "Namaste" to guests.
💡 Tip: 3 Magic Words: Please, Sorry, Thank You.
❓ Quick Check: What do you say when you hurt someone? (Answer: Sorry)
TOPIC 5: Moral Values
🖼️ Image: Helping an old person
Definition: Good principles that guide our behavior.
Values: Honesty (Truth), Kindness (Helping), Respect (Listening).
🇮🇳 Example: Returning a lost pencil to your classmate is Honesty.
💡 Tip: Treat others how you want to be treated.
❓ Quick Check: Telling the truth is which value? (Answer: Honesty)
TOPIC 6: Daily Routine & Discipline
🖼️ Image: Clock showing routine
Definition: Doing things at the right time every day.
Explanation: Early to bed, early to rise. Brushing teeth twice. Studying on time.
🇮🇳 Example: Getting ready for school bus on time so you don't miss it.
💡 Tip: Time is precious, never waste it!
❓ Quick Check: How many times should we brush teeth? (Answer: Twice)
TOPIC 7: Responsibility
🖼️ Image: Child packing school bag
Definition: Doing your duty without being told.
Explanation: Keeping your room clean, packing your school bag, switching off lights.
🇮🇳 Example: Helping mother in the kitchen or watering plants in the evening.
💡 Tip: A responsible child is a loved child.
❓ Quick Check: Who should pack your school bag? (Answer: I myself)
TOPIC 8: Leadership & Teamwork
🖼️ Image: Kids playing tug of war
Definition: Working together to win or complete a task.
Explanation: Sharing ideas, listening to friends, and helping the group.
🇮🇳 Example: Playing Cricket or Kabaddi requires the whole team to work together.
💡 Tip: T.E.A.M = Together Everyone Achieves More.
❓ Quick Check: Can one player win a cricket match alone? (Answer: No)
TOPIC 9: Decision Making
🖼️ Image: Choosing apple over chips
Definition: Choosing the best option between two things.
Explanation: Thinking before doing. Is it good or bad for me?
🇮🇳 Example: Choosing to finish homework before watching TV.
💡 Tip: Stop -> Think -> Choose.
❓ Quick Check: Should we choose junk food or healthy food? (Answer: Healthy food)
