1. Human Body Organization

Our body is like a building. Cells are the bricks.

  • Cells: Smallest unit of life.
  • Tissue: Group of similar cells working together.
  • Organ: Group of tissues (e.g., Heart, Stomach).
  • Organ System: Group of organs doing one major job.
  • Organism: The complete human body.
[Image: Cell -> Tissue -> Organ -> System]
💡 Tip: C-T-O-S-O (Cell, Tissue, Organ, System, Organism)

2. Digestive System

This system changes food into energy.

  • Mouth: Teeth chew, Saliva softens food.
  • Oesophagus (Food Pipe): Pushes food to stomach.
  • Stomach: A bag that churns food with acids.
  • Small Intestine: Long tube where digestion completes.
  • Large Intestine: Absorbs water.
  • Anus: Removes solid waste.
[Image: Digestive System Diagram]
🍎 Example: Like a mixer grinder turning fruit into juice.

3. Nutrients Absorption

Digestion is useless if the body doesn't take the good parts!

  • Happens in the Small Intestine.
  • Blood picks up nutrients from the walls of the intestine.
  • Blood carries these nutrients to every cell.

4. Respiratory System

Helps us breathe.

  • Nose: Filters air with hair.
  • Windpipe (Trachea): Tube for air.
  • Lungs: Two balloons that fill with air.
  • Diaphragm: Muscle below lungs that helps pumping.
  • Inhale: Oxygen in. Exhale: Carbon Dioxide out.
[Image: Lungs and Windpipe]

5. Circulatory System & Blood

The transport system of the body.

  • Heart: A muscular pump (size of your fist).
  • Blood Vessels: Pipes (Arteries, Veins, Capillaries).
  • Arteries: Carry pure blood (Oxygen-rich) FROM heart.
  • Veins: Carry impure blood (CO2-rich) TO heart.
  • Blood: Red liquid. Red Blood Cells (carry O2), White Blood Cells (fight germs), Platelets (stop bleeding).
🫀 Tip: A for Artery, A for Away (from heart).

6. Nervous System

The "Boss" of the body.

  • Brain: Controls everything.
  • Cerebrum: Thinking part (Big brain).
  • Cerebellum: Balance part (Small brain).
  • Medulla: Automatic actions (Breathing, Heartbeat).
  • Spinal Cord: Thick nerve cable in the back.
  • Nerves: Wires connecting brain to body.
[Image: Brain Parts]

7. Sense Organs

Windows to the world.

  • Eyes: Sight (Optic nerve).
  • Ears: Hearing & Balance.
  • Nose: Smell.
  • Tongue: Taste (Sweet, Salty, Sour, Bitter).
  • Skin: Touch (Feel heat, cold, pain).

8. Skeletal System

The framework of bones.

  • Adults have 206 bones.
  • Skull: Protects brain.
  • Ribcage: Protects heart & lungs.
  • Backbone (Spine): Protects spinal cord.
  • Femur: Thigh bone (Longest bone).
  • Joints: Where two bones meet (Elbow, Knee).
🦴 Fact: Babies have more bones than adults!

9. Muscular System

Helps in movement.

  • Voluntary Muscles: Under our control (Hands, Legs).
  • Involuntary Muscles: Work automatically (Heart, Stomach).
  • Muscles work in pairs: One pulls, one relaxes.

10. Excretory & Urinary Systems

Cleaning crew.

  • Kidneys: Bean-shaped. Filter blood to make urine.
  • Skin: Removes sweat (salt & water).
  • Lungs: Remove Carbon Dioxide.
  • Ureters, Bladder, Urethra: Path of urine.

11. Health & Care

  • Posture: Sit straight to keep backbone healthy.
  • Exercise: Makes heart and muscles strong.
  • Rest: 8 hours sleep is needed for the brain.
  • Balanced Diet: Eating all nutrients in right amount.
