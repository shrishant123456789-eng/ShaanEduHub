Chapter Notes

1. What is an Adjective?

Adjectives are Describing Words. They tell us more about a Noun (Person, Place, Animal, or Thing).

Example: A red apple. (Red describes the apple)
💡 Tip: Ask "How is it?" to find the adjective.
[attachment_0](attachment)
2. Describing People

We can use words to describe how people look or feel.

Example: A tall man. A happy girl.
3. Describing Animals

We use words to tell about an animal's size, color, or nature.

Example: A wild lion. A cute rabbit.
4. Colors & Shapes

Colors (Red, Blue) and Shapes (Round, Square) are also adjectives!

Example: The sky is blue. The ball is round.
💡 Tip: Numbers (One, Two) are describing words too!
5. Quality Words

These words tell us about the quality (Good or Bad) of something.

Example: A brave soldier. A sweet mango.
6. Comparison (Introduction)

When we compare two things, we change the adjective slightly.

Big ➜ Bigger
Small ➜ Smaller

Quick Revision

  • Adjectives describe Nouns.
  • They tell us about Size (Big, Small).
  • They tell us about Color (Red, Green).
  • They tell us about Number (One, Many).
  • They tell us about Quality (Good, Bad).
  • They usually come BEFORE the noun (A red car).
