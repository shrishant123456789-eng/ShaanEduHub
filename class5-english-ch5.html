English Grammar

Chapter 5: Articles, Determiners & Prepositions

Class 5 • Term 2

📝 My Study Notes

1. Articles (A, An, The)

Articles are small words that introduce a noun. They tell us if the noun is general or specific.

A / An (Indefinite Articles): Used for one person or thing (singular) that is not specific.

  • A: Used before words starting with a consonant sound (b, c, d, f, etc.).
    Example: A cat, A university (sound is 'yoo').
  • An: Used before words starting with a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u).
    Example: An apple, An hour (h is silent).

The (Definite Article): Used for specific things, unique things (Sun, Moon), or rivers/mountains.

💡 Tip: Look at the SOUND, not just the spelling! "Uniform" starts with 'U' but sounds like 'Y', so we say "A uniform".
Daily Life Example:
Please give me a pen. (Any pen will do)
Please give me the red pen. (Specifically that red one)
[ DIAGRAM: A vs AN vs THE ]

2. Determiners

Words that determine or "point out" nouns.

  • This / That: Singular. (This book here, That star there).
  • These / Those: Plural. (These apples here, Those birds there).
  • Some / Any: "Some" for positive sentences ("I have some water"). "Any" for questions or negatives ("Do you have any water?").
  • Much / Many: "Much" for things you can't count (water, sugar). "Many" for things you can count (boys, cars).

3. Prepositions of Time

Tell us WHEN something happens.

  • AT: Precise time (At 5:00 PM, At night).
  • ON: Days and Dates (On Sunday, On 15th August).
  • IN: Months, Years, Seasons (In July, In 2024, In winter).
⏰ Tip: AT clock time, ON calendar day, IN long period.

4. Place & Direction

Place (Where?): In (inside), On (surface), Under (below), Between (middle of two), Among (middle of many).

Direction (Movement): To (destination), Into (entering), Towards (facing), Across (one side to other).

Watch Out!
He jumped in the pool? NO! He jumped into the pool (movement).
He is swimming in the pool (location).
