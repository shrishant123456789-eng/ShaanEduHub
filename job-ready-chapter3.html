3.1 Interview Fundamentals

Understanding Interview Types

Interviews aren't just Q&A. They are strategic conversations. In India, most recruitment drives (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, or Startups) follow a specific funnel.

  • Screening/HR Round: Basic filter. Checks confidence, communication, and willingness to relocate.
  • Technical Round: Core skills (Coding, Domain Knowledge). The "Elimination Round".
  • Managerial/Behavioral: Culture fit, problem-solving, and team dynamics.

The STAR Framework (Crucial)

The gold standard for answering behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time when...").

  • S - Situation: Set the scene. "During my final year project..."
  • T - Task: What was the challenge? "We had to build a React app in 2 days."
  • A - Action: What did YOU do? "I organized the team and coded the API integration."
  • R - Result: Outcome. "We delivered on time and got an A grade."
🇮🇳 Campus Example:
Interviewer: "Have you faced a conflict?"
Bad Answer: "Yes, my friend didn't work so I did it."
STAR Answer: "In our college fest (Situation), a volunteer backed out (Task). I reallocated duties (Action), and the event ran smoothly (Result)."
First Impressions Science

Research shows decisions are often made in the first 90 seconds.

  • Attire: Formal for MNCs, Smart Casual for Startups.
  • Punctuality: Login 10 mins early for online, arrive 15 mins early for offline.
  • The Greeting: "Good Morning [Name]" with a smile. Handshake (firm, not weak).
💡 Tip: Always have a specific answer for "Tell me about yourself". Do not recite your resume. Tell your story.

3.2 Behavioral & Technical Strategy

Competency Mapping

Interviewers have a checklist. They tick boxes for specific competencies.

📝

[GRID: Leadership | Teamwork | Adaptability | Ethics]

Map your stories to these traits. If they ask about "Failure", they are testing Resilience.

Body Language Signals

Your non-verbal cues speak louder than words.

  • Eye Contact: Look at the camera (virtual) or the person (physical). Triangle method (Eye-Eye-Forehead).
  • Posture: Sit straight. Leaning back = arrogant/bored. Leaning too forward = aggressive.
  • Hands: Keep them visible. Don't fidget or touch your face.

3.3 Virtual Interview Optimization

The Setup Checklist

Virtual interviews are the norm now. Don't let tech fail you.

  • Lighting: Light source MUST be in front of you, not behind. Window facing is best.
  • Camera: Eye level. Use books to raise your laptop. No "nostril views".
  • Background: Plain wall or blurred. No unmade beds or messy kitchens.
🇮🇳 Real Reality:
Power cuts happen. Have a mobile hotspot ready as backup. If background noise (pressure cooker/traffic) occurs, apologize immediately and mute when not speaking.

3.4 Expert-Level Strategy

Offer & Negotiation

Getting the offer is step one. Understanding it is step two.

  • CTC vs In-Hand: CTC includes PF, Gratuity, Medical Insurance. Your take-home might be 70% of CTC.
  • Negotiation: Never say "Yes" immediately. Say "I am excited, but I need to review the details."
  • Market Standard: Research salaries on Glassdoor/LinkedIn before quoting a number.
💡 Tip: If asked "What is your expected salary?", try to reverse it: "What is the budget for this role?" or give a range.
