Chapter 3: Keyboard and Mouse

📘 Let's Read & Learn

🔤 Alphabet Keys (A to Z)
The Alphabet Keys are the buttons with letters from A to Z on your keyboard. You use these keys to type words, names, and sentences. When you press the 'A' key, the letter 'a' appears on the screen. When you want to write "Cat", you press the 'C', 'a', and 't' keys one by one. These are the keys you will use most when writing stories or typing your name!
🔢 Number Keys (0 to 9)
The Number Keys have digits from 0 to 9 printed on them. You can usually find them in a row at the top of your keyboard. These keys help you type numbers for math problems, write your age, or type a phone number. When you press '5', the number 5 appears. If you need to write "123", you press '1', then '2', then '3'.
⭐ Special Keys
Special Keys help you do special jobs while typing:

• Spacebar: This is the long horizontal key at the bottom. It creates an empty space between words. Use it between "Hello" and "World" to write "Hello World".

• Enter: This is a big key with a bent arrow symbol. It takes you to a new line, like starting a new page in your notebook.

• Backspace: This key has an arrow pointing left. It works like an eraser! It removes the letter to the left of your cursor (the blinking line).

• Delete: This key removes the letter to the right of your cursor. It is like magic erasing for the next letter.

• Arrow Keys: These are four keys with arrows pointing Up, Down, Left, and Right. They help you move the cursor around without using the mouse, like a mini remote control!
🔒 Caps Lock Key
The Caps Lock key is like a shift-lock for your keyboard. When you press it once, all the alphabet keys will type BIG CAPITAL LETTERS like A, B, C instead of small letters a, b, c. Press it again to turn it off and return to small letters. This is helpful when you need to write your name or a title in all capitals!
🖱️ Mouse Buttons
Your computer mouse has three important parts:

• Left Button: This is the button you press most often. You use it to select things, click on items, and choose what you want.

• Right Button: This button opens a special menu box with extra choices when you click it.

• Scroll Wheel: This is the small round wheel between the two buttons. When you spin it with your finger, it moves the page up and down, helping you see more content without clicking!
✋ Mouse Actions
Here is how you use the mouse to do different things:

• Click: Press the left button once and let go quickly. This selects an item or puts your cursor in place.

• Double-click: Press the left button two times very quickly. This opens items, like opening a book to read it.

• Drag and Drop: Hold down the left button, keep it pressed while you move the mouse to carry an item, then let go (drop) to place it somewhere else. It is like picking up a toy and moving it to a new box!
