⚡ Quick Revision Rules

  • Accuracy: Reading words correctly without mistakes.
  • Fluency: Reading smoothly like a river flow.
  • Silent Reading: Reading in your mind (Libraries).
  • Reading Aloud: Using voice and expression (Plays).
  • Paragraph: A group of sentences about ONE topic.
  • Comprehension: Understanding the WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHEN.
