📖 Chapter Notes
1. Types of Animals
Animals are living beings. They are of different types based on where they live and how they help us.
  • 🏠 Domestic Animals: Kept near our homes. Help us in many ways.
  • 🦁 Wild Animals: Live in jungles. We cannot keep them at home.
  • 🐶 Pet Animals: Kept inside homes for fun and company.
  • 🚜 Farm Animals: Kept on farms for milk, eggs, or work.
Example: Cow (Domestic), Lion (Wild), Dog (Pet), Hen (Farm).
💡 Tip: We build homes for domestic animals, but wild animals build their own homes.
❓ Quick Check: Is a Tiger a pet animal? (No)

2. Land, Water and Air Animals
Animals live in different places.
  • Land Animals: Live on ground. Have legs to walk. (Horse, Cat)
  • Water Animals (Aquatic): Live in water. Have fins to swim. (Fish, Octopus)
  • Air Animals (Aerial): Fly in the air. Have wings. (Birds, Bats)
  • Amphibians: Live on both land and water. (Frog, Crocodile)
Example: Fish dies if taken out of water.
💡 Tip: Frogs have webbed feet to swim and strong legs to jump on land.
❓ Quick Check: Where does a Whale live? (Water)
Image: Fish in water, Bird in sky, Horse on land
3. Food Habits of Animals
Different animals eat different food.
  • 🌿 Herbivores: Plant-eaters. (Cow, Goat, Elephant)
  • 🍖 Carnivores: Flesh-eaters. Hunt other animals. (Lion, Tiger, Wolf)
  • 🍛 Omnivores: Eat both plants and flesh. (Bear, Crow, Human)
Example: A cow eats grass (Herbivore). A lion eats deer (Carnivore).
💡 Tip: Scavengers like Vultures eat dead animals and keep the earth clean.
❓ Quick Check: What does a Bear eat? (Both plants and meat)
Image: Cow eating grass vs Lion eating meat
4. Homes of Animals
Animals need homes to protect themselves from heat, cold, and rain.
  • Lion → Den
  • Dog → Kennel
  • Cow → Shed
  • Horse → Stable
  • Hen → Coop
  • Bird → Nest
  • Rabbit → Burrow
Example: Birds use twigs and grass to make nests.
💡 Tip: A spider spins a web to live and catch insects.
❓ Quick Check: Where does a horse live? (Stable)
Image: Bird in Nest and Lion in Den
5. Young Ones of Animals
Babies of animals have special names.
  • Cow → Calf
  • Dog → Puppy
  • Cat → Kitten
  • Lion → Cub
  • Sheep → Lamb
  • Hen → Chick
  • Duck → Duckling
Example: A tadpole grows up to become a Frog.
💡 Tip: The young one of a Goat is called a Kid.
❓ Quick Check: What is a baby Cat called? (Kitten)
Image: Mother Dog with Puppies
6. Sounds of Animals
Animals talk to each other using sounds.
  • Dog → Barks
  • Lion → Roars
  • Cat → Mews
  • Cow → Moos
  • Bird → Chirps
  • Duck → Quacks
  • Elephant → Trumpets
Example: We can hear birds chirping in the morning.
💡 Tip: Snake sounds are called Hiss.
❓ Quick Check: Who trumpets? (Elephant)
Image: Cartoon animals with sound bubbles
7. Uses of Animals
Animals are very useful to us.
  • Cow, Buffalo, Goat: Give us Milk.
  • Hen, Duck: Give us Eggs.
  • Sheep: Gives us Wool for winter clothes.
  • Honeybee: Gives us Honey.
  • Horse, Donkey, Camel: Carry loads for us.
Example: Oxen are used by farmers to plough fields.
💡 Tip: Leather is made from the skin of dead animals (Camel, Snake).
❓ Quick Check: Who gives us wool? (Sheep)
Image: Milk, Wool, and Honey products
8. Special Features
Some animals have special body parts.
  • Elephant: Has a long trunk to lift things and drink water.
  • Giraffe: Has a long neck to reach high trees.
  • Camel: Has a hump to store fat. Can live without water for days.
  • Tortoise/Snail: Have a hard shell for protection.
  • Fish: Have gills to breathe under water.
Example: Birds have hollow bones to fly easily.
💡 Tip: The Camel is called the 'Ship of the Desert'.
❓ Quick Check: Why does a Giraffe have a long neck? (To eat leaves from tall trees)
Image: Giraffe eating leaves
9. Care of Animals
We must look after our pets and farm animals.
  • Give them healthy food and clean water.
  • Bathe them regularly.
  • Clean their homes (shed, kennel).
  • Take them to a veterinary doctor if they are sick.
Example: Brushing a dog's fur keeps it clean.
💡 Tip: Never tease or hurt animals with stones.
❓ Quick Check: Who is an animal doctor? (Vet)
Image: Child feeding a dog
10. Protection of Animals
Wild animals are in danger because people hunt them.
  • We should not cut forests (their homes).
  • Hunting animals for skin or teeth is bad.
  • National Parks are safe places for wild animals.
Example: Tigers are protected in special forests.
💡 Tip: 'Save Animals, Save Nature'.
❓ Quick Check: Should we hunt animals? (No)
