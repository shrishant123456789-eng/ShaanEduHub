🎨 Paint & Fun

Class 2 • Chapter 9 • Drawing & Coloring
🎨 What is Paint?

Paint is a special drawing program on the computer.
It is like a Magic Drawing Book!
We can draw pictures, color them, and make fun shapes.
"Let's make a beautiful scenery today!" ✨

🚪 How to Open Paint?

Follow these easy steps:
1. Click the Start Button.
2. Type "Paint" or find the Paint Icon 🎨.
3. Click it, and the white canvas opens!

✏️ Drawing Tools

Paint has a tool box with many friends inside:

✏️ Pencil
🖌️ Brush
🧼 Eraser
🪣 Fill Color
⬜ Shapes
🛠️ Tool Magic

✏️ Pencil: Used for free drawing, just like a real pencil.
🖌️ Brush: Used to paint with thick and colorful lines.
⬜ Shapes: Draw circles, squares, and triangles easily.
🪣 Fill with Color: Fills a closed shape with color instantly!
🧼 Eraser: Made a mistake? Use this to clean it up!

💾 Saving Your Work

Don't lose your drawing! Save it.
1. Click File.
2. Click Save.
3. Give it a name (like "My House").
4. Click Save button. Done! ✅

🌈 Be Creative!

Paint helps your imagination grow.
You can draw a blue sun ☀️ or a pink tree 🌳!
There are no mistakes in art, only happy accidents.
"Drawing makes learning fun!"
