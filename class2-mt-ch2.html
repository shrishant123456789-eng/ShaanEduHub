📘 Study Notes

1. Respect Parents 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Respect means to care for someone and treat them nicely. Our parents love us and work hard for us.
We show respect by:

  • Listening to them. 👂
  • Helping them at home. 🏠
  • Speaking politely and softly. 🗣️

❓ Activity: Say "Thank You" to your parents today for the yummy food!

2. Respect Teachers 👩‍🏫

Teachers give us knowledge. They are like our second parents at school.
How to respect teachers?

  • Say "Good Morning" everyday. ☀️
  • Listen carefully in class. 📝
  • Do not talk when the teacher is teaching. 🤫

3. Respect Elders 👵👴

Elders are older people like Grandparents, Uncles, and Aunts. They have wisdom.
We should:

  • Greet them with a smile or Namaste. 🙏
  • Offer them a seat in a bus or room. 🪑
  • Help them carry heavy bags. 🛍️

4. Respect Friends 🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Friends make life happy! To be a good friend:
❤️ Share your toys and food.
❤️ Do not push or hit anyone.
❤️ If you hurt someone, say "Sorry".
Never tease or bully others. That is very bad behavior.

5. Obeying Parents & Teachers 📘

Obedience means doing what you are told to do immediately.
✅ If Mom says "Clean your room", you do it happily.
✅ If Teacher says "Open your book", you open it quickly.
Obeying keeps us safe and makes us good children.

6. Following Instructions 🧠➡️

Instructions are steps to do something right.
Example: "Stand in a line", "Wash hands before eating".
If we do not follow instructions, we might make mistakes or get hurt. Always listen to the steps!

7. Respecting Belongings 🎒

Belongings are things that belong to someone.
🚫 Never take your friend's pencil without asking.
🚫 Do not tear pages from library books.
✅ Always ask "May I take this?" before touching others' things.

8. Respecting National Symbols 🇮🇳

We love our country, India. We must respect its symbols.
🇮🇳 National Flag (Tiranga): Never let it fall on the ground.
🎵 National Anthem (Jana Gana Mana): Always stand straight and still when it is played. Do not talk or move.
