शब्द ज्ञान एवं शब्द भंडार

Class 3 – Hindi
"शब्द ही भाषा के मोती हैं"

📘 Chapter Notes

Welcome students! In this chapter, we will learn about different types of words in Hindi to make our language strong.

1. Noun Identification (संज्ञा)

Definition: Noun (Sangya) is the name of a person, place, animal, or thing.

Everything around us has a name. That name is called a Noun.

  • Example: Ram, Agra, Table, Lion.
💡 Tip: If you can name it, it is a Noun (Sangya)!
❓ Check: Is 'Running' a noun? (No, it is an action)

2. Names of Person, Place, Thing

Let's look at specific categories:

  • Person (व्यक्ति): Mohan, Sita, Teacher, Doctor.
  • Place (स्थान): School, Park, Delhi, Home.
  • Thing (वस्तु): Pen, Book, Chair, Ball.
[Image showing Boy, School, and Pencil]
💡 Tip: Ask "Who is this?" for person and "Where is this?" for place.
❓ Check: 'Mumbai' is a Person or Place? (Place)

3. One Word Substitution (अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द)

Sometimes we use one single word instead of a long sentence.

  • One who teaches (जो पढ़ाता है) = Teacher (अध्यापक)
  • One who treats patients (जो इलाज करता है) = Doctor (डॉक्टर)

💡 Tip: Short words make our language smart!
❓ Check: One who grows crops? (Farmer/Kisan)

4. Synonyms (समानार्थी/पर्यायवाची शब्द)

Words that have the same meaning are called Synonyms.

  • Water (पानी) = Jal (जल), Neer (नीर)
  • Sun (सूरज) = Surya (सूर्य), Ravi (रवि)
  • Flower (फूल) = Pushp (पुष्प), Suman (सुमन)
[Image showing Sun with labels: Suraj, Surya]
💡 Tip: Synonyms are like friends with different names but same heart.
❓ Check: Another name for 'Hawa' (Air)? (Pawan)

5. Antonyms (विलोम शब्द)

Words that have the opposite meaning are called Antonyms.

  • Day (दिन) ↔ Night (रात)
  • Good (अच्छा) ↔ Bad (बुरा)
  • Up (ऊपर) ↔ Down (नीचे)
[Image showing Day vs Night split]
💡 Tip: Think of opposites like Happy vs Sad.
❓ Check: Opposite of 'Big'? (Small)

6. Gender (लिंग)

Words that tell us if someone is Male or Female.

  • Pulling (Male): Boy (लड़का), Father (पिता), Lion (शेर).
  • Striling (Female): Girl (लड़की), Mother (माता), Lioness (शेरनी).
[Image showing Raja and Rani]
💡 Tip: Most 'aa' ending sounds are Male, 'ee' ending are Female (Example: Ladka - Ladki).
❓ Check: Gender of 'Raja'? (Male/Pulling)

7. Number (वचन)

Words that tell us if something is One or Many.

  • Ek-vachan (Singular): One Book (किताब), One Child (बच्चा).
  • Bahu-vachan (Plural): Many Books (किताबें), Many Children (बच्चे).
[Image showing 1 Ball vs 3 Balls]
💡 Tip: Generally 'a' becomes 'e' (Kela -> Kele).
❓ Check: Plural of 'Pankha'? (Pankhe)

📝 Important Questions

Q1. What is a Noun (Sangya)?

Ans: Name of a person, place, or thing. Exp: Noun is a naming word.

Q2. Give two examples of 'Place' nouns.

Ans: School, Market. Exp: Any location name is a place noun.

Q3. What is the synonym of 'Suraj'?

Ans: Surya or Ravi. Exp: Both mean the Sun.

Q4. Write the antonym of 'Day' (Din).

Ans: Night (Raat). Exp: Day and Night are opposites.

Q5. Change gender: 'Brother'.

Ans: Sister. Exp: Male is Brother, Female is Sister.

Q6. Change number: 'Kela' (Banana).

Ans: Kele (Bananas). Exp: One Kela, Many Kele.

Q7. One word for: One who sings.

Ans: Singer (Gayak). Exp: A person who sings songs.

Q8. Is 'Happy' a noun?

Ans: No. Exp: Happy is a feeling (adjective), not a name of a thing.

Q9. Synonym of 'Vriksh'.

Ans: Ped (Tree). Exp: Both mean Tree.

Q10. Antonym of 'Friend' (Mitra).

Ans: Enemy (Shatru). Exp: Friend and Enemy are opposites.

Q11. Gender of 'Morni'?Ans: Female (Striling).

Q12. Plural of 'Aankh' (Eye)?Ans: Aankhein.

Q13. One word: One who flies plane.Ans: Pilot.

Q14. Is 'Ganga' a noun?Ans: Yes, it is a name of a river.

Q15. Synonym of 'Aakash'?Ans: Gagan or Aasman (Sky).

Q16. Antonym of 'Sacch' (Truth)?Ans: Jhooth (Lie).

Q17. Change Gender: 'Chacha'.Ans: Chachi.

Q18. Change Number: 'Ghoda'.Ans: Ghode.

Q19. Identify noun: 'He runs fast'.Ans: None (Pronoun/Verb used). If 'Ram runs', Ram is noun.

Q20. Synonym of 'Putra'.Ans: Beta (Son).

Q21. Antonym of 'Rich' (Ameer).Ans: Poor (Gareeb).

Q22. Feminine of 'Bail' (Ox).Ans: Gaay (Cow).

Q23. Plural of 'Raat'.Ans: Raatein.

Q24. One word: Place where we study.Ans: School (Vidyalaya).

Q25. Is 'Table' a living thing?Ans: No, it is a non-living thing (Vastu).

Q26. Synonym of 'Mata'.Ans: Maa or Janani.

Q27. Antonym of 'Heavy' (Bhaari).Ans: Light (Halka).

Q28. Gender of 'River' (Nadi)?Ans: Female (Striling).

Q29. Plural of 'Ladki'.Ans: Ladkiyan.

Q30. Is 'Agra' a common noun or proper noun?Ans: Proper Noun (Vishesh Sangya).

Quick Recall

  • Sangya (Noun): Name of Person, Place, Thing.
  • Synonyms: Words with same meaning (Jal = Pani).
  • Antonyms: Words with opposite meaning (Din x Raat).
  • Gender: Male (Raja) vs Female (Rani).
  • Number: One (Taara) vs Many (Taare).
  • One Word: Jo padhata hai = Adhyapak.
