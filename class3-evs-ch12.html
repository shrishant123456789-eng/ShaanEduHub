📖 Study Notes

Read carefully to become an Earth Hero!

1. Meaning of Environment

Definition: Everything around us is called our environment. It includes living things (plants, animals, people) and non-living things (air, water, land, sun).

🖼️ Image: A park with kids, trees, sun, and birds

Example: Your classroom, home, and garden are all part of your environment.

🧠 Memory Tip: Environment = Everything around ME!

❓ Check: Is the sun part of the environment? (Yes)

2. Natural and Man-made Things

Explanation: Things in our environment are of two types.

  • Natural: Made by nature. Humans cannot make them. (Examples: Trees, Rivers, Mountains, Sun)
  • Man-made: Made by humans using tools. (Examples: Cars, Buildings, Books, Toys)
🖼️ Image: A mountain (Natural) vs A car (Man-made)
❓ Check: Is a plastic bottle natural or man-made? (Man-made)

3. Importance of Clean Environment

Why clean? A clean environment keeps us healthy and happy. Dirty surroundings bring germs and diseases.

Animals too: Animals and plants also need clean air and water to live safely.

🖼️ Image: Happy kids playing in a clean park

🧠 Memory Tip: Clean Earth = Happy Health.

4. Reduce, Reuse and Recycle (The 3 Rs)

To save nature, we follow the 3 Rs:

  • Reduce: Use less. (Do not waste food or water).
  • Reuse: Use things again. (Use a glass bottle for water instead of buying plastic).
  • Recycle: Make new things from old waste. (Old newspapers made into new paper bags).
🖼️ Image: The 3R Symbol (Arrows in a triangle)

5. Saving Water

Water is precious. Life is not possible without water.

How to save?

  • Close the tap while brushing teeth.
  • Fix leaking pipes immediately.
  • Use a bucket to bathe instead of a shower.
🖼️ Image: A child closing a tap tightly
❓ Check: Should we leave the tap open while washing hands? (No)

6. Saving Electricity

Electricity is made from natural resources like coal and water. We must not waste it.

Action: Turn off lights and fans when you leave a room. Open windows for natural light.

🖼️ Image: Finger pressing a switch to 'OFF'

7. Avoiding Pollution

Pollution means making the environment dirty.

  • Air Pollution: Smoke from cars and factories.
  • Water Pollution: Throwing garbage in rivers.
  • Land Pollution: Throwing plastic on the ground.
  • Noise Pollution: Very loud honking or music.
🖼️ Image: Smoke coming from a car (Bad!)

8. Caring for Nature

Nature gives us food, water, and air. We must be kind to it.

How? Do not pluck flowers. Do not hurt animals. Plant more trees (Afforestation).

🖼️ Image: Kids watering a small plant

🧠 Memory Tip: Trees are our best friends!

9. Role of Humans in Protecting Earth

Humans are the smartest beings. It is our duty to protect the Earth, not destroy it.

If we destroy nature, we destroy our own home.

🖼️ Image: Hands holding the Earth globe gently

10. Good Environmental Habits

Simple habits make a big difference:

  1. Always throw waste in dustbins.
  2. Use cloth bags instead of plastic bags.
  3. Walk or cycle for short distances.
  4. Do not burst crackers (fireworks) that cause smoke.
🖼️ Image: A boy using a cloth bag for shopping
