📝 Detailed Study Notes

1. What is a Fraction?

A fraction represents equal parts of a whole or a collection.

NumeratorDenominator

  • Numerator (Top): How many parts we have.
  • Denominator (Bottom): Total number of equal parts.

Example: 34 means 3 parts out of 4.

💡 Memory Tip: "D" for Denominator, "D" for Down!

2. Fractions on a Number Line

Fractions live between whole numbers (like 0 and 1).

To show 12, we divide the space between 0 and 1 into 2 equal parts.

0 —— [1/2] —— 1

3. Types of Fractions

We classify fractions based on their values:

  • Proper Fraction: Numerator < Denominator. (Small on top). E.g., 25
  • Improper Fraction: Numerator > Denominator. (Big on top). E.g., 73
  • Mixed Number: Whole number + Proper fraction. E.g., 212
  • Unit Fraction: Numerator is always 1. E.g., 19

4. Like & Unlike Fractions

  • Like Fractions: Have the SAME Denominator. E.g., 27, 57
  • Unlike Fractions: Have DIFFERENT Denominators. E.g., 23, 45

Note: We can only add/subtract easily if they are Like Fractions!

5. Converting Fractions

Improper to Mixed: Divide Numerator by Denominator.

Convert 92

9 ÷ 2 = 4 (Quotient) and 1 (Remainder).

Answer: 412

Mixed to Improper: (Whole × Denom) + Num / Denom.

Convert 312

(3 × 2) + 1 = 7

Answer: 72

6. Equivalent Fractions

Different numbers representing the same value. E.g., 12 = 24.

To Find: Multiply top and bottom by the same number.

To Check (Cross Multiply):

Are 23 and 46 equivalent?

2 × 6 = 12

3 × 4 = 12

Yes! They are equal.

7. Comparing & Ordering

Case 1: Like Fractions (Same Denominator)

Just look at the Numerator! Bigger numerator = Bigger Fraction.

58 > 38

Case 2: Unlike Fractions (Different Denominator)

Method: Cross Multiply.

Compare 34 and 25

3 × 5 = 15 | 4 × 2 = 8

15 > 8, So 34 is bigger.

8. Addition & Subtraction

Rule: You can only add/subtract numerators if denominators are the SAME.

27 + 37 = 2+37 = 57

If denominators are different, find LCM first to make them Like Fractions.

9. Fraction of a Collection

To find a part of a number: Divide by Denominator, Multiply by Numerator.

Find 23 of 12 apples.

Step 1: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 (Value of one part)

Step 2: 4 × 2 = 8

Answer: 8 apples.

  • Mixed Number: Whole Number + Proper Fraction.
  • Like Fractions have SAME denominator.
  • Unlike Fractions have DIFFERENT denominator.
  • To compare unlike fractions: Cross Multiply.
