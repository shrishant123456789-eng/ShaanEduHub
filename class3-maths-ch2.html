1. Addition of Big Numbers! 🐘

Welcome back! In Class 2, we added small numbers. Now, let's add 4-Digit Numbers (Numbers up to 9999). It's easy! We just add friends together.

Imagine we are building a tower using blocks:

  • 🧱 Thousands (Th)
  • 🧱 Hundreds (H)
  • 🧱 Tens (T)
  • 🧱 Ones (O)

Always start adding from the Ones place (Right side) and move to the left!

2. The "Carry Over" Magic ✨

What happens when a room gets too full? We send people to the next room! That's Carry Over.

If the sum of any column is 10 or more, we keep the ones digit and carry the tens digit to the next neighbor.

Example: 58 + 44
8 + 4 = 12 (Keep 2, Carry 1)
1 + 5 + 4 = 10
Answer: 102

This works for Thousands too!

3. Horizontal & Vertical Addition 📏

We can add numbers in two ways:

Horizontal (Sleeping Line)
120 + 30 = 150
Vertical (Standing Line)
120
+ 30
-----
150

4. Word Problems (Math Stories) 📖

Math is everywhere! Let's read a story.

"Rahul has 1250 red marbles 🔴 and 1100 blue marbles 🔵. How many marbles in total?"

Solution:
Red Marbles = 1250
Blue Marbles = 1100
Total = 1250 + 1100 = 2350 Marbles

Look for words like: Total, Sum, Altogether, In all.

5. Number Line Addition 🛤️

We can jump forward on a number line to add.

50 + 5 = ?
Start at 50 -> Jump 5 steps -> Land on 55!

6. Mental Math Tricks 🧠

Add numbers in your head without paper!

Break it down:
35 + 22 = ?
30 + 20 = 50
5 + 2 = 7
Total = 50 + 7 = 57! ⚡ Fast!

7. Checking Addition ✔️

Did you get the right answer? Check it by reversing!

If A + B = C, then C - B should be A.

Ex: 20 + 30 = 50. Check: 50 - 30 = 20. Correct! ✅
