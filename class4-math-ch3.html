📝 My Study Notes

1. Multiplication Terms

Multiplication is repeated addition.

  • Multiplicand: The number to be multiplied.
  • Multiplier: The number by which we multiply.
  • Product: The answer we get.

15 (Multiplicand) × 5 (Multiplier) = 75 (Product)

💡 Remember: Learn tables 2 to 20 by heart for speed!

2. Properties of Multiplication

  • Order Property: A × B = B × A (e.g., 4 × 5 = 20 and 5 × 4 = 20).
  • Grouping Property: (A × B) × C = A × (B × C).
  • Property of 1: Number × 1 = Same Number.
  • Property of 0: Number × 0 = 0 (Always Zero!).

3. Multiplying by 10, 100, 1000

This is a magic trick! No calculation needed.

  • × 10: Put ONE zero at the end. (5 × 10 = 50)
  • × 100: Put TWO zeros at the end. (5 × 100 = 500)
  • × 1000: Put THREE zeros at the end. (5 × 1000 = 5000)

45 × 100 = 4,500 (Just add 00)

4. Lattice Multiplication Method

A fun way to multiply using a grid/box!

  1. Draw a box and divide it based on digits.
  2. Draw diagonals in each small box.
  3. Multiply rows and columns.
  4. Add along the diagonals.
[Grid Box Diagram Concept]
For 12 × 34, we use a 2x2 grid.

5. Division Terms

Division is equal sharing or repeated subtraction.

  • Dividend: The big number to be divided.
  • Divisor: The number dividing it.
  • Quotient: The answer.
  • Remainder: What is left over (must be less than Divisor).

20 ÷ 6 = 3 (Quotient), Remainder = 2

6. Checking Division

We can check our answer using this secret formula:

🔑 Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder

If LHS = RHS, your answer is Correct!

7. Unitary Method

Finding the value of ONE unit first, then finding the value of MANY.

Steps:

  1. Many → One (Divide)
  2. One → Many (Multiply)

Cost of 5 pens = ₹50.

1. Cost of 1 pen = 50 ÷ 5 = ₹10.

2. Cost of 3 pens = 10 × 3 = ₹30.

8. Tests of Divisibility

Quick check without dividing!

Divisible by Rule Example
2 Ends with 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 (Even No.) 124
5 Ends with 0 or 5 135
10 Ends with 0 500

⭐ Important Questions (Min 30)

Q1: What is the result of multiplication called?

Ans: Product

Q2: 125 × 0 = ?

Ans: 0 (Property of Zero)

Q3: 999 × 1 = ?

Ans: 999 (Identity Property)

Q4: Identify Dividend in 15 ÷ 3 = 5.

Ans: 15

Q5: Formula to check division?

Ans: Dividend = (Divisor × Quotient) + Remainder

Q6: Remainder must always be smaller than ________?

Ans: Divisor

Q7: 45 × 1000 = ?

Ans: 45,000

Q8: Is 452 divisible by 2?

Ans: Yes, because it ends in 2 (Even).

Q9: Is 452 divisible by 5?

Ans: No, it does not end in 0 or 5.

Q10: If cost of 1 chocolate is ₹10, cost of 5?

Ans: 10 × 5 = ₹50

Q11: 50 × 40 = ?

Ans: 5×4=20, add two zeros → 2000

Q12: Largest 2 digit number × smallest 2 digit number?

Ans: 99 × 10 = 990

Q13: Division by Zero is possible?

Ans: No, it is not defined.

Q14: 0 ÷ 100 = ?

Ans: 0

Q15: 8 × (3 + 2) = ?

Ans: 8 × 5 = 40

Q16: Find Quotient: 8000 ÷ 10.

Ans: 800 (Remove one zero)

Q17: Number divisible by 10 is always divisible by 5. T/F?

Ans: True (Ends in 0).

Q18: How many wheels in 12 cars?

Ans: 12 × 4 = 48 wheels.

Q19: Distribute 20 sweets among 4 kids.

Ans: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 sweets each.

Q20: Product of 12 and 9?

Ans: 108

Q21: Is 255 divisible by 10?

Ans: No (Doesn't end in 0).

Q22: 15 × 200 = ?

Ans: 3000

Q23: If Remainder is 0, Divisor is a _____ of Dividend?

Ans: Factor

Q24: Lattice multiplication uses a _____?

Ans: Grid or Box

Q25: 144 ÷ 12 = ?

Ans: 12

Q26: Cost of 10 books is 500. Cost of 1?

Ans: 500 ÷ 10 = 50 (Unitary Method)

Q27: 25 × 25 = ?

Ans: 625

Q28: Odd numbers are divisible by 2. T/F?

Ans: False.

Q29: 7200 ÷ 100 = ?

Ans: 72

Q30: Double of 35?

Ans: 70 (35 × 2)
