Key Rules to Remember:

  • Prepositions: Show position (in, on, under) or time (at, on, in).
  • Conjunctions: Glue words (And = add, But = opposite, Or = choice).
  • Interjections: Sudden feelings (Wow! Ouch! Hurrah!).
  • Capital Letters: Always start sentences and Interjections with capitals.

My Study Notes 📖

1. What is a Preposition?

Definition: A preposition is a word that tells us where something is or when something happens. It shows the relationship between a noun and another part of the sentence.

Image: A cat sitting ON a table and a ball UNDER the table.

Example: The book is on the table.

Memory Tip: Pre-position = Position (Placement) before a noun.

❓ Check: "The cat is ___ the box." (in/but)
👉 Answer: in

2. Prepositions of Place, Time, Direction

  • Place (Where?): in, on, under, behind, near.
  • Time (When?): at (time), on (day), in (month/year).
  • Direction (Movement): to, into, towards, up, down.
Image: Clock showing 5 PM (AT), Calendar showing Monday (ON).

Example: I go to school at 8 AM.

Memory Tip: "AT" is for precise time, "ON" is for days (Sunday).

❓ Check: "We play ___ Sunday."
👉 Answer: on

3. Correct Use of Prepositions

Sometimes we get confused! Let's learn correct usage.

  • Between vs Among: 'Between' for 2 people. 'Among' for many people.
  • In vs Into: 'In' means inside (resting). 'Into' means movement entering inside.
Image: A boy jumping INTO a pool (movement).

Example: She walked into the room.

❓ Check: "Distribute sweets ___ the class." (between/among)
👉 Answer: among

4. What is a Conjunction?

Definition: Conjunctions are "Joining Words" or "Connectors". They act like glue to join words, phrases, or sentences.

Image: Two puzzle pieces being joined by glue labeled 'AND'.

Example: Bread and butter.

Memory Tip: Conjunction = Junction (where things meet).

❓ Check: "I like tea ___ not coffee."
👉 Answer: but

5. Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

For Class 4, we focus on:

  • AND: To add similar things (I like apples and bananas).
  • BUT: To show difference/opposite (He is tall but slow).
  • OR: To show choice (Do you want tea or juice?).
Image: A fork showing two paths labeled 'OR'.
❓ Check: "Run fast ___ you will miss the bus."
👉 Answer: or

6. Correlative Conjunctions (Pairs)

Definition: These always come in pairs. They are best friends!

  • Either... or: One of the two (Either Rohan or Sohan will go).
  • Neither... nor: None of the two (Neither hot nor cold).
Image: Scales balancing two items equally.

Example: You can either study or play.

❓ Check: "___ he nor she is wrong."
👉 Answer: Neither

7. What is an Interjection?

Definition: Words that express sudden strong feelings like joy, sorrow, or surprise.

Image: Emoji face with mouth open saying 'WOW!'.

Example: Hurrah! We won the match.

Memory Tip: They always take an Exclamation Mark (!).

❓ Check: Word for pain?
👉 Answer: Ouch!

8. Exclamatory Expressions

Different words for different feelings:

  • Joy: Hurrah!, Yippee!
  • Sorrow/Sad: Alas!, Oh no!
  • Surprise: Wow!, What!, Oh!
  • Pain: Ouch!
  • Silence: Hush!
Image: A library sign saying 'Hush!'.
❓ Check: "___! The dog is dead."
👉 Answer: Alas!

9. Practice Sentences

Let's put it all together!

  • Prep: The cat jumped over the wall.
  • Conj: He worked hard but failed.
  • Interj: Wow! What a beautiful painting.
Image: A sentence train connecting cars.
❓ Check: Identify the connector in "Do or Die".
👉 Answer: or
