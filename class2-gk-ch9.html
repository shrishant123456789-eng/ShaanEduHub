1. Days & Months 📅

Days of the Week: There are 7 days in a week.
Order: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
😊 Sunday is a holiday!

Months of the Year: There are 12 months in a year.
First: January. Last: December.
A Calendar helps us check dates and holidays.

2. Day & Night 🌞🌚

Our day is divided into parts:

  • Morning: The Sun rises. We wake up and go to school. ☀️
  • Afternoon: Sun is high in the sky. We eat lunch. 🍛
  • Evening: Sun sets. We play outside. 🏸
  • Night: It is dark. Moon and stars appear. We sleep. 🌙

3. The Three Main Seasons 🌧️❄️

In India, we have three big seasons:

1. Summer (Hot):
Sun shines brightly. We wear Cotton clothes. We love ice-creams, mangoes, and cold water. 🍦🥭

2. Rainy / Monsoon (Wet):
It rains heavily. Dark clouds come. We use Umbrellas and Raincoats. Peacocks dance! ☔🦚

3. Winter (Cold):
It is very cold. We wear Woolen clothes (sweaters, caps). We like hot soup and tea. ☕🧤

4. Telling Time ⌚

A clock tells us the time.

  • It has 2 hands: A Short hand (Hour hand) and a Long hand (Minute hand).
  • When the long hand is on 12 and short hand is on 3, it is 3 O'Clock.
