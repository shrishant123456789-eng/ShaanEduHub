Chapter 4: Verbs & Action Words

"Action is the foundational key to all success!"

📝 Chapter Notes

1. What is a Verb?

Definition: A verb is an action word. It tells us what someone or something does.

Verbs are very important in sentences. They show action, movement, or state of being. Without verbs, we cannot make complete sentences!

Examples: Run, Eat, Sleep, Play, Write, Jump, Read
💡 Memory Tip: Verb starts with V and so does Very important! Every sentence must have a verb!
[Image: Children playing and doing actions]

Quick Check

A verb is a/an _______ word.

2. Action Words (Run, Eat, Jump)

Definition: Words that show physical movement or activity.

Action words tell us what we do with our body. When you run, you move your legs fast. When you eat, you put food in your mouth. All these are verbs!

Examples:
• Run – The boy runs fast.
• Eat – I eat rice.
• Jump – The cat jumps high.
• Write – She writes in the notebook.
• Play – We play cricket.
💡 Memory Tip: If you can act it out with your body, it is an action verb!
[Image: Kids running, eating, jumping]

Quick Check

Which is an action word? (Sleep/Table)

3. Verbs in Daily Life

Definition: Actions we do every day from morning to night.

From the time we wake up until we sleep, we use many verbs! Brushing teeth, bathing, eating breakfast, going to school, reading books – all are verbs.

Morning Verbs: Wake up, Brush, Bathe, Eat, Wear
School Verbs: Read, Write, Learn, Play, Listen
Evening Verbs: Return, Rest, Drink, Study, Sleep
💡 Memory Tip: Make a list of 5 things you did today – they are all verbs!
[Image: Daily routine activities]

Quick Check

We _____ our teeth in the morning. (brush/eat)

4. Present Tense (Is, Am, Are)

Definition: These special verbs tell us about now – the present time.

We use 'am' with I, 'is' with he/she/it, and 'are' with you/we/they. These are called 'be' verbs. They show what is happening now or how someone is.

Rules:
• I am happy. (First person singular)
• He/She/It is good. (Third person singular)
• You/We/They are students. (Plural/You)
💡 Memory Tip: I = am, He/She = is, You/We/They = are!
[Image: Children studying present tense]

Quick Check

I ___ a student. (is/am/are)

5. Simple Present Tense

Definition: Actions we do regularly, every day, or habits.

Simple present tells us about things that happen daily, always, or usually. With he/she/it, we add 's' or 'es' to the verb.

Examples:
• I play cricket. (Regular action)
• She plays cricket. (Add 's' for she)
• He watches TV. (Add 'es' for verbs ending in ch)
• They go to school.
💡 Memory Tip: For he/she/it – give the verb a tail (s/es)!
[Image: Daily habits and routines]

Quick Check

She ____ to school daily. (go/goes)

6. Past Tense (Basic)

Definition: Actions that happened before, yesterday, or earlier.

Past tense tells us about finished actions. For many verbs, we add 'ed' to show past. Some verbs change completely (go→went, eat→ate).

Regular (add ed): Play→Played, Walk→Walked, Jump→Jumped
Irregular (change): Go→Went, Eat→Ate, Come→Came, Run→Ran
💡 Memory Tip: Think of yesterday! If it is finished, add 'ed' or change the word!
[Image: Yesterday activities - past tense]

Quick Check

Yesterday I ____ an apple. (eat/eats/ate)

7. Helping Verbs (Has, Have)

Definition: Verbs that help the main verb. They come before the main verb.

'Has' and 'have' show possession or actions completed. Use 'has' with he/she/it, 'have' with I/you/we/they. These are also called auxiliary verbs.

Examples:
• I have a pen.
• She has a book.
• They have eaten. (Have + eaten = present perfect)
• He has gone. (Has + gone = present perfect)
💡 Memory Tip: Has = He/She/It (all have letter 'i' except 'He', but remember: She has, He has, It has!)
[Image: Possessions - has/have]

Quick Check

We ___ a big house. (has/have)

8. Correct Verb Usage in Sentences

Definition: Choosing the right verb form for the subject and time.

The verb must match the subject (I, he, they) and the time (now, yesterday, tomorrow). Subject-Verb agreement is very important!

Correct Examples:
• She runs fast. (Not: She run)
• They play here. (Not: They plays)
• He went home. (Not: He goed)
• I am happy. (Not: I is)
💡 Memory Tip: Check the subject first, then check the time!
[Image: Correcting sentences]

Quick Check

Correct this: He go to school.

9. Verb-based Sentence Formation

Definition: Making complete sentences using verbs correctly.

A sentence needs: Subject + Verb + Object (sometimes). The verb is the heart of the sentence!

Structure:
• Subject: Ram, Sita, The dog, I, They
• Verb: eats, runs, plays, is, have
• Object: rice, fast, cricket, happy, a pen
Full Sentence: Ram eats rice. | They play cricket.
💡 Memory Tip: SVO - Subject Verb Object! Like: Dog (S) bites (V) bone (O)!

🎯 Important Questions

1. What is a verb?

Answer: A verb is an action word that tells us what someone or something does.
Verbs show action, state, or occurrence in sentences.

2. Give two examples of action words.

Answer: Run, Jump, Eat, Play, Write, Read (any two)
Any word showing physical activity is correct.

3. Fill in the blank: I ___ happy. (am/is/are)

Answer: am
We use 'am' with the subject 'I'.

4. What do we add to verbs for he/she/it in present tense?

Answer: We add 's' or 'es'.
Example: play becomes plays, watch becomes watches.

5. Write the past tense of 'play'.

Answer: played
Regular verbs add 'ed' for past tense.

6. Use 'has' in a sentence.

Answer: She has a doll. / He has a bat. / It has a tail.
'Has' is used with he, she, it, or singular nouns.

7. What is the difference between has and have?

Answer: 'Has' is for he/she/it; 'Have' is for I/you/we/they.
Has is singular, have is plural or used with I/you.

8. Give the past tense of 'go'.

Answer: went
'Go' is an irregular verb. It does not add 'ed'.

9. Identify the verb: "The bird flies."

Answer: flies
It shows the action of the bird.

10. Fill in: They ___ cricket. (play/plays)

Answer: play
'They' is plural, so use 'play' without 's'.

11. What are helping verbs?

Answer: Verbs that help the main verb (has, have, is, am, are).
They come before the main verb to give more meaning.

12. Past tense of 'eat'?

Answer: ate
Eat is an irregular verb.

13. Fill in: We ___ to school. (go/goes)

Answer: go
'We' takes plural verb form.

14. Give three daily life verbs.

Answer: Wake up, Brush, Eat, Bath, Study (any three)
Actions we do every day.

15. Correct the sentence: She have a pen.

Answer: She has a pen.
'She' requires 'has', not 'have'.

16. What tense is: "I played yesterday"?

Answer: Past tense
'Yesterday' indicates past time and 'played' has 'ed'.

17. Fill in: It ___ raining. (is/am/are)

Answer: is
'It' always takes 'is'.

18. Write the three forms: Go

Answer: Go – Went – Gone
Present – Past – Past Participle.

19. Make a sentence using 'running'.

Answer: He is running. / The boy is running. / I like running.
Any correct sentence with the action of running.

20. Fill in: You ___ a good student. (is/am/are)

Answer: are
'You' always takes 'are'.

21. Past tense of 'come'?

Answer: came
Irregular verb.

22. Identify the helping verb: "He has finished."

Answer: has
'Has' helps the main verb 'finished'.

23. Fill in: They ___ finished work. (has/have)

Answer: have
'They' takes 'have'.

24. What is the base form of 'went'?

Answer: go
'Went' is past tense of 'go'.

25. Fill in: Ram ___ to market. (go/goes)

Answer: goes
'Ram' is he (singular), so add 'es'.

26. Give example of present tense.

Answer: I play. / She sings. / They dance.
Action happening now or regularly.

27. Which is correct: She sleep / She sleeps?

Answer: She sleeps
Add 's' for she/he/it.

28. Past tense of 'run'?

Answer: ran
Irregular verb: run-ran-run.

29. Fill in: I ___ my homework. (do/does)

Answer: do
'I' takes 'do' (base form).

30. What is the verb in: "Children are playing"?

Answer: are playing (or playing)
'Are' is helping verb, 'playing' is main verb.

🔄 Quick Revision

Key Points to Remember:

  • Verb = Action Word (Shows what we do)
  • Every sentence must have a verb
  • Am/Is/Are = Present tense (be verbs)
  • I → am, He/She/It → is, You/We/They → are
  • Present tense: He/She/It + verb+s/es
  • Past tense: Mostly add 'ed'
  • Irregular past: go→went, eat→ate, run→ran
  • Has = He/She/It (singular)
  • Have = I/You/We/They (plural)
  • Subject + Verb + Object = Sentence
  • If you can act it out, it's an action verb
  • Daily verbs: wake, brush, eat, study, sleep
  • Helping verbs: has, have, is, am, are
  • Regular verbs add 'ed' in past
  • Verbs show time: past, present, future
🌟 Remember: No verb = No sentence! 🌟
