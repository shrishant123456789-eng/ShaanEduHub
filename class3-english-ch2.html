Chapter 2: Nouns & Naming Words

"Learning is the treasure that follows its owner everywhere."

📝 Chapter Notes

1. What is a Noun?

Definition: A noun is a naming word.

A noun names people, places, animals, and things. Everything we see around us has a name. That name is called a noun.

Examples: Rahul, Delhi, Dog, Book, School, Mother
💡 Memory Tip: Noun starts with 'N' and so does Name!
[Image: Various objects and people]

Quick Check

A noun is a _______ word.

2. Naming Words for People

Definition: Names of persons are nouns.

These are names we give to people around us. They can be family members, teachers, doctors, or any person.

Examples: Father, Mother, Teacher, Doctor, Rahul, Sita, Friend
💡 Memory Tip: People are persons who talk and walk!
[Image: Family members]

Quick Check

Which is a naming word for people? (Doctor/Apple)

3. Naming Words for Places

Definition: Names of locations are nouns.

Places are where we go or where things are located. Your school, home, park, and city are all places.

Examples: School, Park, India, Market, Hospital, Home, Delhi
💡 Memory Tip: Place has 'a' and Park has 'a'!
[Image: School building]

Is 'Hospital' a naming word for a place? (Yes/No)

4. Naming Words for Animals

Definition: Names of creatures are nouns.

All animals have names. Pets, wild animals, birds, and insects all have naming words.

Examples: Dog, Cat, Elephant, Cow, Lion, Parrot, Fish
💡 Memory Tip: Animals move and have legs (mostly)!
[Image: Domestic animals]

Quick Check

Is 'Lion' an animal name? (Yes/No)

5. Naming Words for Things

Definition: Names of objects are nouns.

Things are objects we can see and touch (mostly). They are not alive like people or animals.

Examples: Chair, Table, Pencil, Book, Water, Ball, Car
💡 Memory Tip: Things are objects you can hold!
[Image: School items]

Quick Check

6. Proper Nouns

Definition: Special names given to specific people, places, or things.

Proper nouns always begin with a CAPITAL letter. They are unique names. There is only one like it!

Examples: Amit, Mumbai, Ganga, Monday, January, India
💡 Memory Tip: Proper means special, like wearing proper clothes for a party!
[Image: Specific landmarks]

Quick Check

Is 'girl' a proper noun? (Yes/No)

7. Common Nouns

Definition: General names for people, places, or things.

Common nouns are general names that many things share. They do not start with capital letters (unless at the start of a sentence).

Examples: girl, city, river, dog, teacher, school
💡 Memory Tip: Common = General (shared by everyone)!
[Image: General categories]

Quick Check

Is 'City' a common noun? (Yes/No)

8. Singular Nouns

Definition: Nouns that name only ONE person, place, or thing.

When we talk about one thing only, it is singular. Count = 1.

Examples: one book, a pen, the boy, an apple
💡 Memory Tip: Single means one. Like a single piece of chocolate!
[Image: Single items]

Quick Check

Which is singular: Books or Book?

9. Plural Nouns

Definition: Nouns that name MORE THAN ONE person, place, or thing.

We make plurals by adding 's', 'es', or changing 'y' to 'ies'.

Examples: books, boxes, cities, children, teeth
Rules: cat→cats, box→boxes, city→cities
💡 Memory Tip: Plural = Plus (more than one)!
[Image: Multiple items]

Quick Check

What is the plural of box? (Add 'es')

10. Gender Nouns (Boy/Girl)

Definition: Nouns that tell if someone is male or female.

Boys and men are male. Girls and women are female. Some animals also have genders.

Male: boy, father, brother, uncle, lion, bull
Female: girl, mother, sister, aunt, lioness, cow
💡 Memory Tip: Gender tells us He or She!
[Image: Family members male/female]

Quick Check

Is 'Sister' male or female?

11. Use of Nouns in Sentences

Definition: We use nouns as the subject or object in sentences.

The noun is usually the doer of the action or the receiver. Every sentence must have a noun (or pronoun).

Examples:
"Ram eats mango." (Ram = doer, mango = object)
"The cat sleeps." (Cat = subject)
💡 Memory Tip: Find the action word (verb), then ask Who or What!

🎯 Important Questions

1. What is a noun?

Answer: A noun is a naming word.
It names people, places, animals, and things around us.

2. Give two examples of naming words for people.

Answer: Teacher, Doctor (or Mother, Father, Rahul, Sita)
Any name that refers to a person is correct.

3. Is 'Delhi' a naming word for a place?

Answer: Yes
Delhi is the name of a city in India.

4. What type of noun is 'dog'?

Answer: Common noun and naming word for animal.
It is a general name for that animal, not a specific name like Tommy.

5. Write the plural of 'book'.

Answer: books
We add 's' to make most nouns plural.

6. What is a proper noun? Give example.

Answer: A special name for a specific person, place, or thing. Example: Amit, Mumbai, Ganga.
Proper nouns always start with a capital letter.

7. Is 'teacher' a proper noun?

Answer: No
'Teacher' is a common noun because it is a general name. 'Mrs. Sharma' would be proper.

8. What is the singular of 'children'?

Answer: child
This is an irregular plural that does not follow the normal rules.

9. Give example of naming word for animal.

Answer: Cow, Dog, Lion, Elephant, Cat
Any name of an animal is acceptable.

10. What letter does every sentence start with?

Answer: Capital letter (A, B, C...)
The first letter of the first word is always capital.

11. Is 'Ganga' a common noun?

Answer: No, it is a proper noun.
Ganga is a specific river name. 'River' is the common noun.

12. Write plural of 'box'.

Answer: boxes
Words ending in x, s, sh, ch add 'es'.

13. What is a common noun? Give example.

Answer: A general name for any person, place, or thing. Example: girl, city, school.
Many things can share this name.

14. Is 'Rahul' a common or proper noun?

Answer: Proper noun
It is a specific person's name starting with capital R.

15. Give two naming words for places in your city.

Answer: Market, School, Hospital, Park, Temple (any two)
Places are locations where we go or stay.

16. What is the gender of 'father'?

Answer: Male
Father and boy are male nouns.

17. Write the plural of 'city'.

Answer: cities
Words ending in 'y' change to 'ies' (city→cities).

18. Identify the noun: "The sun is hot."

Answer: sun
'Sun' is the naming word (thing) in this sentence.

19. Is 'sister' male or female?

Answer: Female
Sister, mother, and girl are female gender nouns.

20. What do we add to make 'cat' plural?

Answer: 's' → cats
Most nouns just add 's' to become plural.

21. Give example of naming word for thing.

Answer: Chair, Table, Pencil, Book, Ball
Things are objects that are not alive.

22. Is 'India' a proper noun?

Answer: Yes
It is the special name of our country.

23. What is the plural of 'toy'?

Answer: toys
We add 's' because 'y' has a vowel before it.

24. Identify: "My school is big."

Answer: school
'School' is the naming word for a place.

25. Boy is to Male as Girl is to?

Answer: Female
Boy is male gender, Girl is female gender.

26. Write singular and plural of 'Pen'.

Answer: Singular: pen, Plural: pens
One pen, many pens.

27. Is 'river' a common noun?

Answer: Yes
It is a general name. 'Ganga' would be proper.

28. Give example of proper noun for an animal.

Answer: Tommy, Julie, Sheru (pet names)
When we give a special name to our pet, it becomes proper.

29. What is 'run' - noun or verb?

Answer: Verb (action word)
'Run' shows action, not a name. 'Runner' would be the noun.

30. Fill in the blank: ___ apple.

Answer: An
We use 'an' before words starting with vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u).

🔄 Quick Revision

Key Points to Remember:

  • Noun = Naming Word (N for Noun, N for Name)
  • Four types: People, Places, Animals, Things
  • Proper Noun = Special name (Capital letter)
  • Common Noun = General name (small letter)
  • Singular = One (pen, boy)
  • Plural = More than one (pens, boys)
  • Add 's' for most plurals
  • Add 'es' for words ending in s, x, ch, sh
  • Change 'y' to 'ies' when 'y' follows a consonant
  • Gender: Male (he) and Female (she)
  • Subject + Verb + Object structure
Remember: Every sentence must have at least one noun!
