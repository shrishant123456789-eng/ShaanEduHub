LEVEL 1: BASIC CONCEPTS

1. What are AI Tools?

Definition: Software that uses artificial intelligence algorithms to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as generating text, creating images, or analyzing data.

[Visual: Timeline of AI - From Rule-based to Generative]
🇮🇳 Indian Context: Like using Google Translate to convert Hindi text to English instantly for official documents, but much more powerful.

2. Tool Safety & Ethics

Hallucination: When AI confidently generates false information.

Privacy: Never input PII (Personally Identifiable Information) like Aadhaar numbers into public AI models.

💡 Trick: Think "H.A.L.T." - Hallucinations, Accuracy, Leaks (Data), Trust (Verify).

3. Basic Prompting Logic

Context + Instruction + Constraint.

Wrong: "Write an email."

Right: "Act as a Project Manager (Context). Write a formal email to the client about a 2-day delay (Instruction). Keep it under 100 words and be polite (Constraint)."

LEVEL 3: SYSTEM INTEGRATION

1. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

AI that looks up your specific data before answering. It doesn't just guess; it "retrieves" facts from your uploaded PDF/Database.

💡 Trick: RAG is like an "Open Book Exam" for AI. Standard ChatGPT is a "Closed Book Exam".
