📖 Chapter Notes
1. Introduction & Revision
Everything around us is either Living (alive) or Non-Living (not alive).
🇮🇳 India Example: You, a Mango tree, and a Cow are Living. A Cricket Bat, Auto Rickshaw, and Stone are Non-Living.
[Image: A park scene showing children (living) playing on a slide (non-living) near trees (living)]
💡 Tip: If it eats, breathes, and grows, it's alive!
2. Characteristics: Growth
Living things grow in size and shape.
  • Qualitative: Changing form (Baby → Adult).
  • Quantitative: Increasing height/weight.
Example: A puppy becomes a big dog. Your school uniform becomes tight after a year!
[Image: Stages of a plant growing from seed to tree]
3. Characteristics: Movement
Animals move from place to place (Locomotion). Plants move only parts.
Example: A Tiger runs (External). A Sunflower turns its face to the sun (Internal/Partial).
💡 Tip: Cars move too, but they need petrol (non-living). Animals move by themselves!
4. Characteristics: Respiration & Nutrition
Respiration: Taking in air to get energy.
Nutrition: Taking food for growth.
🇮🇳 Example: We breathe air (Oxygen). We eat Roti/Rice (Nutrition). Plants make food using sunlight.
5. Excretion & Reproduction
Excretion: Throwing out waste.
Reproduction: Creating young ones.
Example: Sweating is excretion. A hen laying eggs is reproduction.
6. Living vs Non-Living vs Once-Living
Feature Living Non-Living Once-Living
Breathe? Yes No No (Stopped)
Example Boy, Cat Plastic Chair Wooden Table, Dried Leaf
💡 Wood was once a living tree, so it is "Once-Living"!
7. Needs & Interdependence
Living things need: Air, Water, Food, Shelter.
Interdependence: Animals depend on plants (food/oxygen). Plants depend on animals (CO2/seeds).
