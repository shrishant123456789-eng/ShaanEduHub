📘 Feelings & Values Notes

1. Understanding Feelings 😊😢😡

Feelings are what we feel inside our heart.
😊 Happy: When we smile and laugh.
😢 Sad: When we cry or feel low.
😡 Angry: When we are mad at something.
😨 Scared: When we are afraid.
It is okay to have all these feelings. We can share them with parents.

❓ Activity: Make a happy face and a sad face!

2. Being Happy 😊🌈

Happiness is a beautiful feeling.
🌟 Playing with friends makes us happy.
🌟 Sharing toys makes us happy.
🌟 Helping others makes us happy.
Always try to keep a smile on your face!

3. Handling Sadness 😢💙

Sometimes we feel sad. Maybe we lost a toy or someone scolded us.
💙 Do not cry alone.
💙 Talk to your Mom or Dad.
💙 Hug your favorite teddy bear.
Remember, sadness goes away soon, like clouds in the sky!

4. Controlling Anger 😡➡️😌

Anger is like a red hot fire. It can hurt others.
When you are angry:
1️⃣ Stop and close your eyes.
2️⃣ Take a deep breath. 🌬️
3️⃣ Count backwards: 10, 9, 8... 1.
4️⃣ Drink a glass of water. 💧
Stay calm and talk politely.

5. Saying Sorry 🙏💬

If we make a mistake, we must say Sorry.
✅ "I am sorry I broke your toy."
✅ "I am sorry I shouted."
Saying sorry makes you a brave and good child. It fixes friendships.

6. Forgiving Others 💖🤝

Forgiveness means not being angry anymore.
If a friend says sorry, smile and say "It's okay".
Let go of anger. Be friends again. 🤝
Forgiving makes your heart light and happy.

7. Confidence Building ✨🧍

Confidence means believing in yourself.
🌟 Say "I can do it!"
🌟 Speak loudly and clearly in class.
🌟 If you fail, try again. Do not give up.
You are special and smart!

8. Positive Thinking 🌞💭

Think good thoughts.
✅ Instead of "I can't", say "I will try".
✅ Instead of "It's hard", say "I will learn".
Positive thinking makes difficult things easy!

9. Cursive Writing - Emotional Values ✍️

Practice writing these beautiful sentences:

I am happy 😊
I say sorry 🙏
I forgive others 💖
I stay calm 😌

Write slowly and beautifully in your notebook.
