My Class Notes 📝
1. Name & Location

Our country is named India, also known as Bharat or Hindustan. It is located in the continent of Asia (in the South).

[Map of Asia showing India in South]
Daily Life Example: When you write your address on a letter, the last line is "India".
💡 Memory Tip: India is the 7th largest country in the world!
❓ In which continent is India located? (Ans: Asia)
2. National Symbols

Flag (Tiranga): Saffron (Courage), White (Peace), Green (Prosperity). Navy blue Ashoka Chakra in center with 24 spokes.

Emblem: Lion Capital of Ashoka (4 Lions standing back to back).

Anthem: "Jana Gana Mana" written by Rabindranath Tagore.

💡 Tip: We must always stand still when the National Anthem is played.
❓ Who wrote our National Anthem?
3. States & Union Territories

India is huge! To manage it better, it is divided into smaller parts. Currently, there are 28 States and 8 Union Territories (UTs).

[Political Map of India]
Daily Life Example: Like your school has different classes (States) and the Principal's office (Center/UTs).
💡 Memory Tip: 28 States, 8 UTs (28 + 8).
❓ How many states are there in India?
4. Capital of India

New Delhi is the capital of India. The Prime Minister and President live and work here.

Fun Fact: The Red Fort is in Delhi, where the PM hoists the flag on Independence Day.
💡 Tip: Delhi is the heart of India.
❓ Name the capital city of India.
5. Physical Features

North: The Great Himalayas (guard our borders like a wall).

South: Surrounded by Indian Ocean.

Rivers: Ganga is the holiest and longest river. Yamuna flows by the Taj Mahal.

💡 Tip: India is a Peninsula (water on 3 sides).
❓ Which ocean is named after our country?
6. Freedom Fighters

Brave people who fought for India's freedom from British rule.

  • Mahatma Gandhi: Father of the Nation. Believed in Ahimsa (Non-violence).
  • Jawaharlal Nehru: First PM. Children called him 'Chacha Nehru'.
  • Bhagat Singh: A brave young revolutionary.
[Portraits of Gandhi, Nehru, Bhagat Singh]
❓ Who is called 'Chacha Nehru'?
7. Culture & Food

India is famous for its variety! Food changes every few 100 km.

Food: Dosa (South), Makki di Roti (North), Fish Curry (East), Dhokla (West).

Dance: Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu), Kathak (North India), Bhangra (Punjab).

[Collage of Food & Dance]
💡 Tip: "Namaste" is our traditional way of greeting.
8. Festivals

We are a land of festivals!

  • National: Independence Day (15 Aug), Republic Day (26 Jan), Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct).
  • Religious: Diwali (Lights), Eid (Brotherhood), Christmas (Joy), Gurpurab.
  • Harvest: Pongal, Onam, Baisakhi.
[Festival Celebration Scene]
9. Languages

Hindi is our Official Language. English is also used for work. There are 22 languages recognized by our Constitution (like Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, etc.).

[Script examples of different languages]
💡 Tip: Most currency notes show 15+ languages on the back!
10. Unity in Diversity

This means even though we speak different languages, eat different food, and pray differently, we are all ONE (Indians).

[Children holding hands]
Example: In the Indian Cricket Team, players from all states play together for India.
💡 Core Value: We are one big family.
