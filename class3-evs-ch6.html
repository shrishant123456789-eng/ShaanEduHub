📚 Chapter Notes

1. Why We Need Food

Image: Happy Kids Eating
Food helps us to grow, gives us energy to work and play, and keeps us healthy. It protects us from falling sick.
Example: Like a car needs petrol to run, our body needs food to work!
💡 Tip: No Food = No Energy!
❓ Check: Does food help us sleep or play? (Ans: Play)

2. Types of Food

We eat different kinds of food. They are divided into three main groups based on what they do for our body.
Example: A complete meal has Rice (Energy) + Dal (Body Building) + Salad (Protective).
💡 Tip: Remember "E-B-P" (Energy, Body, Protective).

3. Energy-Giving Food

Image: Potato, Rice, Butter
These foods give us power to run, jump, and study. They contain carbohydrates and fats.
Examples: Rice, Wheat (Chapati), Sugar, Potato, Butter, Oil.
💡 Tip: Eat these before sports day!
❓ Check: Is potato an energy-giving food? (Ans: Yes)

4. Body-Building Food

Image: Eggs, Pulses, Milk
These foods help our bones and muscles grow. They repair our body when we get hurt.
Examples: Milk, Eggs, Fish, Pulses (Dal), Paneer, Nuts.
💡 Tip: Want big muscles? Eat your Dal!

5. Protective Food

Image: Fruits and Veggies
These foods fight germs and keep diseases away. They have vitamins and minerals.
Examples: Spinach, Carrot, Apple, Mango, Orange.
💡 Tip: "Protective" means Bodyguards of health!

6. Balanced Diet

A diet that has the right amount of all three types of food (Energy, Body-building, Protective) is called a Balanced Diet.
Example: Thali with Roti, Sabzi, Dal, Curd, and Salad.
💡 Tip: Don't just eat one thing. Mix it up!

7. Food from Plants

Image: Fields of crops
We get cereals, pulses, fruits, vegetables, spices, and oils from plants.
Examples: Wheat comes from plants. Apples grow on trees.

8. Food from Animals

Image: Cow, Hen
Animals give us milk, eggs, meat, and honey.
Examples: Cow gives milk. Hen gives eggs. Bee gives honey.
❓ Check: Where does honey come from? (Ans: Honeybees)

9. Cooking Methods

We cook food to make it soft, tasty, and germ-free.
  • Boiling: Rice, Eggs
  • Frying: Puri, Samosa
  • Baking: Cake, Biscuits
  • Roasting: Corn, Chicken
  • Steaming: Idli, Dhokla

10. Raw and Cooked Food

Some food can be eaten raw (without cooking), while others must be cooked.
Raw: Cucumber, Carrot, Fruits. Cooked: Rice, Meat, Potato.
💡 Tip: Salad is raw food!

11. Healthy Food Habits

Good habits keep our tummy happy.
1. Wash hands before eating.
2. Chew food well.
3. Eat at fixed times.
4. Do not waste food.
5. Rinse mouth after eating.

12. Junk Food

Image: Burger with a cross
Food that is tasty but unhealthy is junk food. It has too much fat and sugar.
Examples: Chips, Burgers, Pizza, Cold drinks.
💡 Tip: Junk food makes you lazy and fat. Avoid it!

13. Importance of Clean Water

Water helps to digest food and throw out waste from the body. We must drink 6-8 glasses of clean water daily.
Boiling or filtering water kills germs.
❓ Check: How many glasses of water daily? (Ans: 6-8)
