📚 Study Notes

1. What is a Neighbourhood?
The area around our house is called our neighbourhood.
Imagine your house. The houses next to you, the park down the street, and the shops nearby make up your neighbourhood.
[ 🏡 Image: A house with streets and trees around it ]
Example: In many Indian colonies, we have a small park or temple near our homes. That is part of our neighbourhood.
Tip: Neighbourhood = Neighbours + Hood (Area).
❓ Who lives in your neighbourhood? (Answer: Neighbours)
2. What is a Community?
A group of people living together in the same place or sharing interests is called a community.
We cannot live alone. We need people to talk to, play with, and help us. This group is our community.
[ 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Image: People celebrating a festival together ]
Example: During Ganesh Chaturthi or Eid, everyone comes together to celebrate. That is community spirit!
Tip: Community = Common Unity (People united together).
❓ Does living alone make a community? (Answer: No)
3. Community Helpers
People who do special jobs to help us in our daily lives.

🩺 Doctor: Treats us when we are sick. Gives medicines.

👩‍🏫 Teacher: Teaches us good manners and subjects in school.

👮 Police: Keeps us safe from thieves and bad people.

🌾 Farmer: Grows wheat, rice, and vegetables for us to eat.

📮 Postman: Brings letters and parcels to our doorstep.

🧹 Sweeper: Keeps our streets and roads clean.

🏪 Shopkeeper: Sells us milk, bread, pencils, and other things.

Other Helpers: Plumber (fixes pipes), Electrician (fixes lights), Cobbler (mends shoes).

[ 👩‍⚕️ Image: A doctor checking a child ]
Example: Ram Kaka brings letters (Postman), and Lakshmi Didi cleans the street (Sweeper).
Tip: They HELP the Community, so they are Community Helpers.
❓ Who grows food for us? (Answer: Farmer)
4. Why are they Important?
Without helpers, life would be very hard.
Imagine if there was no farmer? No food! No doctor? We would stay sick. No sweeper? Garbage everywhere!
Example: When the garbage truck doesn't come for 2 days, the smell is bad. We realize the sweeper is very important.
Tip: Helpers make life EASY.
❓ Can we do everything alone? (Answer: No)
5. Places in Neighbourhood

🏫 School: Place to learn and play.

🏥 Hospital: Place to go when sick or injured.

🥒 Market: Place to buy vegetables, clothes, and toys.

🌳 Park: Place to play, walk, and enjoy fresh air.

🏤 Post Office: Place to send letters, buy stamps, and save money.

[ 🏥 Image: A hospital building with an ambulance ]
Example: Buying fresh sabotage (vegetables) from the Tuesday Market (Mangal Bazar).
Tip: Think of where you go every week!
❓ Where do we go to buy stamps? (Answer: Post Office)
6. Cooperation and Sharing
Working together and sharing things is cooperation.
Neighbours help each other. If someone is sick, neighbours take them to the hospital. We share sweets during festivals.
Example: In villages, everyone helps to harvest the crop together.
Tip: Sharing is Caring!
❓ Should we fight or share? (Answer: Share)
7. Rules in Society
Rules help us live peacefully without fighting.
Rules like: Do not throw garbage on the road. Drive on the left side. Do not play loud music late at night.
[ 🚸 Image: Kids crossing road on zebra crossing ]
Example: Standing in a queue (line) at the bus stop or ration shop.
Tip: Rules = Discipline.
❓ Should we break traffic rules? (Answer: No)
8. Helping, Respect & Cleanliness

🤝 Helping Others: Help old people cross the road. Help mom in the kitchen.

🚌 Public Services: Buses, trains, street lights belong to everyone. We must not break them.

🙏 Respect for Helpers: Always say "Thank You" or "Namaste" to the sweeper, guard, and maid. Their job is hard.

🧹 Clean Community: Use dustbins. Do not spit on walls. Plant trees.

Example: Swachh Bharat Abhiyan says we must keep India clean.
Tip: Treat others how you want to be treated.
❓ Where should we throw wrappers? (Answer: Dustbin)

🔥 Important Questions

Q1. Who mends our shoes?
Ans: A Cobbler mends our shoes.
Explanation: When shoes are torn, the cobbler stitches them.
Q2. What is a dispensary?
Ans: A small place like a hospital where minor sickness is treated.
Explanation: Usually found in small neighbourhoods for quick medicines.
Q3. Why is a park important?
Ans: A park gives us fresh air and a place to play.
Explanation: Playing outside makes us strong and healthy.
Q4. Who catches thieves?
Ans: The Police.
Q5. Who brings milk?
Ans: The Milkman.
Q6. Where do we deposit money?
Ans: Bank.
Q7. What is a mobile library?
Ans: A library inside a van that moves from place to place.
Q8. Who stitches our clothes?
Ans: Tailor.
Q9. Who fits pipes and taps?
Ans: Plumber.
Q10. Who fits wires and lights?
Ans: Electrician.
Q11. What is an ambulance?
Ans: A special van to take sick people to the hospital fast.
Q12. What sound does a fire engine make?
Ans: A loud siren/bell to clear traffic.
Q13. Who cuts hair?
Ans: Barber.
Q14. Who brings newspapers?
Ans: Newspaper boy/man.
Q15. Who builds walls of a house?
Ans: Mason.
Q16. Who paints the walls?
Ans: Painter.
Q17. Who makes wooden furniture?
Ans: Carpenter.
Q18. Where do we buy envelopes?
Ans: Post Office.
Q19. What is a PIN code?
Ans: Postal Index Number. It helps letters reach the right city.
Q20. Who manages traffic on roads?
Ans: Traffic Police.
Q21. What should we do if we see fire?
Ans: Call the Fire Station (101).
Q22. What number do we dial for Police?
Ans: 100.
Q23. What number do we dial for Ambulance?
Ans: 102 or 108.
Q24. Who treats sick animals?
Ans: Veterinary Doctor (Vet).
Q25. What is a Chemist?
Ans: A shop that sells medicines.
Q26. Who drives the school bus?
Ans: Driver.
Q27. Who guards the bank?
Ans: Security Guard.
Q28. Should we make noise in a hospital?
Ans: No, patients need rest.
Q29. Should we pluck flowers in the park?
Ans: No, flowers make the park beautiful.
Q30. What is a Grocery store?
Ans: A shop selling food and household items.
