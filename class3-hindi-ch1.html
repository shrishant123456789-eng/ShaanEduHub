Class 3 – Hindi

📘 Chapter Notes

Welcome dear students! Today we will learn about the Hindi alphabet and how to make words. Hindi is our national language.

1. Introduction to Hindi Language

Hindi is written in the Devanagari script. Just like English has ABC, Hindi has Varnmala (Alphabet).

  • The smallest unit of language is called Varn (Letter).
  • Letters join together to make words.
💡 Tip: We speak what we write in Hindi. It is a phonetic language.
❓ Quick Check: What is the script of Hindi? (Answer: Devanagari)

2. Swar (Vowels)

Letters that can be spoken freely without help from other letters are called Swar.

  • There are 11 Swar in Hindi: अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ऋ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ.
  • अं (Anuswar) and अः (Visarga) are called Ayogvah.
💡 Tip: 'अ' has no matra (symbol). All other vowels have matras.
❓ Quick Check: How many Swar are there? (Answer: 11)

3. Vyanjan (Consonants)

Letters that need the help of vowels to be spoken are called Vyanjan.

  • They range from क (Ka) to ह (Ha).
  • Total consonants are 33.
  • Example: क + अ = क (Ka).
💡 Tip: Every consonant has the sound of 'a' hidden inside it.
❓ Quick Check: Which is the first consonant? (Answer: क)

4. Sanyukt Akshar (Conjunct Letters)

When two consonants join together, they form a Sanyukt Akshar.

  • क्ष (Ksha) = क् + ष
  • त्र (Tra) = त् + र
  • ज्ञ (Gya) = ज् + ञ
  • श्र (Shra) = श् + र
[Image showing breakdown of क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ]
💡 Tip: These are special letters found at the end of the alphabet list.
❓ Quick Check: 'Tra' is made of? (Answer: T + R)

5. Word Formation (Shabd Nirman)

We join letters to make words. The meaning must be clear.

  • Two letter word: घ + र = घर (House)
  • Three letter word: क + म + ल = कमल (Lotus)
  • Four letter word: अ + द + र + क = अदरक (Ginger)
💡 Tip: Read slowly, letter by letter, then join them.
❓ Quick Check: Join 'न' + 'ल'. (Answer: नल)

📝 Important Questions

Q1. What is the smallest unit of language?

Answer: Varn (Letter)
Explanation: Language starts with letters. We cannot break letters further.

Q2. How many Vowels (Swar) are there in Hindi?

Answer: 11
Explanation: From 'A' (अ) to 'Au' (औ).

Q3. Which letter comes after 'Ka' (क)?

Answer: Kha (ख)
Explanation: The order is Ka, Kha, Ga, Gha, Nga.

Q4. What is 'Gya' (ज्ञ) called?

Answer: Sanyukt Akshar (Conjunct Consonant)
Explanation: It is made of two consonants (J + Nya).

Q5. Write a word starting with 'Aa' (आ).

Answer: Aam (Mango)
Explanation: 'Aam' uses the long 'Aa' sound.

Q6. Is 'Ang' (अं) a vowel?
Ans: No, it is Ayogvah.

Q7. Complete: Ta, Tha, __, Dha.
Ans: Da (द).

Q8. Which animal starts with 'Kha'?
Ans: Khargosh (Rabbit).

Q9. Join: B + S (ब + स).
Ans: Bas (Bus).

Q10. How many consonants are there?
Ans: 33.

Q11. What comes before 'Ha' (ह)?
Ans: Sa (स).

Q12. Identify the vowel: K, P, U, T.
Ans: U (उ).

Q13. Is Hindi written left to right?
Ans: Yes.

Q14. The last vowel is?
Ans: Au (औ).

Q15. Make a word: K+L+M.
Ans: Kalam (Pen).

Q16. What does 'Chh' (छ) stand for?
Ans: Chhatri (Umbrella).

Q17. Which letter looks like a trident?
Ans: Tra (त्र).

Q18. Sound of 'Ri' (ऋ) is like?
Ans: Ri in 'Ring'.

Q19. First letter of 'Ghar' (House)?
Ans: Gh (घ).

Q20. 'Pa' comes in which line?
Ans: Pa-varg (Pa, Pha, Ba, Bha, Ma).

Q21. Is 'Sha' (श) a vowel?
Ans: No, it is a consonant.

Q22. Meaning of 'Jal'?
Ans: Water.

Q23. Which letter is silent in 'Namah'?
Ans: The Visarga (:) produces 'ha' sound.

Q24. Total letters in 'Kamal'?
Ans: 3 (Ka-Ma-La).

Q25. 'Th' as in 'Thermos' is which letter?
Ans: Th (थ).

Q26. Does 'A' (अ) have a matra?
Ans: No.

Q27. 'D' as in Dog is?
Ans: Da (ड).

Q28. 'N' as in Nose is?
Ans: Na (न).

Q29. Join: Ph + L.
Ans: Phal (Fruit).

Q30. Is 'Ksha' a basic consonant?
Ans: No, it is a joint consonant.

Quick Recall

  • Language: Hindi (Devanagari Script).
  • Swar (11): अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ऊ, ऋ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ.
  • Ayogvah: अं, अः.
  • Vyanjan (33): From क to ह.
  • Sanyukt Akshar: क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ, श्र.
  • Words: Formed by joining letters (e.g., क + ल = कल).
