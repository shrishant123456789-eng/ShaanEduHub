🔁 Quick Revision

  • Earth has 3 layers: Crust (Outer), Mantle (Middle), Core (Inner).
  • Soil is formed by weathering of rocks over thousands of years.
  • Loam soil is best for plants; Clay holds water; Sand drains water.
  • Renewable: Sun, Wind, Water. Non-Renewable: Coal, Oil.
  • Solar System: Sun + 8 Planets. Mercury (closest) -> Neptune (farthest).
  • Rotation causes Day/Night. Revolution causes Seasons.
  • Moon has no light of its own; reflects Sun's light.
  • Conservation: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle to save Earth.
