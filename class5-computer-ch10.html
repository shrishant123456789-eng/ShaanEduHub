📘 Study Notes

1. Cloud Computing Concept

[Image: A cloud connecting phone, laptop, and tablet]

Definition: Cloud computing means storing and accessing data (files, photos, games) over the internet instead of your computer's hard drive.

Explanation: Imagine a "digital bag" that follows you everywhere. You don't carry the bag; it floats in the internet "cloud".

🇮🇳 Example: Watching cartoons on YouTube. The video is not on your phone; it is streamed from the YouTube cloud.
☁️ Cloud = Internet Storage (Not the sky!)

Quick Check: Is the cloud inside your CPU? (No)

2. Online Storage

[Image: Icons of Google Drive and OneDrive]

Definition: Services that let you save files on the internet.

Common Examples:
- Google Drive: Gives 15GB free space. Linked to Gmail.
- Microsoft OneDrive: Linked to Windows and Outlook.

🇮🇳 Example: Saving your holiday photos on Google Photos so they don't get lost if you lose your phone.
📦 Think of it as a "Digital Locker".

3. Advantages of Cloud

[Image: Accessibility icon, Cost saver icon]

1. Cost Saving: No need to buy expensive hard disks.

2. Accessibility: Open files from any computer or mobile.

3. Backup: Data is safe even if your device breaks.

🇮🇳 Example: A student doing homework on a school computer and finishing it at home without a pen drive.
💪 Safe, Cheap, Everywhere.

4. Online Collaboration

[Image: Multiple cursors on one document]

Definition: Many people working on the same file at the same time from different places.

Explanation: Using tools like Google Docs, you and your friend can type in the same story together.

🇮🇳 Example: Students making a group project presentation while sitting in their own homes.
🤝 Teamwork over Internet.

5. Digital Payments (UPI)

[Image: QR Code and UPI Logo]

Definition: Paying money directly from a bank account using a mobile app, without cash.

Key Terms: UPI (Unified Payments Interface), QR Code (Quick Response), PIN.

🇮🇳 Example: Buying vegetables and paying by scanning a Paytm or PhonePe QR code.
💳 Cashless India. Never share your PIN! 🔒

6. Digital India Initiative

[Image: Digital India Logo]

Definition: A program by the Government of India to make government services available electronically.

Goal: To empower citizens with technology.

🇮🇳 Example: Using DigiLocker to show your driving license on your phone instead of carrying the card.
🇮🇳 Power to the People via Tech.
