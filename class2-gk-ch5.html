🍃 Garden Walk Notes

1. Meet the Plant Family! 🌱

Plants are living things like us! They need food and water to grow.
There are different types:

  • Trees: Big, tall, and strong plants. They have a hard wood stem called a Trunk. (Example: Mango Tree 🥭, Banyan Tree 🌳)
  • Shrubs: Small and bushy plants. (Example: Rose plant 🌹, Tulsi)
  • Herbs: Very small plants with soft green stems. (Example: Mint, Coriander 🌿)
  • Climbers: They need support to stand up. (Example: Money Plant)

2. Body Parts of a Plant 🌿

Just like we have hands and legs, plants have parts too!

  • Roots: They are underground. They hold the plant tight and drink water. 💧
  • Stem: It stands tall and carries water to leaves. 🎋
  • Leaves: They are green. They make food for the plant using sunlight. ☀️
  • Flower: The most beautiful part. It becomes a fruit later! 🌸
  • Fruit: The yummy part we eat. It has seeds inside. 🍎

3. What do Plants give us? 🎁

Plants are our best friends because they give us so many things:

  • Food: Fruits, Vegetables, Grains (Rice, Wheat).
  • Clean Air: They take in bad air and give us fresh Oxygen to breathe. 🌬️
  • Wood: To make chairs, tables, and pencils. ✏️
  • Medicine: Plants like Tulsi, Neem, and Mint help us when we are sick. 💊
  • Shade: Trees give cool shade on a hot sunny day. 😎

4. Fruits, Flowers & Seeds 🌻

Fruits & Vegetables:
We eat roots (Carrot 🥕), stems (Sugarcane), and leaves (Spinach 🥬).

Flowers:
They have sweet smells and bright colors to attract butterflies 🦋. (Rose, Jasmine, Sunflower).

Seeds & Germination:
A baby plant sleeps inside a seed. When we put a seed in soil and give it water and sunlight, it wakes up and grows. This is called Germination. 🌱

5. Plants Need Care ❤️

To stay alive, plants need 4 friends:
☀️ Sunlight
💧 Water
💨 Air
🟤 Soil

Save Trees: Do not cut trees. Plant more trees. Trees make our earth happy! 🌍
