Chapter Notes

1. What is a Pronoun?

A pronoun is a word used in place of a Noun (Name). We use pronouns so we don't repeat names again and again.

Without Pronoun: Ram is a boy. Ram likes cricket.
With Pronoun: Ram is a boy. He likes cricket.
💡 Tip: Pronouns help us speak faster and smoother!
[Image: A boy with label 'He', Girl with label 'She']
2. Common Pronouns
  • He: Used for boys/men (He is my father).
  • She: Used for girls/women (She is my sister).
  • It: Used for animals or things (It is a cat).
  • I: Used for yourself.
  • We: Used when you are with others.
  • They: Used for many people or things.
[Image: Group of kids labeled 'We' and 'They']
3. Articles: A, An, The

Articles are little words that come before nouns. There are three articles: a, an, the.


When to use 'A': Before words starting with a Consonant sound (b, c, d, f...).
a boy, a car, a mango.
When to use 'An': Before words starting with a Vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u).
an apple, an egg, an ice-cream.
4. The Special Article: 'The'

We use The when talking about specific things or unique things in the world.

The Sun (Only one sun)
The Taj Mahal (Specific building)
The book on the table (Specific book)
💡 Tip: Never say 'A Sun' or 'A Moon'. Always 'The Sun'.
