📘 Study Notes

1. Speaking Truth 🗣️

Truth means saying exactly what happened.
🌟 When we speak the truth, everyone trusts us.
🌟 Story: Rohan broke a vase. He was scared but told his mom, "Sorry Mom, I broke it." His mom hugged him for being brave and truthful.

❓ Activity: If you forget homework, will you tell the truth to your teacher?

2. Being Honest ⭐

Honesty means being good and truthful in your actions.
✅ An honest child does not steal.
✅ An honest child plays games fairly.
Honesty makes you a Super Star! ⭐

3. Not Lying ❌🤥

A Lie is saying something that is not true.
🚫 Lying is bad because one lie leads to more lies.
🚫 If you lie, people will stop believing you.
Always be brave and tell the truth!

4. Not Cheating 🚫✏️

Cheating means copying from others or breaking rules to win.
📝 In exams, write your own answers.
🏃‍♂️ In games, follow the rules.
Winning by cheating is not a real win. Real winners play fair!

5. Returning Lost Things 🎒➡️

If you find something that is not yours (like a pencil, money, or toy), do not keep it.
✅ Give it to the teacher or parents.
✅ This is what honest children do.
Imagine if you lost your favorite toy; you would want someone to return it, right?

6. Admitting Mistakes 🙋‍♂️

Everyone makes mistakes. It is okay!
But hiding a mistake is wrong.
🌟 Say: "I am sorry, it was my mistake."
When you admit your mistake, you learn and become better.

7. Right vs Wrong ✔️❌

We must know the difference:
✔️ Right: Sharing, Helping, Speaking Truth.
❌ Wrong: Stealing, Lying, Fighting.
Always choose the Right path!

8. Honesty in Daily Life 🌈

Be honest everywhere:
🏠 At Home: Tell parents if you break something.
🏫 At School: Do your own homework.
👫 With Friends: Do not cheat in games.
Honesty brings happiness and peace. 😊
