📖 Chapter 4 Notes
1. Different Types of Birds
Birds are animals with feathers, wings, and two legs. There are many kinds of birds around us.
Examples: Crow (black), Parrot (green), Peacock (beautiful feathers), Duck (swims).
🖼️ Image: A collage of Parrot, Crow, and Peacock
💡 Tip: Most birds can fly, but some like Ostrich cannot fly!
❓ Check: Which is our national bird? (Ans: Peacock)
2. Parts of a Bird
A bird's body helps it to fly and eat.
  • Beak: To eat food (no teeth!).
  • Wings: To fly high.
  • Feathers: Keep the bird warm.
  • Claws: Feet to hold branches.
  • Tail: Helps to change direction while flying.
🖼️ Image: Diagram of bird pointing to Beak, Wings, Tail
💡 Tip: Beak = Mouth of a bird.
❓ Check: What helps a bird change direction? (Ans: Tail)
3. Food Habits of Birds
Different birds eat different food using their beaks.
  • Grain eaters: Sparrow, Pigeon, Hen.
  • Flesh eaters: Eagle, Vulture (sharp hooked beak).
  • Insect eaters: Woodpecker.
  • Water food: Duck, Crane (fish).
🖼️ Image: Sparrow eating grains, Eagle eating meat
💡 Tip: Shape of the beak tells us what the bird eats!
❓ Check: What does an Eagle eat? (Ans: Flesh/Meat)
4. Nests of Birds
A nest is a bird's home. They lay eggs there.
  • Sparrow: Makes nest in houses or trees.
  • Tailor Bird: Stitches leaves together.
  • Weaver Bird: Weaves grass into hanging nests.
  • Woodpecker: Makes a hole in tree trunk.
🖼️ Image: Weaver bird nest hanging from a tree
💡 Tip: Birds leave the nest after babies grow up.
❓ Check: Which bird stitches leaves? (Ans: Tailor bird)
5. Common Insects Around Us
Insects are tiny animals. They have 6 legs. Most have wings.
Examples: Housefly, Mosquito, Ant, Cockroach, Butterfly.
🖼️ Image: Drawing of an Ant and a Butterfly
💡 Tip: Count the legs! If 6, it is an insect.
❓ Check: How many legs does an insect have? (Ans: Six)
6. Useful Insects
Some insects help us or give us things.
  • Honeybee: Gives us sweet honey.
  • Silkworm: Gives us silk for clothes.
  • Butterfly: Helps flowers to grow fruits (pollination).
🖼️ Image: Honeybee on a flower
💡 Tip: Honey and Silk come from insects!
❓ Check: Who gives us silk? (Ans: Silkworm)
7. Harmful Insects
Some insects spread diseases or spoil things.
  • Mosquito: Bites and spreads Malaria and Dengue.
  • Housefly: Sits on dirt and food, spreads cholera.
  • Termite: Eats wood and furniture.
  • Lice: Live in hair and drink blood.
🖼️ Image: Mosquito biting a hand
💡 Tip: Keep food covered to stop houseflies.
❓ Check: Which insect eats wood? (Ans: Termite)
8. Life Cycle of Insects
Insects change shape as they grow.
Butterfly Life Cycle:
Egg ➡ Larva (Caterpillar) ➡ Pupa ➡ Adult Butterfly.
🖼️ Image: Cycle circle: Egg -> Caterpillar -> Pupa -> Butterfly
💡 Tip: The caterpillar looks nothing like a butterfly!
❓ Check: What comes out of a butterfly egg? (Ans: Caterpillar)
9. Small Creatures
Some small animals are not insects but live near us.
  • Spider: Has 8 legs (not an insect!). Makes a web.
  • Earthworm: Lives in soil. Farmers' friend. No legs.
  • Snail: Carries a hard shell on its back.
🖼️ Image: Spider on a web
💡 Tip: Spiders spin webs to catch insects.
❓ Check: How many legs does a spider have? (Ans: Eight)
10. Importance of Insects and Birds
Nature needs everyone.
  • Birds eat insects so there aren't too many insects.
  • Some birds clean the earth by eating dead animals (Vulture).
  • Insects help flowers grow.
🖼️ Image: Bird feeding a baby bird
💡 Tip: Do not hurt them. They are nature's helpers.
❓ Check: Who eats dead animals to clean up? (Ans: Vulture)
