📘 Study Notes

1. Being a Good Friend 💖

A Good Friend is like a treasure.
🌟 Listen when your friend talks.
🌟 Help them when they are sad or need something.
Story: Sonu and Monu were best friends. When Monu fell down, Sonu helped him get up instead of laughing. That is true friendship!

❓ Question: How can you be a good friend today?

2. Sharing & Caring 🥰

Sharing means giving a part of what you have to others.
✅ Share your snacks during lunch break.
✅ Share your crayons in art class.
Caring means showing love. Ask your friend "Are you okay?" if they look sad.

3. Playing Together ⚽🎨

Playing alone is boring. Playing Together is fun!
✅ Play team games like football or cricket.
✅ Play board games like Ludo peacefully.
Playing teaches us to work as a team and enjoy together.

❓ Activity: Plan a fun game with friends.

4. Helping Classmates 🙋‍♂️

Your classmates are your friends too.
✅ If someone doesn't understand homework, explain it to them.
✅ If someone forgets a pencil, lend them yours.
Helping makes the classroom a happy place!

5. Not Fighting ❌🥊

Fighting hurts everyone. It is bad behavior.
🚫 Don't push or hit anyone.
🚫 Don't use bad words.
If you are angry, talk politely or walk away. Fighting solves nothing.

6. Solving Problems Peacefully ☮️

Sometimes friends disagree. That's okay!
✅ Talk about the problem calmly.
✅ Say "Sorry" if you made a mistake.
✅ Take turns speaking.
Peace is better than fighting.

7. Teamwork 🤝

Teamwork means working together to finish a task.
Example: Cleaning the classroom together makes it clean faster!
Together Everyone Achieves More (TEAM).

8. Respecting Differences 🌈

Everyone is different. Some are tall, some short. Some like blue, some like red.
✅ Respect everyone's choices.
✅ Don't make fun of anyone.
Differences make the world colorful like a rainbow!
