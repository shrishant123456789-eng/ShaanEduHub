🌐 What is Internet?

Imagine a huge spider web that connects the whole world!
The Internet is a big network that connects all computers together.
Because of it, you can talk to friends, watch cartoons, and learn new things from home 🏠.

✨ Magic of Internet

What can we do online?
• Learn: Read books and solve sums 📚.
• Watch: See funny videos and cartoons 🎥.
• Play: Enjoy fun games 🎮.
• Listen: Hear your favorite songs 🎵.

🌍 Websites

A website is like a shop or a house on the internet.
• Google helps us find answers 🔍.
• YouTube shows us videos 🎬.
• Wikipedia gives us information 📖.
"Just type the name, and you are there!"

🔒 Be Safe Online

The internet is big, so we must be careful.
• Always Ask Parents before opening a website 👨‍👩‍👧.
• Do not talk to Strangers ❌.
• Keep your name and address Secret 🤐.
"Safe internet is happy internet!" 😃
