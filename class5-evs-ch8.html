🔁 Quick Revision

  • Air is a mixture of gases (78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen).
  • Air occupies space, has weight, and exerts pressure.
  • Water exists in solid, liquid, and gas forms.
  • Evaporation: Water to Vapour. Condensation: Vapour to Water.
  • Water Cycle: Evaporation -> Condensation -> Precipitation.
  • Weather is daily condition; Climate is long-term pattern.
  • Pollution makes air and water dirty and harmful.
  • Conservation: Rainwater harvesting and closing taps helps save water.
