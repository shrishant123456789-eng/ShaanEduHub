📖 Chapter Notes

💬 Speaking Simple Sentences

Speaking simple sentences means putting words together in the correct order (Subject + Verb + Object) so others can understand you clearly.

Example:
Subject: I
Verb: eat
Object: an apple
"I eat an apple." 🍎
Always start with small words and speak slowly.
Quick Check: Make a sentence using "Cat", "drinks", "milk".
[Image: Child speaking clearly]

🌅 Daily Conversation

Daily conversation includes greetings and talking about everyday activities with family, friends, and teachers.

Common phrases:
"Good morning!" ☀️
"How are you?" 🤗
"What did you eat?" 🍽️
Practice daily conversations with your family at home.
Quick Check: What do you say when you meet your teacher in the morning?
[Image: Children greeting each other]

👋 Introducing Oneself

Telling others about yourself including your name, class, age, and hobbies makes people know you better.

Example:
"Hello! My name is Ravi. I am 8 years old. I study in Class 3. I like to play cricket. 🏏"
Always smile and make eye contact when introducing yourself.
Quick Check: How will you introduce yourself to a new friend?
[Image: Child introducing themselves]

❓ Asking Questions

Questions help us learn new things. Use WH words: What, Where, When, Who, Why, How.

Examples:
• What is your name?
• Where is my bag?
• How are you feeling?
Questions always end with a question mark (?).
Quick Check: Ask a question about the time.
[Image: Question marks floating]

✅ Answering Questions

Give clear and polite answers. Always answer in complete sentences, not just one word.

Q: How are you?
Bad Answer: Fine.
Good Answer: I am fine, thank you. How are you? 😊
Look at the person who is asking and speak clearly.
Quick Check: What is the full answer to "What is your name?"
[Image: Teacher asking student]

👂 Listening Carefully

Listening is not just hearing. It means understanding what the speaker is saying with full attention.

When your teacher says:
"Open your books to page 10."
You should: Open book to exactly page 10, not page 15.
Ears open, mouth closed, eyes on the speaker.
Quick Check: Should you talk while someone is explaining?
[Image: Child listening attentively]

📖 Listening to Short Stories

Stories teach us lessons. Listen to the beginning, middle, and end to understand the full story.

The Thirsty Crow Story:
Beginning: Crow was thirsty
Middle: Found water in a pot but level was low
End: Put stones to raise water level and drank
Imagine the story like a movie in your mind.
Quick Check: Name one character from a story you heard.
[Image: Storybook illustration]

🗨️ Listening and Responding

After listening, respond appropriately. It shows you understood and care about what was said.

If someone says "Good morning":
Response: "Good morning!" with a smile 😊

If someone asks "Are you ready?":
Response: "Yes, I am ready" or "No, please wait a moment."
Think before you speak, but don't take too long!
Quick Check: What do you say when your name is called in class?
[Image: Two children talking]

🎩 Polite Words

Magic words that make people happy: Please, Sorry, Thank you, Excuse me, Welcome.

Usage:
Please: When asking for something
Sorry: When you make a mistake
Thank you: When someone helps you
Excuse me: When you want to pass by
Polite children are everyone's favorite!
Quick Check: What do you say when you step on someone's foot by mistake?
[Image: Magic words illustration]

📝 Oral Grammar Practice

Speaking correct English without writing. Focus on using 'is', 'are', 'am' correctly and proper tense.

Correct:
I am going to school.
He is playing.
They are sleeping.

Incorrect: He playing. ❌
Read aloud every day for 10 minutes.
Quick Check: Fill: She ___ (is/are) a good girl.
[Image: Child speaking correctly]

🎭 Role Play

Acting as different characters like doctor, teacher, shopkeeper to practice real-life conversations.

Doctor Role:
"What is your problem?"
"Open your mouth and say Aaaah..." 🩺

Shopkeeper Role:
"What would you like to buy?"
"That will be 50 rupees please."
Feel like the character you are playing. Use their tone!
Quick Check: What does a teacher say when taking attendance?
[Image: Children in different costumes]

💪 Confidence While Speaking

Speak without fear. Making mistakes is okay - it helps you learn better!

Confidence tips:
1. Stand straight 🦸
2. Speak loudly enough to be heard
3. Don't look down at the floor
4. Breathe deeply before speaking
5. It's okay to say "I don't know"
Practice makes perfect! Every expert was once a beginner.
Quick Check: Should you speak softly or loudly in class?

🔄 Quick Revision

🎯 Key Speaking & Listening Tips:

Always face the person you are speaking to
Use polite words like Please, Thank you, and Sorry
Listen completely before answering
Speak clearly and at medium speed (not too fast, not too slow)
Ask questions if you don't understand something
