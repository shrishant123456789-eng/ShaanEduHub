📘 Let's Explore

1. Places Around Us 🏡🏫

The area around our house is called our Surroundings. We see many important places:

  • Home: The place where we live with our loving family.
  • School: The place where we go to learn, read, and write.
  • Classroom: A special room in school with a blackboard, tables, and chairs.
  • Park: A beautiful place with swings and trees where we play! 🛝
  • Market: A place with many shops to buy vegetables, milk, and toys. 🛒

2. Village & City 🏙️🚜

People live in different types of places:

Village (Gaon):
It is quiet and peaceful. We see farms, cows, and tractors. Houses are often small. 🌾

City (Shahar):
It is busy and noisy. We see tall buildings, many cars, and big markets. 🚗

3. Clean vs. Dirty Surroundings 🧹

Clean Surroundings: No garbage on the road. Fresh air. Green trees. This makes us healthy and happy! 😊

Dirty Surroundings: Garbage everywhere. Bad smell. Flies and mosquitoes. This makes us sick. 🤢

Remember: Always throw waste in the Dustbin! 🗑️

4. The 3 Magic Words (3Rs) ♻️

To keep Earth happy, we must follow the 3Rs:

  • Reduce: Use less. Do not waste water or paper.
  • Reuse: Use things again. Example: Use an old jam jar to keep pencils.
  • Recycle: Make new things from old things. Old newspapers can be made into new paper!
