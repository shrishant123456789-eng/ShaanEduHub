📘 Study Notes

1. Following Rules 📜

Rules are like guide maps. They help us stay safe and happy.
🌟 School Rules: Listen to the teacher, keep the class clean.
🌟 Home Rules: Put toys away, sleep on time.
Story: Ravi broke a traffic rule and fell from his cycle. He learned that rules are for safety!

❓ Question: Do you follow rules at home?

2. Coming on Time (Punctuality) ⏰

Being Punctual means doing things at the right time.
✅ Reach school before the bell rings.
✅ Wake up early in the morning.
Time waits for no one. If you are late, you miss fun things!

3. Doing Homework Regularly 📚

Homework helps us remember what we learned.
✅ Finish homework before playing.
✅ Pack your bag for the next day.
A disciplined child never forgets their books!

4. Sitting Properly 🪑

Good posture keeps our body strong.
🧘‍♂️ Sit straight with your back against the chair.
🚫 Do not slouch or sleep on the desk.
When you sit properly, you can write better and listen better!

5. Standing in Line (Queue) 🏃‍♂️➡️

Always stand in a Queue.
✅ Wait for your turn at the water tap.
✅ Line up to board the bus.
🚫 Pushing others is bad manners.
Patience (waiting calmly) is a superpower!

6. Controlling Anger 😡➡️😌

Anger is like a hot fire. It hurts others and us.
If you feel angry:
1️⃣ Stop and take a deep breath. 🌬️
2️⃣ Count to 10 slowly. 🔢
3️⃣ Drink some water. 💧
Don't hit or scream. Stay cool like a cucumber! 🥒

7. Not Shouting 📢❌

Shouting is rude and hurts ears.
✅ Speak softly and politely.
✅ Use words like "Please" and "Excuse me".
A soft voice is loved by everyone.

8. Being Calm & Patient 🕊️

Patience means waiting without getting angry.
🌟 If a puzzle is hard, try again slowly.
🌟 If mom is busy, wait quietly.
Calm children solve problems faster than angry children.
