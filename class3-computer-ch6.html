🖥️ What is a Window?

Think of a window as a rectangular box on your computer screen where you can see a program. Just like you open a drawer to see your toys, you open a window to see your work!

🖱️ Using your Mouse

  • Single Click: Used to select an item.
  • Double Click: Used to open a program or window.
  • Right Click: Shows a list of commands (like a secret menu!).
  • Drag and Drop: Used to move icons around the desktop.

🔘 The Three Magic Buttons

At the top right corner of every window, you see three buttons:

  • Minimize (-): Hides the window in the Taskbar. The window is still open but sleeping!
  • Maximize/Restore: Makes the window cover the whole screen, or brings it back to its original size.
  • Close (X): This red button shuts the window completely when you are finished.

🔄 Switching Between Windows

When you have many windows open, you can click on their names in the Taskbar at the bottom of the screen to switch between them. It's like flipping through different books on your desk!
