📘 What are Moral Values?

Moral values are the good habits and principles that guide our behavior. They help us decide what is right and what is wrong.

Moral values ka matlab hai wo achi aadatein jo humein ek acha insaan banati hain. Ye humein sahi aur galat ka fark samjhati hain.

❤️ Core Moral Values

Let's learn the pillars of a good character.

1. Honesty (Sachaai)

Telling the truth, even when it is scary. Not cheating or stealing.

Sach bolna, chahe darr lage. Jhooth bolkar bachne se behtar hai sach bolkar daant kha lena.

2. Respect (Izzat)

Treating others the way you want to be treated. Respect elders, nature, and friends.

3. Courage (Himmat)

Standing up for what is right, even if you are standing alone.
