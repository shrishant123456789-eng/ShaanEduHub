🖥️ Parts of a Computer

Class 2 • Chapter 2 • Let's Learn & Play!
🖥️ The Monitor

It looks like a TV screen.
It shows us whatever we do on the computer.
We can see cartoons, movies, photos, and words on it.

🧠 The CPU

CPU stands for Central Processing Unit.
It is called the BRAIN of the computer.
It does all the calculations and thinking. It is inside a box called the CPU Cabinet.

⌨️ The Keyboard

It has many small buttons called Keys.
We use it to TYPE letters, numbers, and words.
Just like we use a pencil to write on paper, we use a keyboard to write on a computer.

🖱️ The Mouse

It is a pointing device. It has 2 or 3 buttons and a scroll wheel.
It helps us to draw pictures and play games.
When we move the mouse, an arrow (pointer) moves on the monitor.

🔌 Additional Parts

🖨️ Printer: It prints your work on paper (Hard Copy).

🔊 Speakers: They help us to listen to music and sounds.

🔋 UPS: It keeps the computer ON for some time when electricity goes off.

🎤 Microphone: We use it to record our voice or talk to others.

📠 Scanner: It copies photos from paper into the computer.

📷 Webcam: It is a camera used for video calls.

🔄 Input & Output

📥 Input Devices: Parts that tell the computer what to do.
Examples: Keyboard (Typing), Mouse (Clicking), Microphone (Talking).

📤 Output Devices: Parts that show us the result.
Examples: Monitor (Shows pictures), Printer (Gives paper), Speakers (Give sound).

💾 Storage Devices: Places where computer saves work.
Examples: Hard Disk, Pen Drive, CD/DVD.
