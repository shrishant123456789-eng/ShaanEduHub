⚡ Rapid Fire Rules

  • Synonyms: Same meaning (Happy = Glad).
  • Antonyms: Opposite meaning (Hot x Cold).
  • Homonyms: Same sound, different meaning (Bat/Bat).
  • Prefix: Added at START (Un-, Dis-, Re-).
  • Suffix: Added at END (-ful, -less, -ly).

📚 Study Notes

1. Synonyms & Antonyms

Definition: Synonyms are words with the SAME meaning. Antonyms are OPPOSITES.

Explanation: Using different words makes your English sound better instead of saying "good" all the time.

[Visual: Happy face vs Sad face]

Example:
Synonym: Big = Huge
Antonym: Day x Night

Memory Tip: 'S' for Synonym = Same. 'A' for Antonym = Against.

❓ Check: Antonym of 'Rich'?
✅ Poor

2. Homonyms

Definition: Words that sound the same but mean different things.

Explanation: Spelling might be same or different, but sound is identical.

[Visual: Cricket Bat vs Animal Bat]

Example:
1. Bark (Dog sound)
2. Bark (Tree skin)

Memory Tip: Homo = Same, Nym = Name/Sound.

❓ Check: 'Right' vs 'Write'. Are they homonyms?
✅ Yes

3. Idioms & Phrases

Definition: A group of words with a special hidden meaning.

Explanation: You cannot understand them by reading word-by-word.

[Visual: Cats and dogs falling from cloud]

Example: "Piece of cake" = Very easy task.

Memory Tip: Visualize the funny picture it creates!

❓ Check: Meaning of 'Cry over spilled milk'?
✅ Worrying about past mistakes

4. One-Word Substitutes

Definition: Using one single word to replace a long sentence.

Explanation: It makes writing short and smart.

[Visual: A doctor examining teeth]

Example: One who treats teeth = Dentist.

Memory Tip: Think of it as a job title.

❓ Check: A place where books are kept?
✅ Library

5. Collocations

Definition: Words that act like best friends and always sit together.

Explanation: We say "Fast food", not "Quick food".

[Visual: Burger and Fries holding hands]

Example: "Make a bed" (Correct) vs "Do a bed" (Wrong).

Memory Tip: Listen to how teachers speak.

❓ Check: Do we 'tell' a lie or 'say' a lie?
✅ Tell a lie

6. Contextual Meanings

Definition: Guessing meaning by reading the whole sentence.

Explanation: If you don't know a word, look at its neighbors!

[Visual: Detective looking at clues]

Example: "The arid land had no water." (Arid must mean dry).

Memory Tip: Be a word detective.

❓ Check: "He was furious when his toy broke." Meaning?
✅ Very Angry

7. Prefixes & Suffixes

Definition: Little word parts added to start (Prefix) or end (Suffix).

Explanation: They change the meaning completely.

[Visual: Lego blocks attaching to a word]

Example:
Prefix: Unhappy
Suffix: Careless

Memory Tip: PREfix comes PRE (before).

❓ Check: Suffix for 'Kind' to show state?
✅ Kindness

8. Dictionary Skills

Definition: Using a book to find meanings and spellings.

Explanation: Words are arranged alphabetically (A to Z).

[Visual: Open Dictionary book]

Example: 'Apple' comes before 'Banana'.

Memory Tip: Look at the 2nd letter if the 1st is same.

❓ Check: Which comes first: Cat or Car?
✅ Car

9. Spelling Enhancement

Definition: Writing words with correct letters.

Explanation: Wrong spelling changes meaning or makes it hard to read.

[Visual: A+ written on paper]

Example: 'Receive' (not Recieve). Remember: I before E except after C.

Memory Tip: Break big words: Wed-nes-day.

❓ Check: Correct spelling: Freind or Friend?
✅ Friend

10. Vocabulary Games

Definition: Fun ways to learn words like Crosswords or Scrabble.

Explanation: Learning while playing helps you remember longer.

[Visual: Scrabble tiles forming WORD]

Example: Word Search puzzles.

Memory Tip: Play word games on Sunday!

❓ Check: Game where you act out a word?
✅ Dumb Charades
