⚡ Quick Revision

  • External Organs: Can be seen (Eyes, Nose, Hands).
  • Internal Organs: Inside body (Heart, Lungs, Brain).
  • Digestive System: Breaks down food for energy. Mouth -> Food Pipe -> Stomach -> Intestines.
  • Respiratory System: Helps us breathe. Nose -> Windpipe -> Lungs.
  • Circulatory System: Heart pumps blood to all parts.
  • Skeletal System: Framework of 206 bones. Gives shape.
  • Muscular System: Muscles help bones move.
  • Sense Organs: 5 organs (Eye, Ear, Nose, Tongue, Skin).
  • Good Habits: Bathe daily, brush twice, eat healthy.
  • Diseases: Stay clean to avoid cold, fever, diarrhea.
  • First Aid: Immediate help before doctor arrives.
