MY COMPUTER NOTES 📝

1. What is an Operating System (OS)?
An Operating System is the main software that makes a computer run. It manages all the parts of the computer and lets us talk to it. Without an OS, a computer is just a plastic box!
[IMAGE: A Traffic Police managing cars at a junction]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Think of the School Principal. The Principal manages teachers, students, and classes. Similarly, the OS manages the keyboard, mouse, and screen!
💡 Tip: OS = "Operating Soul" of the computer.
❓ Is Windows 10 an Operating System? (Yes/No)
2. Graphical User Interface (GUI)
GUI (pronounced "Goo-ee") stands for Graphical User Interface. It means we use Pictures (Graphics) to give commands instead of typing difficult words. We use a mouse to click on these pictures.
[IMAGE: Comparison of Black Screen (Text) vs Colorful Screen (Icons)]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Ordering food from a Menu Card with Photos at a restaurant is like GUI. You just point at the photo! (Old computers were like ordering without a menu by memorizing names).
💡 Tip: G for Graphics, G for Good-looking pictures!
❓ In GUI, do we type commands or click pictures?
3. Desktop & Its Components
The Desktop is the very first screen you see after the computer starts. It is like your study table where you keep your things.

Key Parts:
  • Icons: Small pictures representing files or programs.
  • Taskbar: The long strip at the bottom of the screen.
  • Start Button: The button usually on the bottom-left to open the menu.
[IMAGE: Diagram of Desktop showing Icons, Taskbar, and Start Button]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Your School Bag. The bag itself is the Desktop. The books inside are Icons. The zip is the Taskbar!
💡 Tip: Desk-top = Top of your Desk.
❓ Where is the Taskbar located usually?
4. Window Elements
When you open a program, it opens in a rectangular box called a Window.

Parts of a Window:
  • Title Bar: Top bar showing the name.
  • Minimize (-): Hides the window to the taskbar.
  • Maximize (□): Makes the window fill the screen.
  • Close (X): Shuts down the program.
[IMAGE: A Window labeled with Title Bar, Minimize, Maximize, Close]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Like opening a Tiffin Box. The lid is the Title Bar. Closing the lid is the Close button!
💡 Tip: Red 'X' means danger/stop/close!
❓ Which button hides the window but keeps it running?
5. How to Open & Close Programs
To Open: Move the mouse pointer to an icon and Double-Click (click left button twice fast). Or Click Start -> Select Program.
To Close: Click the Red Cross (X) button on the top right corner.
[IMAGE: Mouse showing Double Click action]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Like knocking on a door twice (Double Click) to enter a room!
💡 Tip: "Tick-Tick" (Double Click) to open.
❓ How many times do we click to open a folder on the desktop?
6. Desktop Personalization
You can change how your computer looks!
Wallpaper: The background picture on the desktop.
Themes: A set of sounds, colors, and pictures.
Steps: Right Click on Desktop -> Personalize.
[IMAGE: Screen showing Wallpaper selection menu]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Changing the Cover of your Notebook. The inside pages are the same, but the outside looks different and beautiful!
💡 Tip: Wallpaper on Wall = Wallpaper on Screen.
❓ Can you put your own photo as Wallpaper?
7. Practical Desktop Navigation
Moving Icons: Click and Hold (Drag) an icon to move it.
Sorting: Right Click -> Sort By -> Name/Size.
Refreshing: Right Click -> Refresh (Makes computer feel fresh!).
[IMAGE: Icons being dragged from left to right]
🇮🇳 India Daily Life Example: Like Arranging toys on a shelf. You drag the teddy bear from left to right to make it look tidy.
💡 Tip: Drag and Drop = Pick and Place.
❓ What helps to arrange icons automatically? (Sort/Delete)
