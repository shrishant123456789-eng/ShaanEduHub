Topic Notes
1. Types of Nouns

Nouns are naming words. Let's look at advanced types:

🏫 👨‍🏫 📦
  • Proper: Specific names (India, Rahul, Diwali). Starts with Capital letter.
  • Common: General names (country, boy, festival).
  • Material: Substances (Gold, Wood, Milk).
  • Abstract: Feelings/Ideas (Happiness, Honesty). Cannot be touched.
  • Collective: Groups (Army, Team).
Daily Life: The gold (Material) ring gave me happiness (Abstract).
💡 Proper Nouns are VIPs – they always wear a Capital cap!
2. Countable vs Uncountable

Countable: Can be counted (1 pen, 2 pens). Has plural form.

Uncountable: Cannot count (Water, Sugar, Love). No plural form.

🍎 vs 💧
Check: Can you count 'Rice'? No, it's Uncountable.
3. Collective Nouns (Groups)

Special names for groups of people, animals, or things.

A bouquet of flowers.
A troop of monkeys.
A choir of singers.
💡 Collective nouns usually take a SINGULAR verb (The team IS playing).
4. Gender
🤴 👸
  • Masculine: Male (Lion, King, Hero).
  • Feminine: Female (Lioness, Queen, Heroine).
  • Common: Either (Teacher, Doctor, Baby).
  • Neuter: Non-living (Table, Book).
5. Pronouns

Words used in place of nouns to avoid repetition.

  • Personal: I, We, You, He, She, It, They.
  • Possessive: Mine, Yours, His, Hers. (Shows ownership).
  • Reflexive: Myself, Himself, Ourselves. (Action reflects back).
  • Demonstrative: This, That, These, Those. (Pointing).
  • Interrogative: Who, What, Which? (Asking).
Wrong: Ravi likes Ravi's car.
Correct: Ravi likes his car.

Revision
  • Nouns name People, Places, Animals, Things, Feelings.
  • Abstract Nouns = Feelings (Joy, Pain). No touch!
  • Collective Nouns = Groups (Flock, Herd). Usually Singular verb.
  • Pronouns replace Nouns.
  • Reflexive ends in -self/-selves.
  • Demonstrative points out (This/That).
  • Always check Subject-Verb agreement with Collective Nouns!
