1. Mental Addition & Subtraction ➕➖

Mental Maths means solving problems in your head without paper!

  • Trick 1 (Break it): 24 + 12? Think 24 + 10 = 34, then 34 + 2 = 36.
  • Trick 2 (Make 10s): 8 + 5? Take 2 from 5 to make 8 a 10. So, 10 + 3 = 13.
Try: 50 - 2?
Think backwards: 49, 48!

2. Multiplication & Division Tricks ✖️➗

Speed up your calculations!

  • × 10 Trick: Just add a '0' at the end. (5 × 10 = 50)
  • × 2 Trick: Just double the number. (12 × 2 = 12 + 12 = 24)
  • Division: Think reverse multiplication. (20 ÷ 5? Think: 5 × ? = 20. Ans: 4)

4. Logical Thinking 🧐

Use clues to find the answer.

  • Odd One Out: Find the one that is different. (Apple, Banana, Car, Grape -> Car is odd!)
  • Riddles: "I have 4 legs but cannot walk. Who am I? -> A Table!"
