Chapter Notes

1. What is a Sentence?

A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense.

Example: "The sun rises in the east." (Correct)
"Rises sun east." (Incorrect)
💡 Rule: A sentence always starts with a Capital Letter.
[Image: Train engine starting with Capital Letter]
2. Types of Sentences

There are different types of sentences:

  • Statement: Tells us something. (I like apples.)
  • Question: Asks something. (Do you like apples?)
  • Command: Tells someone to do something. (Eat your apple.)
  • Exclamation: Shows strong feeling. (What a tasty apple!)
[Image: Four icons representing types]
3. Punctuation Marks

We use special marks at the end of a sentence.

. (Full Stop): For Statements and Commands.
? (Question Mark): For Questions.
! (Exclamation Mark): For Strong feelings.
💡 Tip: Look at the first word. Is it 'What', 'Where', 'Why'? It's a question!
4. Capital Letters

We use Capital Letters for:

  • The first word of a sentence.
  • The word 'I'.
  • Names of people (Rahul), places (Delhi), days (Monday), and months (June).
Example: Rahul and I went to Delhi on Monday.
5. Jumbled Sentences

To make a correct sentence, words must be in the right order.

Jumbled: "milk likes cat The."
Correct: "The cat likes milk."
💡 Tip: Find the Action word (Verb) and the Doer (Subject) first.

Quick Revision

  • Start every sentence with a Capital Letter.
  • End Statements with a Full Stop (.).
  • End Questions with a Question Mark (?).
  • End Exclamations with an Exclamation Mark (!).
  • Use Capital Letters for Names and 'I'.
  • Words must be in correct order to make sense.
