1. Introduction to Computational Thinking

Computational thinking is not about thinking like a robot! It is a way of solving problems so that a computer (or a human) can understand them.

IMAGE: A child planning a lego tower
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: Imagine planning a Birthday Party. You don't just "do it". You plan the guest list, the food (samosas & cake), and the games. This planning is Computational Thinking!
💡 TIP: Think -> Plan -> Solve.
❓ Quick Check: Is planning a picnic a type of problem solving? (Yes/No)
2. Understanding Patterns

A pattern is something that repeats or follows a rule. Computers love patterns because they help predict what comes next.

IMAGE: Rangoli design showing repetition
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: Look at a Rangoli design during Diwali. It has repeating shapes (flowers, dots, lines). Or the stripes on a Zebra crossing.
💡 TIP: Look for the repeat! Red, Blue, Red, Blue... what's next?
❓ Quick Check: If a sequence is 2, 4, 6, 8... what comes next?
3. Simple Algorithms

An algorithm is just a fancy word for "Step-by-Step Instructions". It must be in the correct order to work.

IMAGE: Steps to brush teeth
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: Making Maggi Noodles. 1. Boil water. 2. Add noodles. 3. Add masala. 4. Cook. If you eat the masala first, it won't be noodles! Order matters.
💡 TIP: Algorithms = Recipes for Computers.
❓ Quick Check: Can you wear shoes before socks?
4. Decomposition

Decomposition means breaking a BIG problem into SMALL, easy pieces. It makes hard work look easy.

IMAGE: Breaking a chocolate bar into pieces
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: Getting ready for school. Break it down: 1. Brush teeth. 2. Bath. 3. Wear Uniform. 4. Pack Bag. 5. Eat Breakfast.
💡 TIP: Break it to make it!
❓ Quick Check: Is packing a school bag a small part of "Going to School"?
5. Logical Reasoning

Logic is using facts to make a decision. We use "IF... THEN..." rules.

IMAGE: Rain clouds and an umbrella
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: IF it is raining outside, THEN take an umbrella. IF the traffic light is Red, THEN stop.
💡 TIP: If This happens, Then do That.
❓ Quick Check: If you are hungry, then you should...?
6. Debugging

A "Bug" is a mistake. "Debugging" is finding and fixing that mistake.

IMAGE: Finding a spelling mistake
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: You made tea but it tastes salty! The bug: You added salt instead of sugar. The fix: Make new tea with sugar.
💡 TIP: Be a Detective! Find the error.
❓ Quick Check: Is a spelling mistake a type of bug?
7. Hands-on Logic Tasks

Using our brains to solve puzzles, mazes, and sorting games helps us think like a computer programmer.

IMAGE: A maze puzzle
🇮🇳 Indian Life Example: Sorting vegetables from the market. Potatoes in one basket, onions in another. This is sorting logic!
💡 TIP: Practice makes logic perfect.
❓ Quick Check: Is arranging books by size a logic task?
