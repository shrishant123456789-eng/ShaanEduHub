📘 Study Notes

1. Keeping Own Things Safe 🔒

Responsibility means taking care of your things.
🌟 Your school bag, pencil box, and water bottle are yours.
🌟 Keep them safe. Do not leave them on the playground.
Story: Sonu lost his pencil box because he left it in the park. Now he writes with a small pencil. Be like Monu, who checks his bag before leaving!

❓ Activity: Check your bag. Is everything safe inside?

2. Completing Work on Time ⏰

Time is precious. Good children finish their work on time.
✅ Finish homework before watching TV.
✅ Wake up on time for school.
If you finish work early, you get more time to play! 🏃‍♂️

3. Taking Care of Books & Toys 📚🧸

Books give us knowledge. Toys give us fun.
🌟 Do not tear pages from books.
🌟 Do not break your toys. Put them back in the box after playing.
When you take care of things, they last longer!

4. Helping at Home 🏠❤️

You are a part of the family. You must help!
✅ Keep your shoes in the rack.
✅ Fold your small clothes.
✅ Water the plants. 🌿
Helping makes parents happy and proud.

5. School Duties 🏫

School is like our second home. We have duties there too.
✅ Keep the classroom clean.
✅ Do not scribble on desks or walls.
✅ Help the teacher carry books.
A clean school is a happy school!

6. Following Class Monitor Rules 👩‍🏫

The Class Monitor helps the teacher maintain order.
🌟 Listen to the monitor when the teacher is away.
🌟 Do not make noise or fight.
Be a good student and cooperate with the monitor.

7. Saving Electricity & Water 💡💧

Resources like water and electricity are precious.
🚫 Do not leave the tap open while brushing.
🚫 Switch off lights and fans when you leave the room.
Saving today means having enough for tomorrow!

8. Being a Responsible Citizen 🌍

A Citizen is a member of the country.
🌟 Throw garbage in dustbins, not on the road.
🌟 Plant trees to help nature.
🌟 Follow traffic rules.
Small good actions make a great country!
