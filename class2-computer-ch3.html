🖥️ Types of Computers

Class 2 • Chapter 3 • Learn & Play
🖥️ Desktop Computer

Meet Mr. Desktop! He is big and heavy.
He sits on a desk or table. He needs electricity to work.
You can find him in schools, offices, and homes. He cannot walk with you!

💻 Laptop Computer

This is Ms. Laptop. She is smaller than a desktop.
You can keep her on your lap. She has a battery inside.
You can carry her in your bag anywhere! She folds like a book.

📱 Tablet

A Tablet looks like a big mobile phone.
It has a Touch Screen. You don't need a mouse or keyboard!
Kids use it to watch cartoons, play games, and study.

🤳 Smartphone

This is the smallest computer! It fits in your pocket.
We use it to make calls, take photos, and use the internet.
Your mom and dad have one!

📺 Smart TV

A Smart TV is also a type of computer.
You can watch YouTube and play games on it.
It connects to the internet to show you movies.
