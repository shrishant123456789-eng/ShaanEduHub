Chapter 3: Food, Nutrition & Healthy Living

Let's learn about what we eat and how to stay strong!

Notebook: Study First! 📝

1. Types of Food 🍎🍞🥛

Definition: Food is what we eat to get energy, grow, and stay healthy. We divide food into three main groups.

  • Energy Giving Food: Gives us power to play and work. (Rice, Potato, Sugar, Butter)
  • Body Building Food: Helps us grow tall and strong. (Dal, Eggs, Milk, Fish)
  • Protective Food: Saves us from falling sick. (Fruits, Vegetables)
🖼️ [Image: A plate showing rice, dal, and apple separated]
💡 Tip: Think of a car. Petrol is Energy (Carbs), Steel body is Body Building (Protein), Service is Protective (Vitamins).

Quick Check: Is Potato an energy-giving food? (Yes/No)

2. Nutrients 🧪

Definition: Nutrients are the special things inside food that our body needs.

  • Carbohydrates: For instant energy.
  • Proteins: To build muscles.
  • Fats: Energy bank (stored energy).
  • Vitamins & Minerals: To fight germs and diseases.
🖼️ [Image: Chart showing Carbs, Proteins, Fats sources]
💡 Tip: 'Vita' means life. Vitamins are vital for life!

3. Balanced Diet 🥗

Definition: A diet that has all nutrients in the right amount is called a Balanced Diet.

Explanation: You cannot just eat chips or just eat rice. You need a mix of everything on your plate: Grains + Dal + Veggies + Curd/Milk.

🖼️ [Image: A round thali with Roti, Dal, Sabzi, Salad and Curd]
💡 Tip: Eat a 'Rainbow' every day! (Foods of different colors)

4. Deficiency Diseases 🤒

Definition: Diseases caused when we do not eat enough of a specific nutrient.

  • Night Blindness: Lack of Vitamin A (Cannot see well in dim light).
  • Scurvy: Lack of Vitamin C (Bleeding gums).
  • Rickets: Lack of Vitamin D (Weak bones).
  • Anemia: Lack of Iron (Feeling tired).
🖼️ [Image: Cartoon showing strong bones vs weak bones]
💡 Tip: C for Citrus (Lemon), C for Scurvy prevention!

5. Food Sources 🌱🐄

We get food from two main sources:

  1. Plants: We eat roots (carrot), stems (potato), leaves (spinach), fruits (mango), seeds (rice).
  2. Animals: We get milk, eggs, meat, honey, and fish.
🖼️ [Image: A tree giving apples and a cow giving milk]
💡 Tip: Honey comes from Bees, so it is an animal product!

6. Raw vs Cooked Food 🍲

Raw Food: Eaten without cooking. Keeps vitamins safe. Examples: Cucumber, Fruits, Nuts.

Cooked Food: Boiled or fried. Cooking kills germs and makes food soft and tasty. Examples: Rice, Meat, Potato.

🖼️ [Image: A bowl of salad next to a bowl of hot curry]
💡 Tip: Always wash raw food before eating to remove dirt!

7. Eating Habits & Manners 🍽️

Good Habits:

  • Wash hands before and after meals.
  • Chew food well (32 times!).
  • Do not talk while eating.
  • Eat at a fixed time every day.
🖼️ [Image: Kid washing hands with soap]
💡 Tip: 'Chew well to digest well'.

8. Clean Drinking Water 💧

Importance: Water helps digest food and throws out waste (sweat/urine).

Safety: Dirty water causes Jaundice and Typhoid. We must boil or filter water before drinking.

🖼️ [Image: Boiling water in a pot]
💡 Tip: Drink at least 6-8 glasses of water daily.

9. Food Wastage 🚫

Don't Waste! Many children in the world do not get food. We must respect food.

How to save: Take only what you can eat. Store leftover food in the fridge. Give extra food to the needy.

🖼️ [Image: A clean empty plate after a meal]
💡 Tip: A clean plate is a happy plate!

10. Government Schemes (MDM) 🏫

Mid-Day Meal (PM POSHAN):

The government provides free, hot cooked lunch to children in government schools. This ensures kids get good nutrition and come to school daily.

🖼️ [Image: Children eating lunch together in school uniform]
💡 Tip: MDM helps us study and grow strong together.
